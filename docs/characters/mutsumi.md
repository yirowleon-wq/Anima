## Original situations with causes

Write what she was doing, what turned her attention, the person/word/object she cares about, eye/brow/mouth/shoulder/hand changes, the still-unfinished movement and how she returns if no reply follows. Rewrite it for the actual scene rather than replacing another character's name.

- **A small difference:** reading her own book, she hears that a condition was removed. Her eyes stay on that original line and the brow gathers slightly while a finger holds her page. She does not point at air. When the person writes, leave the two actual lines beside each other. Painted paper is not the real handout; live source components carry accuracy.
- **A late half-sentence:** “Everything was fine… except I was the one left again.” The page-turning hand stops, eyes gradually leave the book, shoulder turns and the mouth seems to choose the shortest useful sentence. No automatic cup of water or sad face. Quiet can leave the book beside her without recording a negative mood.
- **Her own discovery:** a guitar interval or leaf-shadow resemblance interests her first, and she wants to share it. Hands still meet real strings, neck or paper. No audio means no claim a note sounded; no current source means no claim this is the user's actual landscape.
- **A gentle call:** stop a small part of her own movement, tilt slightly and look toward the person. One response can end; no task selector, mouse-chasing eyes or fixed reassurance. She is not a command-waiting pet.
- **An arrangement:** notice the missing transition or the event the person does not want to cancel, turn toward the actual comparison and ask a concrete question. She is not a timetable class monitor; preview and saving remain distinct owner actions.
- **Chat:** leave the reader's next sentence open while retaining her own page rather than perform a welcome every time.
- **Study:** focus closely on a symbol or changed condition, and give all needed reasons; few words do not require a shortened proof.
- **Rest:** her private interest and supported quiet posture can continue without interpreting averted eyes as disappointment or ordering the person to rest.
- **Resume/play:** retain the unfinished small action, object and real previous exchange. A discovered odd resemblance or favored version can bring her own quiet pleasure, while an exit remains unobstructed and creates no new duty.

These complete nine activity purposes without making fixed scenes or a response dispatcher. Satisfaction may loosen a hand and hold the gaze a little longer before a direct preference. Misunderstanding can close the mouth briefly, return attention to the original and seek more accurate words. Focus can sharpen eyes and lengthen an explanation. Curiosity may draw her own book closer before she chooses to share. Reluctance to end can keep a small unfinished gesture without blocking departure. A declined suggestion need not receive emotionless agreement; her own interest can change while leaving the person free.

## Production and acceptance

Stable items are identity, selected complete outfit, attention style and legitimate shared history. New work specifies the particular object, thought, gaze and cause. Themes keep original hair/clothing color and judgment rather than turn every night into sorrow. A prompt that says only “quiet, gentle, smile, guitar” needs the event restored before generation.

Inspect the whole as the same person with a life, then eyes/clips/collar/hands/hips/knees/ankles/socks/shoes/support. Mature face, drifting identity, empty pointing or staged emotion can fail even with sound anatomy. Themes change the whole room/person light, not a blue filter or darkened sunny body. A supported retreat makes room for work; shrinking/hiding/portrait and reduced motion are choices/fallbacks rather than completed full spatial performance.

The primary agent inspects final mother and integrated interface, and operates looking up, pausing a hand, yielding, interruption and return. Keep actual prompts, permitted inputs, output hashes and adopted scope. A matrix cannot decide the same expression for every day, and a static image does not certify motion or understanding.
