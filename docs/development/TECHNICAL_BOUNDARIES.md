## Original evidence, memory and changes

Original content, conditional/bitemporal assertions, decisions/blockers, shared works, domain observations and derived indexes have separate roles. A title, preview, summary or retrieval hit is navigation, not original content read. Read neighboring text and pagination within permission. Indexes locate originals and can be rebuilt; they do not establish facts.

Do not send restricted content out first to classify it.

Correction, reality changing, an exception, dispute, disabling a method, withdrawing a proposal, revoking a source and permanently forgetting have different effects. Independent lawful fragments and the user's separate writing survive; withdrawn content must not revive from history/cache. A processed watermark is contiguous, not the highest seen sequence number.

Generated, rendered, seen, understood, accepted and executed are different evidence. A displayed answer or maximum cursor does not establish full reading, acceptance, learning mastery or completed work.
