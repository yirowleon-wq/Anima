# Dated mobile research references

This working register retains **39 public primary-source entries and eight explicit design briefs** from research recorded on September 12, 2026. The reading scopes describe that earlier research record, not a new full-document audit. Design briefs preserve transferable questions without presenting them as a named product's verified behavior. Neither category establishes software execution, installed capability, account eligibility or product reliability. Software and paper authorship does not itself determine suitability; licenses, actual scope and current platform conditions do.

On November 1, 2026, the Apple announcement and WWDC session, Android AppFunctions overview, Google Play policy, and four named paper entries were opened directly to check their identity and the portions returned. The Manifest permission page timed out. The remaining entries retain the dated ledger's earlier reading scope. No product was installed and no paper experiment or device workflow was reproduced. Check the current official API, policy, SDK and actual device before relying on an earlier limitation or version number.

The current plan remains independent iOS/Swift and iPad first, then Android. The ledger's Android and vendor examples supply methods and conditional alternatives; they neither change that sequence nor promise universal system privileges. Exposing Anima's semantic actions differs from obtaining permission to call another app. The [72 requirements](MOBILE_REQUIREMENTS.md) separate ordinary distribution, a personal enhanced experiment and vendor-qualified integration.

## Earlier retrievals that did not establish technical evidence

These attempts do not add successful primary-source entries to the register:

- A JavaScript shell or search index is not a readable whitepaper or SDK contract. Until an actual body is read, do not infer a permission architecture or invent a web/native bridge method.
- [A Samsung ecosystem announcement address](https://news.samsung.com/global/galaxy-ai-expands-multi-agent-ecosystem-to-give-users-more-choice-and-flexibility) did not yield its exact announcement body. A search home page or secondary report is not equivalent evidence of integration permission.

Use references to answer a concrete implementation question, not to install everything listed. They do not grant access to a private VM, browser credentials, product memory or account integration, or establish platform qualification.
