## Progressive acceptance
Stages 01-03 establish current object/subject, correction, original reading and withdrawal. Stages 04-05 establish real version/adoption/attempt and host responsibilities. Stage 06 verifies those meanings actually control the cast, layouts and cues. Stages 07-10 deepen spatial, shared-paper, creation, sound and ordinary-life use. Stage 11 adds actual independent hosts, modality and causal reconnection; Stage 12 follows continuous and playful life and reconciles actual evidence. A later device or service requirement stays open until its condition is actually selected and exercised.
The main Stage 02 path is 02-A/B -> 03-A; 02-C is optional. Its four listed gates refine formal external result claims only if that extension is chosen. Keep local/manual inspection useful in the meantime. All eight stage workers keep their substantive ownership; these gates do not create eight new sessions per block or another scheduler.

## Stop and continue
Before stopping, preserve current object/revision/draft, the exact gate and observed scope, lawful source/coverage, last owner receipt or unknown original attempt, unresolved condition and one next action. Restore the same work only after current permissions/barriers/revisions are checked. Keep a current-turn correction even if durable persistence fails. Resume an unknown action by inspecting its original attempt rather than resubmitting. No paused visit, saved plan or completed document executes itself.
