# Situations worth preserving

Anima should remain useful across work, relationships, interests and ordinary time. These materials explore that breadth through hypothetical student situations while the runtime and interaction system develop. Every person, course, receipt and circumstance here is synthetic. They are not the owner's biography, schedule or grades, and they do not establish university policy.

| Collection | What it contains | How to read it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [128 situations](scenarios.json) | Sixteen domains with eight situations each. Every item retains situation, competing goal, trigger, evidence, action mode, example response, counterfactual and observable outcomes. | Follow the actual relationships and conditions. The six modes are possible judgments, not keyword routes or notification quotas. |
| [Continuous timelines](timelines.json) | Twelve timelines, 59 steps and 91 available events, with source/object revisions, actual arrival times, late results, changed permissions and recovery. | `available_input` describes what is available at that step. `review_criteria` and future steps are separate design material. |
| [Forty design directions](DESIGN_DIRECTIONS.md) | A1–E8: immediate life, possible futures, other people, inner experience and the relationship with the system. | Use these lenses to notice a missing behavior or tradeoff. They are not forty autonomous services. |
| [Two hundred questions](QUESTIONS.md) | Five hypothetical inputs for each A1–E8 direction, preserving different language and combinations of needs. | Questions invite reasoning; they are not two hundred functions or a production lookup table. |
| [Two hundred memory/context gates](MEMORY_GATES.md) | Every full question has an item-specific required context, first responsible block and observable acceptance distinction. | Design/acceptance requirements, not recorded passes, personal biography or executable tests. Keep expected answers out of the acting model context. |
| [Memory gates across 38 blocks](MEMORY_GATE_SEQUENCE.md) | Introduced, extended, native and integrated acceptance scope for every substantial work block. | The corresponding block text includes the actual relevant gate wording. Later platform/provider evidence cannot be borrowed from an earlier local observation. |
| [Experience stories](EXPERIENCE_STORIES.md) | V01–V36: continuous character, tool, source, creation, action, map and recovery experiences. | Follow a meaningful current risk through ordinary product use. A story is not a recorded pass. |

The collections overlap and are not additive evidence counts. Their presence does not mean they were exercised, that all related features exist, or that the user approved each historical proposal as a feature. The [current capability baseline](../CURRENT_STATUS.md) records actual implementation and observation boundaries; [project navigation](../../PROJECT_NAVIGATION.md) identifies the owners and entry points.

Example dialogue conveys purpose and voice. The companion should respond to the actual person and current question, without reciting these sentences or being routed by a scenario ID. A quiet response, a complete proof, playful creation and a useful action are all possible good outcomes. Silence and waiting preserve real agreements rather than cancelling fixed reminders.

An actual source, action and platform must support the corresponding claim. Imported records are not live institutional confirmation; local activity is not official recognition; displayed content is not understanding or acceptance; a saved schedule is not completed work. A provider, device or independent mobile host described in a story may still be a future condition.

These are static design documents. They contain no executable cases, assertions or runner. Do not feed the whole file, future changes or review criteria to the product model. Any later enactment needs its own authorized environment and actual sources. Keep OS location services and permissions off on every platform, including emulators and guests. Use manual origins, explicit imports or app-internal virtual positions and trajectories. Preserve simulated provenance, quality, expiry, cancellation and recovery; simulated movement establishes neither real arrival nor official activity credit. Test development, external messages, purchasing, enrollment and account access require their own applicable authorization.

Time-zone examples use a declared IANA zone, with America/Los_Angeles as the configurable default. Neither a calendar gap nor a date implies the student's availability. Rules and requirements stay tied to their actual institution, program, year and sources. An explicitly fictional activity challenge is not an American campus requirement.
