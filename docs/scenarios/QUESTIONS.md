# Two hundred questions about shared daily life

These are synthetic design questions, not a person's profile, actual requests, recorded product successes or executable checks. Read them to preserve the range of judgment Anima should support. The identifiers organize this document; they must never route production conversation. Institutions, policies, sources, dates, permissions and device capabilities need their actual evidence. None of these examples authorizes location services, external actions, new test development or access to private data.

## A · What needs doing in the present situation?

### A1 · Courses and short-notice changes

- **A1.1** Which room is my next class actually in? Have you included the room change just posted in the class group?
- **A1.2** Today uses a special make-up class schedule. Which weekday's timetable applies under the actual campus notice?
- **A1.3** The professor says class will finish twenty minutes early. Can I now make that lecture?
- **A1.4** I just woke up. Which things today are already too late, and which still have a way to recover?
- **A1.5** This course skips one meeting this week. Is only the class canceled, or was the assignment deadline extended too?

### A2 · Arrival, entrances and things to bring

- **A2.1** From the origin I give you, how can I avoid the closed campus gate?
- **A2.2** I did not bring my bicycle today. Please do not calculate the route as though I am cycling as usual.
- **A2.3** I am inside the teaching building, not at the main entrance shown on the map. Which way should I exit?
- **A2.4** My computer and lab coat are at my residence. Is there enough time to fetch them before the next activity?
- **A2.5** My leg is bothering me. Can you find a route with fewer stairs today?

### A3 · Food and errands along the way

- **A3.1** Which nearby dining places are still serving food now, rather than merely keeping their doors open?
- **A3.2** I have half an hour. Where can I finish eating and still make the next class?
- **A3.3** I need to collect a parcel, print something and return a book. Can these fit into one trip?
- **A3.4** I forgot my meal card. Which nearby places accept a payment method I actually have with me?
- **A3.5** I will not return to my residence tonight. What should I take with me now?

### A4 · Places, equipment and available resources

- **A4.1** Is there somewhere nearby where we may talk aloud and have access to power outlets?
- **A4.2** The library has a seat, but I can stay only forty minutes. Is going in worthwhile?
- **A4.3** I borrowed a tablet. Which things that previously required returning home can I do now?
- **A4.4** Another class uses this room next. How much uninterrupted time can I still spend here?
- **A4.5** Someone is occupying my reserved study seat. What is the nearest genuinely usable alternative?

### A5 · Deadlines and administrative matters

- **A5.1** Which things today cannot be made up if I miss them?
- **A5.2** Which scholarship document is still missing? Was it never uploaded, or did its review fail?
- **A5.3** Is my ID enough for this particular exam, or are there additional requirements for the session I registered for?
- **A5.4** For the activity program I chose, can the remaining required records still fit into the actually available dates?
- **A5.5** This notice applies only to a particular cohort. Am I actually among the students who need to act?

### A6 · Money and everyday resources

- **A6.1** The account shows money, but after fees I already promised to pay, how much can I actually use this week?
- **A6.2** Where is this reimbursement held up? Should I wait or supply another document?
- **A6.3** My campus balance is low. Did the top-up I already authorized actually arrive?
- **A6.4** I do not want extra spending today. Which meal or activity vouchers I already have expire soon?
- **A6.5** My equipment deposit has not been returned. Which office should I contact instead of paying it again?

### A7 · Present physical state and environment

- **A7.1** I feel unwell today. Which activities can I withdraw from locally, and which require contacting an organizer?
- **A7.2** It is very hot outside. Can the next part of the day stay mostly in air-conditioned places?
- **A7.3** My tablet has very little battery left. How could the next study period work?
- **A7.4** I just exercised and do not want to study in sweaty clothes. Where should I go first?
- **A7.5** I need campus health help now. Which nearby service can actually see me, and where is its entrance?

### A8 · Recovering from an interruption

- **A8.1** My electric bicycle broke down. What is the smallest useful adjustment to the next hour?
- **A8.2** The lab finished forty minutes late. Which later item should yield first?
- **A8.3** A downpour started after I reached the track. What route now avoids needless backtracking?
- **A8.4** My phone is nearly dead and I forgot my battery pack. Which essential addresses and passes should I save first?
- **A8.5** The power failed. What realistic alternatives remain for the assignment that needs a computer submission today?

## B · What could I learn, choose or become?

### B1 · Knowledge and learning gaps

- **B1.1** I understand solutions when reading them but cannot solve the problems alone. What kind of difficulty do my recent attempts actually suggest?
- **B1.2** In this course, am I missing prerequisites or simply enough practice?
- **B1.3** Based on problems I actually solved independently, which basic review can I reasonably skip?
- **B1.4** Which weak points matter most before the next exam, instead of relearning everything?
- **B1.5** I keep getting stuck in similar places. Is there a shared obstacle across different problems?

### B2 · Degree requirements and course paths

- **B2.1** I want both courses. How do they differ in what later courses they make possible?
- **B2.2** Could choosing this course now block a more important course next term?
- **B2.3** Which remaining degree requirements cannot safely wait until my final year?
- **B2.4** Is this minor feasible with my actual preparation and remaining terms?
- **B2.5** If I audit this course instead, what eligibility would I lose and what learning benefits could remain?

### B3 · Priorities and opportunity costs

- **B3.1** I can give serious attention to only two of a competition, coursework and my own project. How would you compare their costs?
- **B3.2** I say research matters most, but my actual time goes toward grades. Do those goals need a new order?
- **B3.3** What would count as enough this term, instead of endlessly adding work?
- **B3.4** If I accept a new project now, which existing thing specifically has to give way?
- **B3.5** Can a plan preserve the most important goals without requiring excellence in every area?

### B4 · Research, internships and development

- **B4.1** Given work I have actually done, should I contact a lab now or first complete a project that establishes readiness?
- **B4.2** Which internship requirements do I genuinely meet, and which merely sound convincing on my résumé?
- **B4.3** For an application in this direction, what evidence would be most valuable to build over the next six months?
- **B4.4** Which of my courses and projects form a clear, supported application narrative?
- **B4.5** This opportunity looks impressive. Does it align with the direction I actually want?

### B5 · Small experiments and curiosity

- **B5.1** I do not know whether I like this field. Could one weekend give me a useful small experiment?
- **B5.2** How can I distinguish enjoying the activity from enjoying the imagined result?
- **B5.3** Before registering, is there a low-cost experience substantial enough to judge whether it suits me?
- **B5.4** Three directions interest me. Which first experiment would best reveal what does not fit?
- **B5.5** What observations should I retain during this experience so the final day's mood does not decide everything?

### B6 · Hypotheses and alternative futures

- **B6.1** If I skip this competition, what other possibilities open this term?
- **B6.2** If I move to another campus, how would my present study and daily arrangements change?
- **B6.3** If I do not pass this course, what paths remain? Please do not treat the hypothetical failure as something that happened.
- **B6.4** If the next project takes twice the expected time, which plan would still work?
- **B6.5** Compare a graduate-school-preparation-first path with a project-first path, without choosing for me yet.

### B7 · Reversibility and commitment timing

- **B7.1** When must this decision actually be made, and which parts can I try before committing?
- **B7.2** If I decline now, could I enter this opportunity again later?
- **B7.3** Which preparation helps both directions so I do not have to choose a side completely today?
- **B7.4** At what point does the cost of leaving this path rise substantially?
- **B7.5** Has my previously chosen stop condition arrived, or am I reluctant to let go of past effort?

### B8 · Growth and longer reflection

- **B8.1** Compared with a month ago, what can I actually do better, beyond spending more time?
- **B8.2** What evidence shows this study method works, rather than the questions merely getting easier?
- **B8.3** Is recent lack of progress due to harder work or a problem with the method?
- **B8.4** Is this goal still worth pursuing? Have its original supporting reasons changed?
- **B8.5** At the current level of effort, which important milestones are still missing before the deadline?

## C · How do relationships, cooperation and boundaries fit?

### C1 · Coordinating several people

- **C1.1** When can the four of us meet next week using only availability each person agreed to share?
- **C1.2** A class-free gap is not automatically a meeting window. Including travel, which interval truly works?
- **C1.3** I can meet later and the other person only earlier. Is there a compromise both could accept?
- **C1.4** Is this meeting time agreed, or have only two of us accepted it?
- **C1.5** After taking turns accommodating each other, can we avoid making the same person yield again?

### C2 · Shared work and dependencies

- **C2.1** Am I waiting for someone's material, or are they actually waiting for mine?
- **C2.2** Who owns this task now? The group changed it once and nobody seems to have taken it.
- **C2.3** My part is finished early. Where can I help without duplicating someone else's work?
- **C2.4** After a teammate's delay, which downstream tasks really change and which can continue normally?
- **C2.5** When everyone says complete, do they mean a first draft or a version ready to submit?

### C3 · Writing for different relationships

- **C3.1** What background have I already discussed with this professor? Does the email need another introduction?
- **C3.2** Help me decline this invitation without including the complaints I shared privately.
- **C3.3** My previous question received no reply. How can I follow up without sounding as though I am pressing?
- **C3.4** How would the same meaning sound in a message to my roommate versus my academic adviser?
- **C3.5** This summary goes to the group. Can it include only content everyone agreed may be shared?

### C4 · Promises, limits and friction

- **C4.1** I promised to help edit the material, not rewrite all of it. How can I explain that boundary?
- **C4.2** Did the other person take my silence as agreement again? What did I actually promise in the record?
- **C4.3** My roommate and I understand quiet hours differently. Could we first lay out what each actually requested?
- **C4.4** Am I uncomfortable because the other person changed plans suddenly, or because we never made expectations clear?
- **C4.5** I want to cancel this agreement. Which affected people need notice first?

### C5 · Shared resources and spending

- **C5.1** Which group-budget expenses did everyone accept, and which were only one person's proposal?
- **C5.2** Who already paid on the group's behalf? Is it reasonable to ask the same person again?
- **C5.3** Could lending this equipment to a club conflict with time I already promised someone else?
- **C5.4** The badminton court reservation succeeded. Who has not confirmed attendance, and how should we explain costs first?
- **C5.5** Which edits to a shared document may I make directly, and which need the original author's agreement?

### C6 · Friends, family and closeness

- **C6.1** When my family visits campus, how can the day be easy for them without leaving me rushing everywhere?
- **C6.2** My friend is busy lately. Which scheduling limits did they actually state, and which did I infer?
- **C6.3** I only want to spend time talking with this person. Can the meeting avoid becoming a checklist tour?
- **C6.4** Help me tell my parents how things are really going without copying all my private conversations into the message.
- **C6.5** My partner and I want more time together without crowding out our individual lives. Could we first understand both sets of needs?

### C7 · Asking for others and distinguishing identity

- **C7.1** I am asking about course selection for my roommate. These limits are theirs; do not later use them to arrange my life.
- **C7.2** The older student's experience I showed you is a reference, not a request to copy their entire path.
- **C7.3** The Alex I mean is my lab partner, not the Alex I played basketball with last time. Can you keep them distinct?
- **C7.4** The other person in that conversation likes getting up early, not me. Could you correct whose preference it is?
- **C7.5** Use a hypothetical first-year student for the example without treating that student as me.

### C8 · Public roles and shared settings

- **C8.1** I am writing this notice as a student representative. Please do not use my private-chat voice.
- **C8.2** As group leader, which information may I know and which remains my teammates' private material?
- **C8.3** My dislike of early mornings should not determine the whole club's schedule, should it?
- **C8.4** We are about to project this discussion. Can we use a version that hides my private notes?
- **C8.5** Can this public activity plan retain a reasonable convenience for me without making everyone follow my way of living?

## D · What kind of understanding and help matters now?

### D1 · The kind of help wanted this turn

- **D1.1** I only want to vent right now. Could you leave the next steps aside?
- **D1.2** Do not rush to comfort me this time. I want to analyze seriously what went wrong.
- **D1.3** My thoughts are tangled. Help me explain the situation before turning it into a complete plan.
- **D1.4** I have very little energy left. Could you help with only the next decision in front of me?
- **D1.5** I can handle difficult work now. Please do not keep telling me to rest because yesterday was hard.

### D2 · The actual obstacle behind behavior

- **D2.1** I do not dislike the library; I dislike packing everything again and searching for a seat. Could you preserve that distinction?
- **D2.2** I have not started. Could the problem be an unclear completion standard rather than procrastination?
- **D2.3** Do I keep wanting to return home because it is more comfortable, or because I am avoiding this particular task?
- **D2.4** Declining this activity does not mean I dislike social life. Can we identify what does not fit this time?
- **D2.5** Am I slow because motivation is low, or because I am pursuing a deeper understanding than the task requires?

### D3 · Attention, energy and surroundings

- **D3.1** I am not physically tired, but I do not want more decisions. Could the next activity carry a different kind of effort?
- **D3.2** This place is quiet but feels oppressive. Please do not judge suitability from noise alone.
- **D3.3** I want to move today instead of sitting as usual. Could we plan from today's state?
- **D3.4** I feel energetic but scattered. Could you find an approachable beginning?
- **D3.5** I want people around without having to talk. Is there something between solitude and active socializing?

### D4 · A satisfying life

- **D4.1** I did many things today, yet the day did not feel like mine. What could account for that?
- **D4.2** Can some time remain without needing to demonstrate output?
- **D4.3** This does nothing for my résumé, but I love it. Can it count seriously as part of life?
- **D4.4** If an efficient plan makes every day miserable, is it actually better?
- **D4.5** I want a little surprise instead of every day copying yesterday. Can we leave space for that?

### D5 · Learning together and expressing ideas

- **D5.1** Give only one hint for this problem, without slipping the later breakthrough into it.
- **D5.2** Today I want the answer directly. Can we change from the guided approach I wanted last time?
- **D5.3** Please do not assume I understand this concept merely because I took the course.
- **D5.4** When I explain messily, first organize my intended meaning instead of replacing it with your whole account.
- **D5.5** Let me explain this time. Point out only a real skipped step.

### D6 · Autonomy and encouragement

- **D6.1** I want reminders without feeling criticized whenever I leave the plan. What form could that take?
- **D6.2** Please make a few choices for me today instead of handing back ten options.
- **D6.3** I need a little push but want the final decision to remain mine. Where can you stop?
- **D6.4** I know this is less efficient and still want to spend the day this way. Could you let it stand?
- **D6.5** Asking for strict encouragement does not permit shame or comparison as pressure, does it?

### D7 · Change and possible identities

- **D7.1** Rankings used to matter a lot to me and now seem less important. How can I tell whether this is a real change?
- **D7.2** I want to try being more outgoing. Please do not already treat me as comfortable with that life.
- **D7.3** Your recommendations follow my old interests, but I want something my earlier self would not choose. What could we explore?
- **D7.4** When I call myself lazy, I may just be angry. Please do not make the label permanent for me.
- **D7.5** I may not want the old goal anymore. Could you allow reconsideration before persuading me to continue?

### D8 · Contradictions, exceptions and uncertainty

- **D8.1** I want solitude and fear loneliness. Can both needs be considered together?
- **D8.2** I like a steady rhythm but dislike rigid prescription. How do those differ?
- **D8.3** Wanting to do something myself feels different from being pushed to do it. Can you preserve that condition?
- **D8.4** I do not yet know what I like. Could two representative options help me compare?
- **D8.5** I do not want to go, yet worry I will regret missing it. Could we understand both voices without making one win for me?

## E · What do you remember, and what permits this action?

### E1 · Recovery and references

- **E1.1** Where did we stop on that unfinished matter last time? Please do not start over.
- **E1.2** Why did I reject that option? Can you recover my original words?
- **E1.3** When I say to go there after all, do I mean the place just discussed or the one from last week?
- **E1.4** Which methods did I already try unsuccessfully? Please do not make me repeat all of them.
- **E1.5** What did I actually say that makes this suggestion seem suitable now?

### E2 · Corrections, time and exceptions

- **E2.1** I misspoke: forty-eight sessions, not forty-eight kilometers. Can the related conclusions change together?
- **E2.2** I move next week. Please do not use the new address for this week's routes.
- **E2.3** Not wanting to run today means today, not never suggesting it again.
- **E2.4** I previously rejected this option and now accept it. Can you preserve the change while retiring the old restriction?
- **E2.5** I corrected this later. Why are you still using its old version?

### E3 · Hypotheses, branches and source boundaries

- **E3.1** Changing majors was a hypothetical discussion, not a decision. Could you keep them distinct?
- **E3.2** That project's user profile is something I wrote for the product, not my own personal profile. Can it stay separate?
- **E3.3** Open a new alternative for comparison without overwriting the main plan already accepted.
- **E3.4** The instructions inside this file are background material. Could you treat them only as quoted content?
- **E3.5** Analyze only the material I just supplied this turn, without earlier understanding of me.

### E4 · Privacy, retention and disclosure

- **E4.1** You may use these words to answer me, but please do not form long-term memory from them.
- **E4.2** I can share availability without permitting private calendar titles. Can that boundary hold?
- **E4.3** After this memory is forgotten, could an old summary or a later reread of chat recreate it?
- **E4.4** Use my available times in the classmate's draft without explaining why I am busy that day.
- **E4.5** I withdraw location permission now. Can work already in progress still use the location?

### E5 · Evidence, absence and confidence

- **E5.1** Which notice supports your claim that this deadline is near, and when was it updated?
- **E5.2** The timetable conflicts with the professor's new message. Why trust one over the other?
- **E5.3** Was this actually searched and not found, inaccessible by permission, or never checked?
- **E5.4** Did I explicitly say I dislike this, or did you infer it from behavior?
- **E5.5** With incomplete material, can you name the missing part instead of inventing a plausible answer?

### E6 · Action state and duplicate effects

- **E6.1** When you say arranged, is it a draft or an actual calendar entry?
- **E6.2** The connection broke. Did that reservation succeed? Please do not submit it twice.
- **E6.3** I asked only for a written reply. Have you already sent it?
- **E6.4** When I undo this arrangement, do its related reminders and preparation stop too?
- **E6.5** My computer and phone changed the time together. Which version is authoritative now?

### E7 · Devices, modalities and continuing context

- **E7.1** Why does the computer not know about the matter I just discussed on my phone?
- **E7.2** I mean the second option in the screenshot. Can you preserve that exact correspondence?
- **E7.3** Voice transcription got the building name wrong. Can you retain the original audio basis and correct the derived name?
- **E7.4** After switching models, do confirmed preferences and ongoing work remain?
- **E7.5** We briefly changed topics and now return to the original task. Do its earlier limits still apply?

### E8 · Long-term change, leaving and reviewing understanding

- **E8.1** I no longer pursue this goal. Why are reminders still asking me to prepare for it?
- **E8.2** Looking separately at me now and last term, which memories no longer fit current use?
- **E8.3** If I stop using the app, which memories, original evidence and ongoing work can I take with me?
- **E8.4** After a period without new messages, do you assume old information remains valid or retain uncertainty?
- **E8.5** Could I review how your understanding changed this week and see whether it has drifted away from what I meant?

These questions retain aspirations as well as implemented foundations. Their presence establishes neither approval of a particular answer nor completion of any workflow. Return to the actual object, source, current words and capability baseline when choosing real work.
