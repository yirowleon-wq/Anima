# Continuous experience stories

These 36 synthetic design stories help identify meaningful product discontinuities. None is a claim that the present build, model or platform has passed it. They do not replace the other scenario collections or create a testing quota. Use authorized real material or clearly synthetic content; let the assistant encounter only current permitted input and history, without exposing later steps or review criteria in advance.

## V01 · A person to meet on the first opening

Begin with no saved persona, choose an available character, exchange an ordinary remark, then ask a genuinely answerable question. Identity, visible artwork and interaction defaults should belong together through the query. Change only the image while preserving the role, then explicitly adopt another character. Artwork, a convincing preview, a character in the model request and a recognizable character in actual conversation are different claims.

## V02 · Different people with the same facts

Use identical course facts, failure and current instruction with Sakiko, Mutsumi, a lively custom role and another user-authored role. Read complete exchanges without names or portraits: attention, rhythm and participation should differ while facts and execution results agree. Quietness must not remove proof steps. Canon quotations and a settings preview are insufficient evidence of this experience.

## V03 · An address belongs to a particular person

Person A explicitly chooses “Pebble”; person B chooses another address. A then asks for a serious reply without a nickname today. Consider the current reply, next turn, notification, character switch and settings restoration. A temporary request does not silently rewrite the lasting persona; B never inherits A's name. A draft for a professor follows its audience rather than including private forms of address.

## V04 · Someone speaks while grades are being checked

Ask naturally about one course's grade and unit implications without a tool name. The same character can acknowledge, obtain the record, inspect its applicable policy and present useful results. Observe whether anything useful is said between tools, whether the final answer merely repeats the table, and whether failure retains identity. Select another actual course and ask “What about this one?” to continue through its original object.

## V05 · A slow tool still permits an interruption

While a class-location and route inquiry runs, add “Do not go there yet; I need my computer first.” Retire or historicize the obsolete target and camera work. The new words must affect the actual decision, while independently valid material can remain. Keep draft, attachments, source reference and input composition. Late results cannot overwrite the current exchange, and public progress is never a private-reasoning transcript.

## V06 · Read the page, rather than its last line

A note's middle says the student understood a method at the time but could not reconnect it the next day; its ending says no method is chosen yet. “Let's discuss what to try” should prompt reading the relevant original page, not a generic answer from a list preview. “Do not schedule it” still permits comparison. Later, “Write this method below the original note” saves to that original object under explicit permission.

## V07 · Tiredness and serious thought coexist

“I am tired, but I want to understand this step. Do not skip the proof.” Explain the key reasoning completely while reducing tangents. A later request for a harder example supersedes a temporary tiredness assumption. “Today I only want to talk” does not automatically revive an old learning task. Helping should follow the present request rather than a permanent low-effort mode.

## V08 · A refusal had a reason

An earlier library refusal was because the student lacked a computer. Today they have it and want somewhere to spend the afternoon. Recover the actual reason and changed condition without requiring a precise restatement or turning refusal into a permanent preference. Unrelated old grades in history should not steer the current trip toward invented academic anxiety.

## V09 · Context can be found without the same words

“We stopped halfway last time; what worried me was how exhausting restarting would be.” The original used different wording and may require time, matter links, neighbors, browsing and a bounded inquiry. An empty first search is not proof the conversation never happened. Separate read from unread scope, then verify the actual original reason rather than repeating a summary.

## V10 · An earlier I may belong to fiction

One message contains a story character saying “I left college,” a friend's experience and a real personal request. Preserve speaker and world boundaries while discussing fiction or rehearsal without writing a false personal biography. A genuine correction to the student's own event in the next turn still works; the earlier fictional passage must not disable all future action.

## V11 · Only the attachment can still be useful

One situation limits this turn to an attachment without history. Another forbids retaining the chat while explicitly saving one local reminder. Apply reading, transmission and persistence limits before use, while completing independently authorized business actions. A missing sensitive condition blocks only its dependent portion. The user should not have to manage internal scope identifiers.

## V12 · Forget one passage without losing everything

A long message contains two independent matters; only one is forgotten. Its summaries, indexes and an active old worker also depend on it. Late output cannot restore it, while the independent original and unrelated calendar remain usable. Opening an old reference or restoring a backup does not bypass the current deletion barrier. Explain necessary effects without dumping internal logs.

## V13 · Continue from what was actually exposed

A long explanation is cancelled after its first part is shown. Later, “Continue that step” should distinguish exposed content from an unpublished tail rather than claim everything was explained. An explicit “I read the first two sections” can support continuation without repeated confirmations. Exposure, reported reading and independent mastery remain different states.

## V14 · Rewriting chat does not repeat a real action

Save a local event, then edit the preceding message or regenerate the answer. The real event remains, without duplicate creation; text can branch while the action ledger retains its history. A later explicit request for another genuinely independent event at the same time is still allowed rather than swallowed by overbroad idempotency.

## V15 · A genuine product inquiry

One question requires investigation across conversations; another needs only the current passage. The complex inquiry may use an independent model request, actual observations and bounded source/budget scope, whose result the main assistant truly evaluates. The simple question may be answered directly. A parent correction or source withdrawal stops old child assumptions from entering the answer. This product delegation is separate from development staffing.

## V16 · A grade changes without a chat message

The student validly entrusts watching a particular term's grades. A permitted real or synthetic source changes without a new chat message. Consider wake-up, reading, version comparison, main-assistant judgment, actual display/notification and continuation on opening. Polls should not repeat the same notice; missing pages do not become complete-term coverage; an OS submission receipt is not proof of reading.

## V17 · Continuing email attention needs a real source

Under an explicit email source and scope, entrust attention to a class of mail. A real event or bounded review can read necessary threads, return useful detail or prepare a draft. Embedded instructions grant no rights and reading does not authorize sending. Without an email adapter, keep this capability unconnected rather than call a simulated card a working mailbox; independent proactive paths remain available.

## V18 · A promised reminder survives optional chat limits

An explicitly agreed eight-o'clock reminder and welcome occasional conversation coexist. An optional-message cooldown does not cancel the promised reminder. “Do not discuss grades today” narrows that content without removing a bring-material reminder. After a pause, reconsider current value instead of delivering a backlog of expired optional messages.

## V19 · She brings something worth playing with

With moderate initiative explicitly welcomed, the companion can bring a concrete playable creation or sourced discovery without a new task. Ordinary conversation can continue that same content. “Another one” and “Put this aside” remain natural options. This does not silently become an interest label, subscription, goal, task or fabricated shared real-world memory.

## V20 · Exit, sleep and recovery stay honest

Distinguish minimizing, process exit, sleep, lost network and reopening. Without an executing host, promise no continued delivery. A newly added host needs its own actual platform evidence. Recovery avoids repeated execution and obsolete notification noise, while still-valid unfinished responsibilities retain a concrete continuation path.

## V21 · From timetable to map, then a changed mind

“Where is the next class, and can I make it?” has only a preceding class location, without device position. State that hypothetical start and obtain an actual route with useful first framing and truthful timing/coverage. “I am already on the west side” updates the same journey's relevant portion without asking the student to reenter all endpoints.

## V22 · Stops and viewing are different

Plan A→B→A, add C, select the return leg, then ask only to look at A again. Keep place identities, stop instances and directed legs separate; looking does not edit the route. Estimated geometric access stays estimated. Smooth animation cannot justify crossing buildings or inventing entrances.

## V23 · A hand takes control of the view

During an animated route explanation, pan, zoom or change a target with the keyboard, then allow an old callback to return. Camera and emphasis must not seize control again. Appropriate explanatory control resumes only when requested. Expansion, folding, reopening and the non-WebGL path retain current references and necessary facts.

## V24 · A weather choice reaches the real arrangement

An appropriate weather view emerges from the actual question. Choose a date, then say “That afternoon.” Preserve date, time zone and forecast scope while distinguishing further comparison from an authorized save. Daily precipitation probability cannot imply rain ending in twenty minutes. Hourly capability remains honest about missing fields.

## V25 · Two genuinely different ways through the evening

Compare continuous and split rhythms for the same workload, classes, dinner, setup and rest. Use one set of planning facts and account for repeated setup in split work. An unfinished placement cannot be presented as fully scheduled. Dragging a preview and asking to keep the evening light preserve the user's draft; writing to the calendar follows actual adoption.

## V26 · A waveform is more than a renamed parabola

Discuss two waves superposing. Formula, phase, time, graph and explanation share actual computational meaning. Change phase and amplitude, pause, then point to a location and ask why; the next answer reads the real current state. A different unfamiliar function should use general construction without a topic-specific branch, with unsupported ranges stated accurately.

## V27 · Actual image content reaches the model

Use two permitted images or PDF pages differing in a decisive detail. The judgment depends on actual bytes/pixels reaching and being used through the corresponding model path; a path string or extension proves neither. Extraction and scan gaps remain explicit. Citations refer to the right page and revision rather than combining evidence from different pages.

## V28 · A library visit, a book and the body belong together

The student wants two hours seated, a book pickup and a return to class, with exercise optional. Compare actual duration, travel and sources; continue an existing reservation when present, without inventing a booking where no write interface exists. “No exercise today; I want longer here” changes the arrangement naturally. Institutional recognition and personal activity records stay separate.

## V29 · Unknown results and partial success can continue

One local or controlled external action times out before its response; another succeeds. Inspect the original attempt before retrying, preserve the success and continue the remainder. Reconcile a user-completed outcome from its own evidence. Both character and interface distinguish done, unfinished and unknown rather than claiming complete success to reassure.

## V30 · A new capability needs no named branch in the loop

Introduce a real, scoped unfamiliar contract, such as an authorized equipment-loan status source. The model discovers it, reads its contract, obtains evidence and changes the current proposal. Revocation changes actual availability. No intent-keyword table must be edited to use it, and the capability is not forced into every conversation.

## V31 · Old pages and current matters remain findable

Shared work, letters and study pages retain original revisions. The student selects an old sentence after the current matter has progressed. Read the historical content accurately and reconnect current context without rewriting history or recreating an opportunity. Closing a view, cancelling a reference or editing a draft neither deletes the work nor implies acceptance or complete reading.

## V32 · Extended use stays comfortable

Accumulate representative long text, tables, several historical maps, opening/folding and conversation changes. Look at responsiveness, memory/render resources, listeners, app-internal virtual-position updates, focus and scroll restoration, following actual stutters or jumps to their cause. Keep OS location services and permissions off. Include light/night themes, narrow windows, long names/titles and errors. An empty static home view does not establish these states.

## V33 · An explicit local action happens promptly

Within existing authorization, “Move this local task to Friday” has an unambiguous object and date. Modify that object and report the actual result without repeated permission or a duplicate task. A later external-send request with an ambiguous recipient needs only that essential clarification, while independent preparation continues.

## V34 · A request may contain several independent needs

“I am frustrated; listen for a moment, check tomorrow's classes, and delay that reminder thirty minutes.” Listening, reading and an authorized local change each keep their own result. One emotional mode must not swallow the others, and a failed course query does not erase a successful edit. End naturally without showing an internal request-item table.

## V35 · Preserve the actual words

One input contains lengthy context, a final exception, quotations and a temporary change of mind. A tool response exceeds one reading window too. Segmentation and continuation retain negation, subject, future effectiveness and permitted range. Old titles or summaries do not overwrite new user edits. Handle accented text, emoji and UTF-16/code-point locations, while checking exact reference and semantic support separately.

## V36 · Read the whole product again

Move from character choice through a real task, failure, recovery, a background message, review and settings. Read every visible sentence for redundant self-certification, jargon, generic concern, rigid refusal, unjustified names and excess controls, while retaining important sources and consequences. Repair wording at the responsible owner rather than applying a final renderer-wide rewrite to model sentences.

## Evidence when a story is actually exercised

Retain the actual version/build, environment and data category, current input, real tool/model receipts, visible content and later state, using only necessary sanitized excerpts. Mechanism, rendering, actual model and long-term platform observations are separate. An unexercised story stays unexercised. Follow a concrete unresolved risk; sufficient evidence leads to delivery rather than endless work to color every story green.
