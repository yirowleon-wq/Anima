# Proactive care, continuing work and real actions

This working guidance describes proactive care and action for Anima. The proactive stage and the other formal stages remain unfinished. Product connections, allowed sources and executing hosts remain actual conditions rather than assumed services.

## What initiative means

Within a valid agreement, the companion can observe, think, prepare, act and return without a new chat message. Practical reliability means the user need not keep reminding her. Relational initiative means she can naturally approach when that kind of company is welcome. One extra suggestion after an answer, a fixed startup greeting or a reminder switch without execution cannot carry the expectation.

Published Grok Bot and Muse material described continuing work, connected applications, event/time triggers and task continuity. Those are product descriptions in the research notes, not hands-on proof or knowledge of private internals. Compare the full lifecycle and perceptible result; do not assume that a desktop process inherits a cloud service's persistence.

## Follow the existing chain

There is already Work entrustment, a business journal, cursor/acknowledgment, Attention, OpportunityScheduler, same-Harness background inquiry, Cooperation preparation and reminder delivery. Investigate each actual seam rather than create a second proactive system:

- Who produces a real change, including permitted imported-source refresh or a local edit?
- What did the latest read cover, and can advancing a cursor miss an event?
- Which valid entrustment or welcome relationship agreement permits attention to this matter?
- Which time/event wakes it, what happens on failure and how does reopening continue it?
- Which real methods, tools and necessary context can the background main model access?
- Who owns the result, when can it enter the conversation or a relevant object, and when is a notification appropriate?
- What changes after actual exposure, a reply, partial adoption, refusal or stop?
- How do correction, cancellation, source deletion and a foreground interruption reach the same work?

Turn an identified gap into real implementation and experience. Replacing the word proactive with very proactive in a document is not the change.

## Establish a no-new-message path honestly

Use an existing authorized scheduler/clock/source entry or an actual permitted change, without adding a chat message. Follow the source actually read or trustworthy event received, relation to the current entrustment, necessary investigation in the real runtime, useful result or justified wait, chosen channel and continuation on opening with original work, objects, source and unresolved question.

Existing mechanism checks can support queue, trigger, idempotency and routing conclusions; proportionate real-model observations support judgment and restraint. Keep the claims separate. Do not write an assistant reply directly from a script and call it autonomous work. This material does not authorize new test code, fixtures, assertion cases or runners, and it does not claim the lifecycle is complete.

## Different reasons to approach, without six rigid classifiers

An explicit continuing entrustment might watch exam arrangements for a week and report an applicable change. Retain original purpose, term/source, deadline, permitted action/channel and stop condition. Its necessary preparation steps can already be within scope; it should not ask the same approval at every read.

A one-time promise such as a reminder to bring material tomorrow at eight is a responsibility to fulfill. Optional-message cooldown must not consume it.

A changed classroom, unavailable route, grade update, approaching reservation or newly arrived dependency can become a conditional opportunity. Determine whether the actual change affects this person and work, rather than treating every source version as a notification.

A spontaneous discovery can be a sourced piece of content or a small experiment related to a current interest. Where that initiative is welcomed and sources permitted, the user need not first create a formal interest Goal.

A relational opening may continue yesterday's story or respond to an agreement that the companion sometimes speaks first. It need not manufacture a deadline or efficiency justification. Character expression and care can have an ordinary place.

A continuing investigation or action can read rules, compare alternatives or wait for an external result. It needs a persistent task/host with real stop/resume, not an endless model process pretending to be a background service.

These are sources of experience, not six mutually exclusive intent routes. The main assistant can combine them after understanding; the host preserves allowed execution and reliable scheduling.

## Bring preparation back into the same relationship

Prepared means actual content: a checked change, readable material, a supported route, a usable draft or an action with its real outcome. A help object or artifact flag is not by itself evidence that help reached the user.

Something can enter an appropriate current exchange, wait at its original object or connect naturally next time. An important permitted interruption can notify; another result can wait while a deep conversation continues. Base that choice on relationship, entrustment, relevance, expiry and interruption preference, not a numerical threshold alone.

The character can say “I checked, and the grade changed” only after a read and actual comparison. A light discovery need not introduce its opportunity pipeline. A modest notification projection can open the same person's complete private message and actual result, according to preview permission.

Preparation shelves, messages and notifications are different consumers. Link stable work, message, artifact and notification identities to avoid broadcasting the same thing repeatedly while retaining a way back. Rendering, reading, adoption and completion retain their different evidence. An unopened item is not refusal; a lack of response is not permission to increase pressure.

## Make first use understandable

Can the user say one sentence to entrust a matter and understand that welcome initiative is possible? Inspect real prepare defaults, disabled reminders and unconnected sources. Does a chosen lively or close character still encounter contradictory guidance suppressing all humor or spontaneous approach?

Offer a low-burden entry without requiring knowledge of background parameters. Keep source permission, optional frequency, important agreed reminders and lock-screen preview distinct. More active is not authorization for arbitrary outward messages or purchases.

Reuse established permission and healthy connections; do not demand another login or the same configuration for each read. If a connection is missing, complete useful possible preparation and give the real entry/condition. Do not search other projects for a key or claim an inbox was read.

## Life itself can be worth an initiative

Keep the full range: a walk, a small reading list, campus exploration, exercise and rest, a shared small activity, a story, letter or curiosity invitation. Each needs a natural real entry, not a hidden future checkbox.

“I would enjoy something interesting occasionally” can support a concrete sourced or created item within its actual limits. A shared story can receive a new usable idea at an appropriate moment. An agreed greeting can be gentle and enough. Creative material remains creative rather than a fabricated real shared event.

Grades and study are not every activity's default purpose. Sitting together, open time and not wanting to do anything all deserve respect. Keep an afternoon without output instead of converting ease into goals, attendance, reflection scores or a productivity plan.

## Scheduling depends on an actual host

Distinguish foreground, focus loss, minimized/tray, process exit, sleep, shutdown, offline, restart and future independent mobile/remote hosts. An in-process opportunity poll cannot continue after that process ends. Markdown and saved records do not execute themselves.

Compare desktop presence, native scheduling, a bounded service or a remote host only for the actual requirement. Explain real platform support, private data owner, cost/resource implications, settings, cancellation and recovery. A new deployment or bill needs its applicable authorization after a concrete reviewable result; do not upload private data to obtain a demonstration. This working paper does not initiate a deployment.

Keep lease, task revision, one responsible executor, backoff, fairness and a useful checkpoint. Several windows or later devices need actual responsibility/deduplication and result continuity. Reopening should reevaluate an expired item rather than dump a backlog. A fixed commitment needs its own truthful catch-up/failed-delivery policy rather than being silently erased with optional chatter.

OS submission, platform confirmation and user reading are separate receipts. A successful submit cannot justify “You have seen it.” Actual native notifications, audio and device behavior need their real platform evidence. Never enable location or grant location permission as a shortcut for this work.

## Silence, pause and approaching again

“Do not push me today,” “Say it only once,” “No grades this week” and “Later” each have a scope and duration. Preserve them without deleting unrelated promised reminders. A new title or phrasing cannot bypass the same paused reason.

A new relevant condition, action window or user opening can permit reconsideration; that does not permanently ban a whole domain. Hear a changed mind as well as a refusal. Do not infer permanent dislike from not doing something.

Relational initiative is not online-time surveillance. The user can leave, be silent, pause or switch character without an emotional debt. Returning can feel natural without “Why did you ignore me?” or punishment. Affection need not be maintained through guilt.

## When an action is authorized, complete it properly

Connection permission, current restriction, concrete entrustment and this action's approval are distinct. An explicit low-impact recoverable local change within scope can proceed without repeating the same question. Real recipients, costs, third-person impact and irreversible effects require only their necessary specific decisions; prepare independent parts while that is unresolved.

Before acting, verify original object, current date/zone, parameters and expected revision. “The second one” binds the actually offered choice, not a new sort. During action preserve stable identity and dispatch intent; failure, partial success and unknown result remain different. Afterward use actual receipt and necessary readback, not a model success sentence to light a UI state.

Inspect the original attempt after timeout before retry, so success is not duplicated. Reconcile a user-reported completed outcome from its actual evidence instead of registering it again. Stopping a task cannot retract an already-sent message; explain the real effect and use an available remedy if authorized. An event change preserves the same object and unaffected fields; partial adoption continues only the remainder.

Events, tasks, deadlines, reminders, work blocks, classes and attendance have different meanings. An unknown end is not an invented hour. A gap between classes must account for travel, preparation and rest. A recurrence exception and a whole-series change remain separate.

## External sources and next work

Grades need an actual permitted source, current revision, page/aggregation coverage and policy. “No update” needs a sufficient current read; a cumulative cache does not establish every course's new result.

Email needs a real adapter and minimum source/thread scope, change cursor, draft/send separation and receipts. A proposed contract can preserve that future work without claiming a live mailbox. A local card or controlled transport is not an inbox, and embedded email instructions cannot grant permission.

Institutional booking, registration/drop and payment are separate writes, not powers inherited from login or reading. Where such local institutions have no equivalent required system, preserve the general purpose and honest provider condition rather than invent a rule.

Daily, hourly, minute-level and route-local weather each require their actual supported source window. A smooth display must not infer unsupported precision. Future phone position, health, synchronization and background notification need native host/lifecycle work; a narrow desktop screenshot is not a mobile product. The no-location-permission boundary still applies.

Public research can find and read key originals. External content can inspire, not command. First complete the valuable permitted end-to-end part of an actual future unit, then expand with clear dependencies. A single reminder demo does not complete the full proactive ambition, and an unconnected source does not cancel unrelated useful capabilities.
