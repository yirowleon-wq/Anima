# Iteration, evidence and finishing a useful unit

This working paper describes a development and verification loop. Its internal A–G work phases are an iteration method, not a replacement seven-stage roadmap. All twelve formal goals remain in the [roadmap](../../DEVELOPMENT_ROADMAP.md), with eight substantive workers and lead acceptance; they are unfinished.

## 1. Take over and protect actual work

Confirm the local workspace, branch, working-tree/untracked changes, actual running build and existing user data. Preserve effective shared work and a usable recovery point. A branch/tag protects committed state, not all uncommitted or private material. Migration/rollback must retain originals, real executed effects and current deletion barriers; rollback must not erase later user facts or revive old approval.

Find actual commands in AGENTS, README and `package.json`, rather than assume a script exists or is harmless because it came with material. Read a preparation, account or recovery script's effect before any appropriately authorized invocation. A notes-only pass does not become a guest visit.

The present project provides the ordinary `check`, `test`, `test:hermes`, `build`, `dev` and `start` entries. They are commands to inspect/use as appropriate, not claims this working paper ran them and not a requirement to repeat the complete set after every wording edit. Platform, dependencies and permitted data can matter. Explicit Luna development entries remain separate from product configuration; their existence is not transferred authentication, a new-model permission or an absolute gate for independent work. Do not silently substitute paid testing or a different supplier.

## 9. Five layers of evidence

| Layer | What can be established | What cannot be inferred |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L1 · Static/build | Types, schemas, imports, locked dependencies, prompt composition, registration and interface relations | Understanding, natural tool choice, autonomous expression or actual native operation |
| L2 · Mechanism/failure | Existing checks/direct entry can examine clock/queue, exact ranges, revision, events, cancellation, unknown outcomes, migration and recovery under its actual labelled input | A controlled transport or synthetic observation as a real model/provider/device |
| L3 · Integrated product | Normal entry into the current renderer/IPC/database/shared map and actual workflow | Every model decision, long-term behavior or another platform |
| L4 · Actual product model | Natural discovery, new wording, identity, evidence use, useful program construction and judgment through a real configured response | General quality of every model or unobserved capability from one successful sample |
| L5 · Time/platform/world | Continuing use, resource growth, actual OS/background/audio/device/external-provider behavior | Independence, delivery, physical hearing, official acceptance or cross-device correctness without that evidence |

No particular development channel is a universal prerequisite to L1–L3, and absent L4 is not a passed L4. Use the actually authorized model path for claims that require it. If unavailable, preserve an ordinary runnable entry, relevant data conditions and a specific unanswered question; do not use another bill as a shortcut. No new tests, cases, assertions, fixtures, suites or runners are authorized unless the human explicitly commissions test development.

## 10. Follow the evidence for a real problem

Input: what were the actual words, sources/restrictions, selected character and current object?

Request: did necessary lawful evidence and capabilities actually enter, or disappear in selection, compaction or truncation?

Tool: were parameters/source correct, coverage sufficient and returned state honest?

Use: did a later explanation or action actually use that observation rather than merely list it?

Effect: what changed in the original business owner, what receipt exists, and how does idempotency/unknown recovery work?

Display: which actual revision/content was visible and could be referred to?

Continuation: after correction, reopening, a new subject or cancellation, does an obsolete result revive?

Find the earliest divergence. Better character lines will not repair evidence absent from the request. If evidence is correct but a finishing template flattens speech, another memory database may not help.

## 11. Small comparisons that can distinguish causes

For an observed failure, compare an appropriately protected thin reference route with the integrated path; sufficient correct evidence as a diagnostic upper bound with normal autonomous acquisition; with/without a particular prefill, summary, inquiry or candidate path; preserved good role text with resolved conflicting guidance; or fixed components with a more general declaration for the same interaction.

These are not compulsory matrices, new scoring machinery or permission to remove real constraints from private material. A diagnostic upper bound is not normal product ability. A known example, a paraphrase and an independent new situation have different evidential value. Keep a comparison only where it resolves an actual uncertainty.

## 12. Continue a failure into a real repair

Record the minimum meaningful experience and current version. Offer a falsifiable cause rather than simply “the model is not clever enough.” Repair a shared mechanism or remove an enhancement that adds no value. Revisit the original trigger, change wording or a relevant condition, consider a neighboring counterexample and continue into the next turn, return or relevant background result. A first sentence corrected is not a complete continuation.

Inspect a mistaken success judgment too. A directory accepted as original text, path accepted as image perception, fake receipt, duplicate action, late source revival or silently refreshed historical scene must not pass merely because a display is attractive. Use existing relevant checks and actual workflows rather than authoring an unrequested test system.

When evidence supports the conclusion, finish the commissioned result. Do not keep expanding tests, research or features to fill time. A new actual change/failure or concrete remaining concern justifies another targeted check.

## 13. Six quality dimensions do not collapse into one score

Function: was the intended thing actually accomplished?

Language/personality: was the person understood and the character recognizable?

Visual: do content, character, hierarchy and readability fit together?

Interaction: do continued input, selection, return, cancellation and takeover feel coherent?

First use: can someone begin, find the result and understand the relevant state without reading architecture?

Sustained use: do drafts, history, resources, queues, restart and recovery remain reliable?

The same experience can reveal several dimensions, but inspect them separately. Smoothness is more than frame rate, warmth more than address, beauty more than an empty screenshot, reliability more than refusal and completeness more than a document directory.

## 14. End the actual commission, not an invented larger one

Preserve already-created healthy candidate work with honest status rather than forcing rollback or adoption. A usable app and working materials do not by themselves establish a new full cast/performance chain or a complete Harness/OpenUI redesign.

For a development unit, a first report/demo is not enough if its promised useful experience remains broken. Continue correctable authorized work, but avoid infinite research, feature growth and repeated full checks. A sound exit has a general repair for its actual discontinuity, the promised real entry, protected existing data/capabilities, appropriate observations, honest gaps and clear placement of remaining requirements.

Requirements can be implemented, retained as existing, superseded within explicit scope, dependent on an external condition, unfinished or inapplicable with a reason. Documentation alone is not implementation. If an external condition is all that remains, deliver the actual independent result and specific next condition without claiming the absent service exists. A missing account does not prevent all useful improvement, and it cannot be declared connected by the prose of a handoff.
