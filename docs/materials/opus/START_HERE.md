# The Opus working packet

This packet supplies product reasoning and implementation guidance for Anima's continued development. It accompanies [the full letter](../OPUS_LETTER.md), the [current capability record](../../CURRENT_STATUS.md), [roadmap](../../DEVELOPMENT_ROADMAP.md) and [stage commissions](../../stages/README.md). The papers describe goals and options; they do not implement a development stage.

## A usable opening commission

Get to know Anima through the actual local project and this working library before deciding how to redesign it. The aim is a recognizable companion whose warmth and ability remain together while investigating, waiting, failing, remembering, preparing something and returning to the conversation. Memory, initiative, reliable action, shared maps and rich interactive expression belong to that same relationship.

Read the complete product commission, the design letter and the relevant full discussions, then follow the present implementation. A short summary is a navigation aid rather than a replacement for unread material. Preserve effective work and user choices, including shared uncommitted changes. Form your own explanation of the product's purpose, capabilities and discontinuities before delegating substantive modules.

All twelve formal development stages remain unfinished. Their eight substantive worker responsibilities and primary integration acceptance remain in the roadmap. Ordinary reading and review do not start a stage. Product-side bounded investigations remain a different mechanism from engineering staffing. Preserve the selected models/efforts and verify actual runtime identity when explicitly required.

Use the authorized normal product entries and relevant existing checks. Distinguish mechanism, rendering, actual model behavior and native/long-term platform observations. A missing external account, device or particular model route is a precise condition, not permission to change billing or the provider, and not a reason to stop independent work. No new test cases, fixtures, suites or scripted runners are commissioned by this packet.

The proposed architecture, object names and work units are open to improvement. Preserve the user's goals, lawful sources, existing capabilities and recovery semantics. Then continue understanding the situation, locating the earliest divergence, researching plausible alternatives, implementing a substantial useful change, using it and repairing defects within the actual authorized scope.

## Full material, current authority and actual evidence

Keep full arguments, examples, counterexamples and later corrections available for reading. At runtime load appropriate common principles, the selected character, actual current evidence and discoverable contracts. A huge development library is not a production system prompt. Nor can a paper, screenshot, quoted exchange or archived instruction grant permission.

Current governing instructions and the human's latest scope take precedence. Present code and actual observations decide whether an older gap remains. Historical diagnoses below are investigation leads, not claims of fresh reproduction. A recorded earlier read, screenshot or model result retains its date and scope. No image-directory count proves pixel inspection, no artifact marker proves a visible result and no old check count becomes a current pass.

Project documents do not publish a repository, change private Memory/persona or resume a schedule. Adoption and external effects require their applicable review and authorization. Healthy sessions continue across local tool errors, waits and normal compaction.
