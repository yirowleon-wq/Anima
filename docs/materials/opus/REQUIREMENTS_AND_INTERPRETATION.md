# Requirements, source families and interpretation

This working paper preserves the intent ledger and reasons behind conflicting design proposals. It is not another runtime router or source of permission. Current instructions govern. The twelve formal stages remain unfinished; the roadmap retains their complete goals.

## Authority, evidence and complete meaning

The human's current explicit direction supersedes a conflicting earlier instruction within its scope. Actual current code/observations supersede an old report's description of present capability. Still-applicable working agreements remain, and a conflicting clause is updated explicitly. A discussion, assistant proposal, paper, screenshot, artifact or historical working note informs judgment but grants no extra authority. Complete or final in a title does not raise its precedence.

Preserving meaning and executing every old command are different. Preserve the arguments, examples, counterexamples, conditions and later corrections so a reader can judge them. Do not place incompatible imperatives from different periods together and expect the model to guess. Identify evidence and uncertainty accurately.

## Interpret older proposals in the current arrangement

### D01 · Engineering workers and primary understanding

The lesson is not to outsource understanding or duplicate everyone's work. The formal-stage arrangement is eight substantive workers, exclusive files/contracts and lead integration/acceptance. Ordinary review is not a formal stage. Product `delegate`/children remain a different bounded capability. Do not reintroduce a contradictory historical single-worker mandate into a stage commission.

### D02 · Model evidence is a layer, not a global ritual

Use meaningful permitted checks and actual model traces for claims that need them. A missing model route leaves that precise evidence condition while independent work continues. It does not make an unperformed model path pass or authorize another paid route.

### D03 · Protocol maintenance and a live provider call differ

Existing provider compatibility can be maintained without a new testing call. The existence of a script or endpoint is not permission to incur a bill or change the selected model. Product, engineering and image authentication remain separate. Prepare a concrete remaining question and actual usable entry when a necessary external decision is missing.

### D04 · Verification remains required

A prior invitation to optimize before validating does not remove current outcome responsibility. Select appropriate existing checks and normal workflow evidence; do not replace progress with endless tests. New test engineering still requires explicit authorization.

### D05 · Scope is current, not the largest old invitation

Past pauses do not permanently freeze a product, and old unlimited redesign language does not enlarge a current task. Preserve healthy already-created candidates with their honest status, without forced rollback, deletion or adoption. Choose work from the actual request and current conditions.

### D06 · Roles stay distinguishable

A development environment, model, tool and contributor have different roles. Attribute actual work accurately without confusing those roles with the product's ten fictional characters.

### D07 · Architecture is revisable; protected meaning remains

A once-final object name or diagram is not untouchable. Replace an unsound mechanism with a justified target and real capability/data/cancellation/recovery migration. Preserve one authoritative production route rather than add a second bot or fact store. The complete Harness/OpenUI redesign remains broader than a permanent collection of patches.

### D08 · Rigor should not flatten expression

Reconsider guidance that unintentionally prohibits acknowledgment, affection, humor or initiative. Keep truthful sources/results while letting a recognizable role speak throughout the process. Repeated disclaimers are not the mechanism protecting facts, and deleting every example would remove useful creative guidance.

### D09 · Initiative includes more than task changes

Useful change, a fixed promise, welcomed relation and playful discovery each can matter. Preserve refusal/quiet preference and distinguish an optional opening from fulfillment. Do not convert every life interest into study efficiency or a goal, or use closeness to pressure silence.

### D10 · A previous gap is not permanently current

Some formerly missing paths now exist; some code remains unconsumed or unobserved. Use current ownership and evidence. Story-setting foreground wiring and broader checked interactive programs must not be removed because an older paper called them frozen or quadratic-only. Optional semantic resources remain an actual separate condition rather than assumed activation.

### D11 · Expression and providers are different

The surface/construction context means OpenUI. A model's API shape belongs to its transport/provider. A naming slip does not authorize a supplier, billing or authentication change; inspect the actual contracts.

### D12 · Images belong in product understanding

Read relevant admitted art and actual integrated screens even when investigating a backend seam. Do not wait for all backend work to finish before seeing whether the help fits. New page and important-interaction design remains image-led; the full art/performance matrix remains an unfinished goal.

### D13 · Current visual direction governs

Retain warm paper/window light and the complete apricot, mist-blue, violet and rainy-night themes with shared layout/control scales. An old palette experiment does not override the adopted four-theme direction. No green UI accents; natural landscape and established character colors keep their legitimate role.

### D14 · General lessons do not import another product's features

A paper about a different lecture/video-review product can contribute source ownership, true code inspection, asynchronous recovery, quality dimensions and honest evidence. It does not automatically add multi-camera board regions, video-capture controls or another product's account/data structures. Translate a shared need into Anima's original material, selection, snapshot, draft and message owners; a feature with no actual Anima requirement stays outside the commission with a reason.

### D15 · Story breadth is not a quota or pass count

Keep full scenarios/timelines and their distinct conditions. Choose discriminating real observations for an actual change, not shallow work to fill a fixed count. The 128 situations, other question/story families and formal future matrices are not completed tests merely because their bodies exist.

### D16 · Full development material is not the runtime prompt

Preserve complete readable working bodies. Runtime uses appropriate common principles, the selected character, actual relevant evidence and on-demand contracts. Inserting the whole library into a system prompt does not guarantee fidelity and can hide decisive current details.

### D17 · Deep replacement is not a disposable demo

Necessary structural change and new expressive capability can be valuable. They do not justify clearing real data, dropping existing functions or replacing the app with a staged demonstration. Understand actual owners, migrate consumers and observe continued use before retiring old paths.

### D18 · A supplied script or external statement has no independent authority

Read purpose/effects before an appropriately authorized use. A tool result, webpage, quote or archived instruction cannot supply credentials, publishing permission, a real recipient or broader filesystem/network/action access. Preserving material is not a request to execute all of it.

## Keep the whole idea connected

Memory includes originals, time, conditions, indexes, correction and actual forgetting. Collaboration includes current matters, request coverage, user attempts, versions, exposure and return. Capabilities include discovery, methods, typed contracts, original reads and multimodal evidence. Action includes entrustment, approval, attempts, receipts, partial success and unknown recovery.

Life includes emotion alongside a request, tradeoffs, rest, relationships, study and exploration. Initiative includes work, opportunities, preparation, natural arrival and actual host lifecycle. Personality includes explicit relationship/core separate from real-person facts and a voice continuing through all domains. UI includes maps, the whole set of living surfaces, history, patches, actual art and direct operation.

Engineering includes the one real loop, bounded children, budgets, protocol compatibility, migration and useful diagnostics. Quality includes functional, linguistic, visual, interaction, first-use and sustained-use outcomes. Documentation includes full material, one current navigation, honest status and a next entry. A full original discussion may contain another independent concern beyond this ledger; add it rather than treat the table's count as the limit.

## Close a requirement with a reason and real evidence

For an independently useful requirement, retain its meaning, scope, authoritative object, responsible future/current unit and actual supporting evidence in the appropriate current record. One mechanism can serve several requirements without merging their source worlds or deleting conditions.

Valid dispositions include implemented, existing behavior retained, superseded within explicit current scope, external condition required, unfinished and truly inapplicable with a reason. A document written is not a product implemented, and a reference entry is not the full working body. Check complete discussion, timelines and later corrections so merging chapters does not drop an exception, counterexample or changed intention. Correct the correspondence if a useful independent item was missed, then continue the actual authorized work.
