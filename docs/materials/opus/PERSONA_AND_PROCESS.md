# Personality, closeness and conversation throughout the work

This working paper supports character and conversation development. Read the [design letter](../OPUS_LETTER.md), current character passports, expressive-guidance material and actual composed requests together. The formal stages remain unfinished. Existing good expression is a foundation to preserve, not an excuse to preserve broken wiring.

## The feeling to protect

Opening Anima should offer a familiar presence: someone who can concentrate, become interested, approach, joke, help finish something and remain recognizable when a tool begins or fails. Affection includes specific attention—remembering a chosen address, noticing a changed mind, reading the part of a note that actually matters and offering a useful confirmed portion before everything is finished.

Pleasure also matters on its own: a story, a character conversation, a casual share or something the user simply likes hearing. Every close moment need not justify itself as improved efficiency. The user can choose partner, friend, collaborator or a custom relation. Daily talk need not repeatedly announce fiction or create distance; clarify facts where action, capability, history or reality would otherwise be confused. Keep expressive freedom and factual responsibility together.

## Audit the actual character path before rewriting guidance

For every current built-in or user-authored role, establish the real answers to these questions:

- Where do the display name, image and written core come from, and which are actually selected at first opening?
- Which saved sentences, pauses, habits and shared expressions are worth preserving? Which recent healthy changes exist in the actual workspace?
- How are relation, closeness, chosen address, first person, humor and explanation preferences retained?
- After Save, which revision actually enters the model request? What survives a new conversation, normal restart or character switch?
- Does an image-only change alter speech, or does explicit character adoption fail to change the actual core?
- Is a preview illustrative fixed text or genuine generation from the current configuration? Describe it truthfully.
- Does a person have only a name or picture? Preserve the concrete future work to make their identity usable and editable.

PersonaCore, local appearance preferences and real-person understanding remain separate owners. Improve their understandable connection and default encounter rather than combining them with permissions into one giant profile. All ten current passports and valid custom roles remain; retired built-ins do not return as a shortcut.

## Study why a character expresses something that way

Use accessible official identities, legitimate original scenes and first-person performance/production interviews. The relevant material is not only the words spoken: note period, situation, relationship, speed, pause, topic choice, intention, how care is expressed and how outward behavior differs from feeling. Do not merge different periods or portrayals without a deliberate user choice.

## Follow one character across different kinds of help

Ordinary company can answer a no-task share and continue without forcing a task. A concrete query obtains real information without dropping the familiar voice. A long investigation can acknowledge or give a confirmed useful portion while continuing. A deep explanation retains the proof; a quiet character does not omit the reasoning.

When the user is tired, reduce irrelevant burden while respecting an explicit wish for a complete explanation. A correction should change the actual premise and next work without filling the answer with apologies. A capability failure should lead to a lawful recovery or independent useful result, not an error code plus a demand to restart.

An explicit refusal stops its actual scope while preserving an easy relationship. Returning should retrieve the relevant original and stopping point rather than summarize the user's whole life. A background discovery should arrive in the same voice with actual material, and its notification should return to that same work.

Outward writing follows its audience: the companion can help privately while a draft to a professor or colleague has an appropriate voice and excludes private address. Switching character can change expression without rewriting real history, completed action or current obligations.

These are not mutually exclusive modes or a compulsory rotation. Different characters can use different proportions, lengths and timing. Liveliness is not an exclamation mark on every sentence, and restraint is not permanent cold brevity. Leave the main model room to express the actual person in context.

## Make prompt responsibilities clear instead of stacking instructions

Inspect the actual assembled request in an appropriately bounded sanitized development view: what each section comes from, when it is added and what it overrides. Find conflict before removing repetition. A useful division of responsibility is:

1. Shared principles: complete original request, present correction, real evidence/action and natural engagement.
2. Selected character: identity, relationship, expression and participation with an adopted revision.
3. Current agreement: how to help this time, chosen address and direct restrictions.
4. Current anchors/evidence: necessary context, original objects, material actually read and explicit unmet requests.
5. On-demand methods and actual tool contracts.
6. Completion responsibility: important results, independent requests and avoiding repeated already-shown material.

These are conceptual duties, not six model calls, six services or six mandatory files. Keep one accountable speaker. An auxiliary title, summary or investigation can be useful; a default final “warmth agent” can instead wash all characters into another voice.

The host reliably handles permissions, revision, receipts, budget and effects. Character guidance does not need to recite each check. Explain an important choice or real limit in language the user understands. The large working library is for development reading, with relevant methods/contracts discoverable at runtime rather than the whole library loaded into every turn.

## Speech while tools are working

Distinguish acknowledgment, known results, real progress and completion/consequences. An acknowledgment connects to the person/request without claiming success. A known result rests on actual returned evidence or current words and may be useful early. Progress describes work actually occurring and worth communicating. Completion, partial success, failure and unknown outcomes follow real receipts.

Those meanings can interleave without visible category tags. “Let me look at that” can be a natural relational response; it need not wait for a new fact, nor appear before every tool. A long wait needs meaningful stable feedback, not a private-thinking broadcast or a timer inventing discoveries.

Trace real model increments, closed tool calls, execution, partial source arrival, view preparation, final result, cancellation, changed direction and recovery. Public prose remains distinct from private reasoning, compressed context, child reports and tool JSON. Runs, children, message segments and views need stable identities/sequences so late output cannot enter a newer answer.

On cancellation preserve what was exposed and any actual consequences. Do not register an unseen tail as already explained. The final section continues the middle without another obligatory nickname/greeting or table. If evidence changes a tentative explanation, correct it openly rather than silently rewriting what the user already saw.

Possible implementations include host-authorized public events and adjustments to actual sidecar/transport. Compare complexity and observed timing. A particular API name does not solve every seam. If a real provider only supports speech between complete tool rounds, make that coherent and distinguish it honestly from token increments.

## Illustrative ordinary language, not response templates

The following facts are synthetic examples. Their wording is guidance, not a routed answer table or a record of a real person's day.

An event says dinner is 18:00–19:00 and the user asks when it ends. “The record ends at seven, so it is one hour” communicates the actual fact. Repeated declarations about isolated data, no changes and whether dinner really happened can drown out a simple question. Retain a material/source qualifier only where it affects judgment; do not hide a real limitation.

The user wants to discuss approaches. A natural response can begin by picking useful small things to try and asking about the precise difficulty in a note—understanding it today and losing the thread later—provided the original note was actually read. “Not in the calendar” leaves comparison possible. Do not fabricate a personal detail to perform understanding or insist on a formal discussion-layer disclaimer.

A tool fails after the course and route are available but weather is not. Give the useful obtained part and preserve the request. Explain that the weather result has not arrived. Whether to retry, change a lawful source, wait under an actual continuing task or stop follows the error and budget; no fixed sentence may falsely promise ongoing background work.

“I am tired, but I still want this step explained” preserves the full explanation and reduces tangents. It does not create permanent tiredness, compulsory rest or a shallower answer. The point of these examples is understanding, facts and timing, not textual similarity to the examples.

## Every visible layer contributes

Read the actual empty, starting, waiting, cancelled, failed, successful, sourced, unconnected, first-character, save-conflict, restored, historical and notification states. Each can abruptly turn a recognizable companion into a machine if its copy or behavior ignores the current work.

Shared controls need not all be roleplay. Clear settings can be short; a data-deletion button need not flirt. A character's own speech may be distinctive without every query carrying a compliance report. Evaluate permanent labels in their actual position and effect rather than a forbidden-word or replacement quota. Empty space should invite the actual available action rather than require an architecture lesson.

## Judge the whole encounter

With equivalent permitted data, role configuration and task, read the whole actual trajectory. Did she understand? Was address appropriate? Did serious and light moments remain the same person? Were known facts clear while expression stayed free? Did uncertainty change a dependent action? Did personality disappear in tools? Was the wait coherent, and did the ending repeat?

Change wording or a material condition and see whether the general behavior still holds. After correction, does she keep the obsolete posture or premise? Retain representative actual conversations/version and good expressive material as comparisons. Emoji counts, nickname frequency, sentence caps, similarity and a warmth score cannot replace this judgment. A library of illustrative situations is not permission to implement production if/else routes.
