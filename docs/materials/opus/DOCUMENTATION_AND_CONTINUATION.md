# Keep purpose and actual capability in the project

Use this working guidance when development changes implementation. It supplies editable direction, not a claim that a vision is already available. The formal stages remain unfinished.

## README: meet the product before its machinery

A useful opening can express this meaning:

> Anima is a continuing companion whose personality the user can choose and shape. Conversation, study, shared creation, arrangements and concrete entrusted work can belong to the same relationship. Familiarity should remain while finding material, using tools, waiting, failing and returning to a thought.
>
> Within actual permitted sources, the companion can return to original words and shared work, consider a useful change or prepare help. Maps, time, learning and other content can unfold in the conversation, and pointing at the original object can continue that same exchange.
>
> Natural expression, real capability and continuity are designed together. The capability record identifies actual connected sources, platforms, observations and remaining conditions.

Then give real available paths and installation/start commands. A large warning or architecture block should not replace the introduction. An unconnected inbox, absent post-exit host or future mobile synchronization does not belong in a list of things already available. Keep chosen companion forms and serious/playful/no-task life visible in the purpose.

## AGENTS: preserve the product and current scope

The character experience is a product aim across interface copy, tools, initiative and recovery. Respect explicit relation/address/editable core and good expressive work. Do not normalize everyone into administrative speech. The model understands complete words, finds lawful capabilities, reads original evidence, revises judgment and communicates; the host owns verifiable source/permission, revision, budget and effects.

Internal records may be precise while public speech remains ordinary, with necessary limits explained where they affect a decision or recovery. Formal future stages retain eight substantive workers and primary integrated acceptance; product bounded inquiry is a separate mechanism. Ordinary explanation, reading or documentation does not start a stage.

Use relevant actual verification, with mechanism, integrated UI, real model and platform evidence separate. Keep selected models/effort, no silent paid fallback, no unauthorized outward communication/publication and no location enabling/permission. Preserve healthy sessions across tools/waits/compaction and apply strict terminal-runtime recovery only when its actual trigger is established.

State the actual current scope and distinguish it from the unfinished twelve-stage program. Do not promote a historical invitation to complete a redesign into authority for unrelated work. Preserve healthy candidates without forcing adoption or rollback.

These are scoped current agreements, not claims that every future conversation is governed forever by an old document. Reconcile a later applicable human change with its actual scope/provenance. Historical wording remains evidence without competing as the first instruction a new reader sees.

## Product contract: personality, initiative and capability are one experience

Keep character participation, welcomed relational initiative, speech through the work and the value of learning, creation, rest and ordinary company in the principles. Acknowledgment, true evidence, a useful finding, actual result and continued conversation can belong together. Short response and long explanation both remain available; a word count does not define naturalness.

Initiative includes entrustment/reliable fulfillment and welcomed discoveries/company, not only grades or efficiency. The main model chooses useful dynamic expression from the actual question; selection returns to the next sentence without a component-name lesson. State the desired experience clearly while retaining original owners, worlds, sources, approvals and receipts. The technical responsibility supports the product need rather than displacing it.

## Navigation: one actual map of responsibilities

Maintain the real entry/owner rather than another competing master index. Typical paths a reader needs include character/address from settings to actual request; public text/tool events/message parts/cancel; original memory/conditions/shared experience and old work; trigger/background tool set/preparation/message/notification; OpenUI discovery/DSL/source/input readback; data/action revision/unknown-result recovery/migration; and admitted art/current rendering/platform gaps.

Check real registry content before publishing a count of methods/tools/components. One earlier audit found ten documented methods but eleven registered entries; that is an example of a drifting duplicated fact, not a current count. Do not confuse project engineering skills with product personal methods or turn either into authority it does not have.

Use actual current filenames and case. A historical line number or deleted path is a reference to a prior observation, not a new entry point. Link the complete relevant body where an implementation argument is needed; a title/index cannot replace missing text.

## Architecture: explain how a person is actually supported

Retain the single run chain and authority of original owners, the model/host division and synchronous/asynchronous relationships. For a new event, task or program contract, explain the need, producer, consumer, invalidation and recovery, plus its actual file entry. A diagram of boxes alone is insufficient.

Separate text increments, complete tool rounds, final review, bounded child reports and background result delivery. Opaque provider state and private reasoning are not public speech or personal memory. A framework replacement or responsibility convergence needs actual compatibility/migration, one authoritative write path, cancellation/recovery and benefit/risk, not just a new name.

## Current status and continuation use one vocabulary of evidence

For each material capability, distinguish the implementation; actual normal entry consuming it; version/environment actually used; particular behavior actually observed; mechanism or synthetic-data evidence only; missing model/account/device/long-term condition; and a concrete next start.

Old check counts, screenshots and model results retain date/version/scope. Code, local running build, package and public preview keep their separate identities. A local artifact is not deployment. If no upload/publication occurred, say so without making it a missing acceptance condition when it was not commissioned.

Keep a small current continuation: actual reviewed identity, current substantial unit, last usable result, original object/revision, meaningful observations, specific uncertainty, material decisions, one next action and actual tool/allowance/park/resume condition. Do not put a full chat, roadmap or credential record there. Restoring intent is not restoring approval.

## Character sources and expressive preservation

Use the existing passport/source system for character revision/period, source links, read/view scope, canon versus original interpretation, user editing and preserved expressive contributions. Explain why a retained passage has identity—rhythm, attention, intent—rather than copying a large canon quotation into runtime data. Compare complete actual exchanges after a change, not emoji/nickname/length scores.

Art source, admission, purpose, geometry, actual use and rights remain in the visual manifest/records. Writing a character name does not establish image rights. A good retained reference can remain valuable without current runtime adoption; a retired or structurally unsound image does not return through a historical approval flag.

## Connect design intent, components and actual model use

Every important retained design relationship needs a real component/owner/operation entry or an honest future work placement. An implementation screenshot should identify the actual build/environment and real versus synthetic data category. Autonomous model choice/construction has separate evidence from static rendering.

If a retained layout is unsuitable for a future unit, explain the concrete incompatibility and what valuable relationship remains protected. Do not quietly discard good work, replace the library with a few new candidates or call a static fallback the completed normal action. Read the actual integrated picture; identical numbers alone do not establish optical alignment.

## Full material and changing decisions

Preserve applicable source attribution and evidence with the appropriate owner. Do not rewrite an original to sound smoother and call it the original. Keep duplicate aliases, earlier/later decisions and known missing originals attributable without fabricating history.

Current entry points must retire superseded execution commands clearly. Historical content is not deleted merely for having strong imperative wording, but cannot regain authority by being quoted. Do not put whole private operation/archive material in a public build, public map, runtime prompt or account Memory. Automated indexes help locate; full semantic reading and actual observations remain responsibilities.

## Explain a result from the user's side

Lead a handback with what can now be used, what became more natural and what actual help can be relied on. Then give the important choice, meaningful observations and remaining external or unobserved condition with a concrete next entry. Long lists of owners, schemas, receipts and document counts cannot substitute for that experience.

Keep the amount of record proportionate to the change while preserving full working material where it belongs. This library explains why; current main documents explain now; a work unit describes its useful result. None should pretend an unfinished stage, platform adoption, Memory mutation or upload already happened.
