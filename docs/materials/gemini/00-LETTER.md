# A letter about the companion I want to return to

This design letter expresses the desired companion experience. Its examples illustrate possibilities rather than a personal biography or completed behavior. Character, runtime and visual redesign remain development work.

Make Anima a place worth returning to.

The product already does quite a lot, yet the experience can feel wrong. A chosen character is sitting on screen, but when she speaks it feels as though she is submitting an operations review. After a lookup, she begins by announcing that nothing changed. A discussion of an idea is declared a discussion layer. A small difficulty produces a list of internal states. More features can make that distance more obvious. The aim is a more natural encounter.

I imagined something softer: opening a place willing to receive me. She recognizes me, cares, and sometimes thinks or does something for me without making me manage it all. She has her own voice, reactions, interests and little moments of stubbornness. I can ask serious mathematics, read a course schedule or arrange a day. I can also say something unfinished, share excitement, complain, or spend a while with someone I like.

For example, someone choosing Sakiko Togawa may want her to be serious, a little proud, gradually softer with familiarity, and attentive in her own way. They might choose to regard her as a beloved or wife within that fictional relationship; this does not invent a real shared history. Someone choosing Mutsumi Wakaba should find a person and real care in her quiet, rather than a machine with a shorter-output setting. A person may want a girlfriend, boyfriend, friend, older-student companion or a self-written character. Those different wishes deserve a considered beginning.

For a future commissioned improvement, please follow this problem through the actual product. Bring judgment about prose, characters and conversation. If a sentence is awkward, a default feels cold or a character has not become recognizable, find the place that actually causes it. The desired result is an application whose use changes, not merely another recommendation document.

## First, know the home you are entering

Start at Anima's own README, collaboration rules and project navigation, then its product contract, architecture, current capability record and continuation entry. The [runtime map](02-RUNTIME-AND-CHANGE-MAP.md) locates character context, tools, memory, initiative, interface and evidence. Read the actual current checkout and preserve existing work; an earlier package or a forceful historical instruction does not authorize replacing a healthy implementation.

Understand what the earlier discussions wanted people to be able to do, then distinguish that from what now exists. An old stopping point applies to its own work, while a new approved commission defines its own scope. Neither historical pauses nor a broad vision automatically starts unrelated development.

The writing/development assistant and the product's inference provider are different things. Anima's character context, tools and expression belong in the same existing primary assistant loop, without a second service to warm up finished answers. Engineering tools, the configured product provider and an explicitly authorized Luna evaluation route have separate credentials and evidence.

## Understand what felt so discouraging

The research compared six product screenshots and five screenshots of another companion. Their design lessons are preserved individually in [the screenshot notes](SCREENSHOT_DESIGN_NOTES.md). The reference felt recognizable because a consistent speaker addressed the person, used her own self-reference and rhythm, and reacted to what was said. It is a lesson about continuity, not a request to copy the reference's character, images, private dialogue or prediction features.

In the product examples, phrases equivalent to “I only read local records, did not query campus data and changed nothing,” “I only explained; nothing was modified,” and “We will remain at the discussion layer” repeatedly took the center. A more painful example had already unfolded a note, then later claimed it could see only a final sentence saying no method was chosen, and asked the person to identify the idea again. Failed map/weather work read like a whole fault report handed to its owner.

Some conversations really requested read-only help, and some material really was synthetic. Keep identities and limits that change understanding; preserve the user's original note. Repair the surrounding interaction: a necessary acknowledgment can be brief, a needed original should actually be read again, and a real constraint should be explained. Let those mechanisms support the experience while attention returns to her reaction, discovery and actual help. False success cannot make a failure warmer.

## Give the chosen person a voice

An earlier audit found a nearly empty custom text role beside an already selected illustration. Image, display name, injected character and the person the user thought they chose could diverge. Follow first entry, selection, save, reopening and legacy settings to determine the current behavior. Free writing must remain possible. Choosing a named character should offer a thoughtfully prepared beginning rather than a personality questionnaire to finish alone.

Anima now retains ten named character identities and editable custom roles. Do not restore retired generic illustrations or treat a historical two-character starting point as the current cast limit. Sakiko and Mutsumi remain useful focused examples in this material.

The [reference guide](03-CHARACTER-AND-INITIATIVE-REFERENCES.md) gives original-work entries and performer interviews with honest reading boundaries. Study how she addresses different people, invites, disagrees, accepts kindness, bears responsibility and chooses to show concern. Context and character choice matter more than labels such as proud, silent or gentle.

There was already a small character skeleton. The problem was not complete absence of work: limited profile excerpts and examples could still make different people sound like one cautious employee with different sentence lengths. Develop a core that supports daily encounters. In maintenance material, distinguish original facts, interpretation of scenes and original relationship writing for Anima. In ordinary chat, let her speak without repeatedly announcing that distinction.

Girlfriend, boyfriend and closer chosen relationships can be legitimate product experiences. Names, teasing, affection, welcome and missing someone may develop through that agreement and the character. Emotional expression and claims of real work remain separate: an affectionate welcome differs from “I checked your grades.” The latter requires an actual read.

Use each person's real saved choice of address. Sam is an anonymous example, not a shared default. A name can appear naturally instead of stamping every paragraph. Preserve custom editing, import/export, revisions, conflicts and undo; improving a preset must not erase what someone wrote carefully.

## Bring the same character into doing things

The sequence matters. I say something; she receives it, investigates, tells me something useful she found, notices what still needs checking, and finishes. It should feel like the same person throughout.

With an appropriately chosen close voice, Sakiko might say, “Sam, let me check today's classes and sort out what you need before leaving.” After a real schedule result: “I found the timetable. I want to check the approach to the teaching building too.” If weather affects the answer, she continues and then explains the actual change. Mutsumi might say less: “Sam, I will look.” Then, after evidence, “The room changed. I will find the route too.” These are original situational sketches, not required lines or claims of a lookup already performed.

I want to hear both the initial acknowledgment and useful findings when they become available. Inspect the real system prompt, conversational rhythm, model transport and publication events. An earlier chain published only some complete tool-bearing model returns, suppressed much natural process text, and could repeat the same result at the end. Repair the actual current seams rather than assuming a single streaming flag fixes them.

The person must be able to interrupt, correct and stop. Preserve valid published content and the same input/draft. Public progress comes from the main assistant's intended speech and real progress, not hidden reasoning, raw child reports, parameters or verification logs. A failure can still be spoken with care: name the actual limitation and usable path, and complete an available authorized recovery.

## Initiative includes moments when I send nothing

Warmly waiting for me to speak is not the whole goal.

After I entrust attention to grades, a course change or another matter, she can inspect within that agreement, consider its implication, prepare useful help and come back with a result at an appropriate time. Leaving the chat page should not make her forget. Returning should not require another complete explanation. A discovery, an unfinished topic, an interest or a greeting can also matter; pleasure and anticipation deserve attention alongside deadlines.

The Grok Bot and Muse research in the reference guide offers concrete ideas: one natural entrustment, continuity, background work and an actual message. Product descriptions are inspiration, not evidence that Anima has another company's cloud host or permissions.

Build on the real existing attention, scheduling, bounded investigation, prepared material, reminder and receipt owners. Follow the whole experience: one natural request becomes retained work; a real event or time wakes it; permitted sources are read; useful material is prepared; a message reaches the original conversation; the person can open and continue. Inspect whether defaults, quiet policy, source selection, notification settings and budgets together keep everything at preparation forever.

Task initiative and relational initiative can have different rhythms. If the person chose more contact and a close relationship, leave room for it. A request for quiet today changes today's company without canceling an independently agreed fixed reminder. Judge relevance and timing from the agreement, matter and actual content; a greeting need not prove productivity value.

An earlier audit identified two specific gaps: background preparation was mainly stored or included in a later context instead of becoming a character message in the original chat, and system notifications reverted to a fixed anonymous title/body. Those observations are historical investigation leads, not current implementation conclusions. Use the actual source, presentation and delivery mechanisms to bring her voice where it is seen, with explicit preview privacy.

Complete already authorized work continuously rather than asking for the same permission repeatedly. If a new account or an external send truly needs a decision, first prepare the concrete useful result, then identify that decision. Begin from existing permitted campus/local arrangements for a substantial natural entrustment-to-result sequence. An unavailable email connection remains its own gap; success in another route does not establish email. A closed desktop process needs an actual independent execution/delivery host before anyone promises continued service. Words must not perform that missing engineering in advance.

## Visit the words throughout the home

Look at actual entry, chat, character choice, settings, today, notes, reading, grades, maps, weather, planning, study, shared work, letters, curiosity and rehearsal, including waiting, empty, failure, retry, save and undo. Find the real origin of wording that is stiff, vague, circuitous or unhelpful.

An operation name should be clear and findable. A character's speech should have her own voice. Preserve already natural phrasing; move redundant explanation out of the way while keeping results and actual recovery entrances reachable. Clarity and closeness belong where each helps.

Warm neutrals, restrained gold, quiet space and natural light are valuable. Preserve the current visual identity while examining typography, controls, character/body placement, waiting jumps and occluded text. The main concern here is conversation and relationship. New art is appropriate when the chosen work genuinely needs it and follows the existing design method.

## Let later contributors understand the reason

This is more than changing a few chat strings. A future reader of the introduction, contract and collaboration rules should understand Anima as a personal companion with character, user-chosen relationship and real initiative. Reliability and particular care are both part of the product.

Keep product identity in the introduction/contract, implementation choices in collaboration guidance and architecture, real paths in navigation, and actual observations/next gaps in the status/continuation entry. Retain character sources, selected narrative period and original Anima interpretation. The [documentation commission](05-INTEGRATION-AND-CONTINUATION.md) explains these responsibilities. Resolve old wording that accidentally flattens affection, process conversation or initiative without discarding the legitimate boundary it protected.

## Spend a little real time together after future changes

The [twenty-one scenarios](04-CONVERSATION-SCENARIOS.md) provide ordinary situations: casual conversation, chosen names, the same task with two characters, consecutive tools, interruption, failure recovery, a background message and return after reopening. Use only the authorized normal model route and existing checks. Character-editor examples do not establish actual generation. An earlier evaluation route being available does not authorize repeating it now.

Preserve calendar, note, source, cancellation and recovery semantics while improving the experience. Do not force a cold sentence merely because an existing check once matched it; preserve the actual meaning and outcome. Label Luna observations as Luna. An unexercised production provider remains unexercised.

For an approved future handback, show a few actual before/after exchanges, changed pages/code, how far initiative really got and any host/connection boundary. Include unfamiliar equivalent wording so memorized screenshot answers cannot pass as understanding.

The wish is simple: when I call, she turns her attention toward me; when I entrust something, she takes it seriously and continues; when I say nothing, she can still have something she cares about. Bring a recognizable person and real help into the same place.
