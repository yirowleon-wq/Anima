## A simple reading route

Read [the full letter](00-LETTER.md), then the other six bodies as context. Use the actual Anima workspace; a design paper is not a substitute for the implementation. Keep a coherent maintained version of each body rather than drifting duplicates.

A natural introduction to the future work can say: I want a recognizable companion who cares, continues entrusted work and speaks as herself throughout. Read the letter, then follow the actual runtime map and character/initiative references. Bring prose and product judgment into the chosen work block, while keeping existing sources, user control and capabilities intact.

Reading this material does not start its development requests. No new image/video generation, account connection, provider call, notification schedule or publishing follows automatically.

## Carry the useful intent into the project

The central goal is a characterful personal companion with a chosen relationship and actual initiative. Character participates in casual exchange, before/between tools, multiround continuity, recovery, background work and adjoining pages. Initiative happens without needing another user message and delivers real usable help.

Let those goals enter the right long-term documents, with current implementation status updated from actual work. Preserve historical constraints as history and current authorization as current scope. A closer relationship and more continuous help must not collapse back into an empty default or a universal caution script.

The actual repository remains the implementation entrance. Use the screenshots' questions and principles without treating their private content as product data. Navigation should lead to the present files, while [the runtime map](02-RUNTIME-AND-CHANGE-MAP.md) and [reference guide](03-CHARACTER-AND-INITIATIVE-REFERENCES.md) locate technical and public-reading entrances.

Keep the design intent, reasons, scenarios, methods and source-reading limits available together. Their presence is not a claim that the intended experience has been implemented or verified.
