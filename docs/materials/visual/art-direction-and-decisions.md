# Art direction, decisions and production experience

Anima should feel worth opening before there is a task to report. A cared-for room, a character with her own attention, readable work and the ability to change one's mind carry the warmth. Serious derivation, enthusiasm, making something silly, rest and an afternoon without an outcome all belong here. A generic consolation sentence or a large portrait covering the work cannot supply that relationship.

The material here preserves that intention while the visual and interaction system continues to develop. Use the [roadmap](../../DEVELOPMENT_ROADMAP.md) to choose substantial work and track what remains.

## The room that became a common direction

A recorded human choice selected the first text-only Sakiko literary reading room as a common spatial and emotional direction. It was not a selection of a later alternate with the earlier camera proportions. The selected picture has tall timber windows, a modest left writing desk and chair, shelving, a right window bench, woven rug, ceramic lamp and cup, and an imagined lake/town/hill distance. Sakiko is reading her own open book. The cup has a place, the book is held plausibly, and the cushion supports her weight. Her attention can belong to the passage without requiring the person to answer.

The human request extended that direction to the affected layouts and matrices. It did not establish calibrated seat polygons, valid alpha/contact layers, every character, responsive behavior or a runtime receipt. The maker's earlier false approval flag and the later explicit static-mother review were different dated records. The later explicit review established that particular full mother as a static source; it did not approve every derivative. This distinction matters when a family of images shares a room name.

The full mother request and empty-room request are preserved in [Room and identity](prompt-collections/room-and-identity.md). The empty plate removes only the person, her held book and her cast shadows. Room-owned lamp, cup, blanket, pillows, desk and floor remain. Hidden cushion and floor surfaces are plausible generated reconstruction, not recovered original pixels or a photograph.

Future page work should protect the room's inhabited scale while giving genuine reading a stable surface. The left half is not permanently a lifeless wall, and an opaque stack of cards is not the only way to make text readable. A desk/easel, open notebook, bench or close reading view can serve different tasks when its camera, furniture, body support and content placement actually agree. The [kept room studies](../../../visual-assets/library/room-studies) preserve such alternatives at their original framing; inspect them before choosing a future solution.

## Identity is distinct from a copied pose

The ten original character identities come from MyGO!!!!! and Ave Mujica. Their passports distinguish checked appearance/canon from Anima's original interactions. Character interpretation is not a fixed business-role assignment: a quiet person can disagree, an energetic person can listen, and a composed person can enjoy a joke.

Earlier identity-expansion requests used one scene as the spatial target and another image only for identity/clothing. That was an effort to preserve room coordinates and prevent a new character from importing a different pose. The useful method remains input-role separation. It does not make the old scene a universal camera, authorize an official reference for redistribution, or guarantee that every copied limb position was sound. Some historical requests use outdated appearance details; the current passport takes precedence, including eye color, side-specific hair clips, clothing and original proportions.

The independent-avatar direction came from a different need. A face in a 34px or 48px circle must convey a specific gaze and small smile. A room's distant quiet expression is not simply a close portrait waiting to be cropped. The later portrait requests redraw a fresh coherent young face: soft cheeks, a naturally short chin, readable eyes and a smile with an individual cause, without a long mature jaw or infant/chibi exaggeration. They do not squash an entire head, enlarge glassy eyes, paste a prior face or spend most of the square on a torso.

The recorded purpose review distinguished the exact human-selected Sakiko source portrait, a later refinement candidate, and the primary review of the other nine. The selected original is a 1254×1254 provided image whose original generation tool and prompt are unknown. Its unchanged bytes and selection cannot be attributed to the later v3 edit or to another older portrait's invocation. The v3 request addresses fringe, eye, chin and cheek feedback; its record did not mark final human or runtime approval. The nine other portraits were admitted for circular-avatar appearance and framing; that did not certify every freeform alpha use, a full-room mother or current runtime. Some warmer edge light was acceptable within a circular portrait while a free cutout still required separate edge inspection. Full recorded requests and their individual moments are in [Portraits, cats and formation](prompt-collections/portraits-cats-and-formation.md).

## Two cat histories, one original project form

An earlier comparison taught a useful general lesson: a character should remain recognizable through understanding, investigation and the return to the person. That observation did not transfer the comparison product's identity or artwork into Anima.

The later project cat was a newly drawn horizontal meme form. A supplied style example contributed only the degree of simplification—white face, simple round eyes, broad hair shapes and flat lines. Its character identity, clothes and body were not to be copied. Sakiko's simplified head and a complete low cat body, paws, hind legs and tail were drawn together. This is not a detailed human portrait glued onto a furry animal, a standing humanoid mascot or a substitute for the human character matrix.

The recorded human response accepted the static shape and asked for either-side placement, position-dependent facing, movement along an edge and speech. The source record explicitly kept transparency, actual small-size appearance, contact, dragging and speech interaction outside that static adoption. Later body/tail layers, separate right-facing versions and runtime controls are separate pieces of work. In particular, Rāna's eye colors and Mutsumi's own-side hair clips cannot be exchanged by blindly flipping a whole image. Keep the [original companion forms](../../../visual-assets/library/companion-forms) and their exact identities available for later use.

The product interaction is also separate from the drawing. A light call should go through the same primary assistant, preserve the person's unsent draft and attachments, and allow an exchange with no task. An animation is not proof of a model reply, memory, permission or business completion. The formation brief and ninety revisable activity directions preserve the richer intention for stages 01/06/10.

## Failure experience that should change future production

| Observed request or recorded limitation | What it established | What future work should do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An earlier request explicitly preserved a generic adult portrayal | The mature direction was in the requested identity, rather than evidence that a youthful request necessarily failed | Use the checked youthful original cast. Do not revive retired adult references by renaming them. Preserve custom user text/data compatibility separately. |
| Upper body and lap, no chair, followed by independent placement in a room | The source did not establish a complete seat, legs, floor and common camera | Generate and inspect the supported full scene first. A portrait is not a universal furniture pose. |
| Complete feet visible | Visibility alone did not establish hip–knee–ankle continuity or load-bearing support | Inspect the full body at normal size and in detail, with the actual support, weight and overlap of that pose. |
| Repeatedly locking leg angles and shoe positions while changing action/identity | This preserves registration but can propagate an unsound original | A structurally faulty character image is not an input for another correction. Start from a clean empty room and a precise new body description. |
| Both shoes required on the floor in a knee-crossed pose | A general anti-floating instruction could conflict with the chosen anatomy | Support is pose-specific. A crossed shoe may be above the floor, a seated foot may hang naturally, and a standing pose may use the floor. No fixed foot count or limb-length ratio replaces inspection. |
| Only changing hands, head or held object | Several activity slots can remain the same physical performance under new labels | Later build distinct compatible mothers for side reading, writing, listening, resting and other needed locations. Do not call ordinary pixel translation a physical transfer. |
| A 1535px mask for a 1536px source | A near-match broke full-canvas registration | Preserve every original row and column. Do not auto-trim a transparent margin, recenter, resize or fit a mask independently. |
| RGBA body alpha peaking at 253/254 and low-alpha exterior mist | RGBA and equal dimensions were insufficient contour/composite evidence | Inspect actual deep/light composites, hair, shoes, floor residue and held-object boundaries. A threshold is not an anatomy repair or proof of correct contact. |
| A source mask redrew or shifted a person | Generated RGB could introduce a new face/body even when the intent was extraction | Retain approved mother RGB and use a checked same-canvas alpha channel as a mask. Keep color art, mask and contact/shadow roles distinct. |
| A generated integral layout contained an unrelated derivative graph | Attractive mathematics did not establish the same original problem | Keep the original integral distinct from an illustrative example, or genuinely choose a matching quadratic source task. Do not change source identity silently. |
| A generated tangent curved, key count/grouping was wrong, or an action link was unsupported | A design image was not scientific or mechanical authority | Use actual calculations, straight tangents, correct keyboard grouping and a checked mechanism. Generated pixels cannot certify causal simulation. |
| A prior statement reported a test/build/static review | It was evidence for its recorded version and scope | Use current status and actual output; later stages require their own real observations. Do not inherit an old completion sentence. |

These lessons are production discipline derived from the records. They do not prove that particular prompt words inevitably cause a particular failure, and they do not establish the image tool's internal model architecture.

## Complete requests retain decisions, not just a style adjective

The shared-life studies describe why one continuous chronological spine was chosen over a narrow-screen split of academic/personal lanes, why academic classes are read-only without completion circles, and why unknown end time must remain unknown. The planning studies compare date strips, folded days and paper spreads under the same synthetic workload. Later revisions make the selected time windows explicit, keep work/preparation/cleanup/rest intact and leave the unchosen rest of life outside the plan. The comparison uses one time axis and an unchanged dinner anchor; an open interval can be intentionally kept for the person rather than shown as unfinished work.

The continuation studies distinguish a part already saved in the calendar from a new proposal. Saved is not completed. A selection references the same original block; it does not adopt a whole plan. Notes preserve the person's writing through history, trash recovery and a conflict, while a source excerpt remains distinct from an independent annotation. Source permission, retention, audience, file selection and actual action are different controls.

The letter study uses one readable paper, a modest date range and source footnotes, rather than a weekly productivity report. The curiosity study arrives with a specific surprising thing already worth replying to; it does not ask the person to choose an interest category first. The rehearsal study separates the person's own words from one explicitly possible counterpart response, allows an alternative at any point, and does not force quick agreement or send anything externally. Its production decision removed an unnecessary disclaimer line from the design while preserving the actual private-draft boundary.

The study papers protect one actual problem, a reported attempt and one requested hint. The later quadratic correction does not pass computed slope off as the learner's work. The wave example specifies same-direction phase-shifted functions whose sum is zero at π; that is not a universal claim about every pair of counterpropagating waves. The piano requests compare one continuous instrument paper with two linked papers separated by ordinary prose. Mechanical connections, sound, key state and formula must later share actual parameters; neither study image is a playable piano.

All of these complete briefs remain in the five collections. Their useful differences, sample text and concrete interactions are preserved alongside the decisions behind them.
