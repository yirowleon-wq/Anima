# One horizontal cat form and its docking

Use the retained complete **horizontal cat sprite** as the canonical cat form. A vertical edge uses that same complete sprite rotated by **90°**, with the direction chosen for the actual side. The face, ears, body, paws and tail transform together. Keep the sprite's proportions and character identity intact; a rotated dock is a different placement of the same resource, not a new character painting.

Read the [current original library](../../../../visual-assets/library/index.json), the relevant [character passport](../../../characters/README.md), the [presence system](../../../design/PRESENCE_SYSTEM.md), and the [motion pipeline](../../../design/CHARACTER_MOTION_PIPELINE.md), in that order. Use the actual horizontal resource and its recorded dimensions/contact geometry. The resource's preservation, generation-input, layer, runtime and rights conditions remain distinct. This direction does not call for new image generation.

## Keep the small form alive

Preserve the simple continuous cartoon treatment shared by head and whole body: broad readable character hair shapes, clear characteristic eyes, a small expressive mouth, dark drawn contours and a complete white cat body. Each character keeps her own identity and manner of attending. Small size is a compact expression of the same person, not a second persona or a random-emotion toy. Original identity, project-original interaction and the person's own facts remain separate.

Horizontal support uses the actual underside/paws and the visible paper, frame or furniture edge. After a quarter-turn, recompute the same resource's bounds, transformed contact point, pointer hit region and safe area against the chosen vertical edge. Rotation must not crop ears, hair, paws or tail, stretch the body, detach a head or make the sprite float beside its support. The complete resource rotates as one coherent object; a special upright face is not substituted into it. Preserve Rāna's iris sides and Mutsumi's paired-clip side when choosing an existing horizontal direction; mirroring is a separate identity-sensitive operation. Distinct scene geometry keeps its own registration.

Make the small form yield when it would cover a source line, formula, current input or the person's hand movement. A drag immediately takes over the old animation; release selects a legal visible dock, cancellation returns to the same prior placement, and changing pages carries the same shared object only where that relationship remains meaningful. Keep a keyboard alternative and reduced-motion behavior. Inspect the actual transition and current hit region, not only the final frame.

## A call is a real interaction

Clicking or lightly calling the cat enters the **same Harness conversation**. It acknowledges the call with the current character's actual attention, can listen without demanding a task and can naturally return to her own activity. Keep the person's existing composer, unsent draft, attachments, selection and focus. A call must not consume, submit or erase that draft. A second call, typing, direct manipulation or cancellation can interrupt the current response gracefully without inventing progress or a completed action.

The cat's attention should have a specific object or cause: a current page, a little joke that received an actual response, a bookmark, a quiet return, or a taskless call. Remembering a joke follows the actual shared history and refusal, rather than a click-count random event. Optional material-related sound has a complete silent equivalent; a gesture finishing does not save, submit or prove that the person has read anything.
