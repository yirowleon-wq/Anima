## Preserve meaning without inventing authority

The bodies are **design briefs**: most adapt recorded production material, while others are independently authored functional specifications or a maintained full-body reference. A brief is not necessarily the exact text submitted to a tool. Its evidence role distinguishes a recorded submitted field, a linked request file, an ordinary request whose submission is not explicit, and a returned revised text. A record's claim does not become an independently recovered tool transport log. The index's `bodySha256` identifies the displayed brief text, including its trailing newline; it is not an original-submission hash. Input roles and historical statements such as approved remain historical. Exact target files are linked separately; a text body attaches no image and grants no new generation-input, layer, motion, runtime or redistribution permission. An unavailable original prompt stays unavailable; an image is not used to invent its text.

Read historical wardrobe, anatomy and light instructions with the current passport and motion pipeline. Earlier Soyo briefs place a crest at the throat; later corrections put it on her left chest with a plain fabric bow. Older and later Mutsumi clip counts and Tomori shoe families differ. Rāna's school and project-original casual outfits are separate families. Mixed Anon/Soyo boilerplate and daylight phrases in later-night variants are historical conflicts, not new character or light authority. Resolve the actual current character, exact admissible original and target light before later use.


Complete independent specifications preserve the valid current design function using clean inputs and the current passport; their previous output recipes are not supplied as references. The per-request records distinguish these newly authored specifications from archived calls. The existing A01 portrait body remains canonical, linked through its preserved RR entry and its actual retained edited-portrait association. Unknown original provenance is not filled from a diagnostic or a later input.

For cats, follow the current [horizontal form and edge-docking direction](retained-requests/horizontal-cat-and-edge-docking.md): the complete retained horizontal sprite is canonical, and a vertical dock rotates that same sprite by 90°. Independent upright-cat output recipes are outside this working library. Preserve character attention, support and real interaction through the one horizontal form.

The multi-document reading bodies apply that current cat direction directly while retaining their complete pickup, frame, focus, tray and return relationships. Their earlier instruction to draw an independently upright cat is replaced by same-resource quarter-turn docking; it is not an alternate future production route.

The geometry belongs to the described canvas and experiment. A crossed pose, folded legs, socked feet or removed shoes must retain its real support and ownership; generic full-foot language cannot turn it into two parallel floor contacts. Fixed coordinates do not justify stretching a person or moving furniture. Matte and contact studies are technical methods, not proof that a layer is healthy or an animation is complete. Use a currently sound complete original before separation. Independent current goals and lessons also remain in the [experience requirements](../../EXPERIENCE_REQUIREMENTS.md), [presence system](../../design/PRESENCE_SYSTEM.md), [motion pipeline](../../design/CHARACTER_MOTION_PIPELINE.md) and [design-intention collection](../../../visual-assets/library/design-intents/README.md); omitted images are not revived as references.

## Specimens and real content

All sample dialogue, dates, course blocks, weather and personal names are illustrative. Alex and Al are two deliberately distinct fictional form-of-address examples. The localization-study filename and page title are a fictional PDF specimen. Other study labels consistently use Algebra and ODEs while keeping each record's actual example page numbers. A selected phrase and its returned quote retain the same exact wording. Real implementation must render the actual original source, mathematical content, chosen time zone, current revision, permission and receipt as live content. It must not treat a composed picture, public-source description or draft preview as a completed read, save or business action.

At the end of an authorized substantial block, preserve the same object, revision, draft, chosen values and next entry in [continuation](../../continuation.md). A quota limit or missing external condition parks that actual dependent step; no generated plan starts a job, restores expired approval or commits an action.
