# Anima working materials

These working materials contain product intentions, discussions, detailed designs, research boundaries, concrete scenarios, production prompts and lessons from unsuccessful attempts. They inform Anima's continuing development. A design document is not an implementation; the [current status](../CURRENT_STATUS.md) and [roadmap](../DEVELOPMENT_ROADMAP.md) distinguish what exists from what remains to be made.

Start with the product's purpose in [the long letter](OPUS_LETTER.md), then the [experience requirements](../EXPERIENCE_REQUIREMENTS.md) and the stage's ordered reading. Read the complete relevant body rather than treating its title, index or condensed roadmap as the original design.

The [scenario library](../scenarios/README.md) preserves 128 complete synthetic situations, twelve timelines with 59 steps, 40 directions, 200 hypothetical questions and 36 experience stories. They are design material, distinct from real biography, production intent routing and passed user workflows. The two independent sets of sixteen extension directions remain separately identified in the roadmap and their complete design bodies.

Three welcome/cover wording candidates are retained exactly: **Here, still.**, **Ever Near.**, and **In Your Corner.** They are alternative lines, not three project names. The foundation uses the first for a fresh cover and keeps the returning-user greeting separate. A later composition may choose the appropriate candidate; there is no requirement to show all three together.

Every art group should connect its original files and English prompt/intent to the relevant future block. A kept unused original remains part of the foundation. A whole-page picture is a design reference, not a working interface, and a static approval is not runtime or motion acceptance. Where an exact generation prompt is unavailable, retain that boundary instead of inventing one from the image.

Use the [five project skills](../../.agents/skills) as the maintained practical methods and the [ten full character passports](../characters/README.md) for identity. Official character facts, dated imagery, design interpretation and Anima's original interactions remain distinct. The material library preserves useful detail while updating old environment and tool assumptions to the actual configurable foundation.
