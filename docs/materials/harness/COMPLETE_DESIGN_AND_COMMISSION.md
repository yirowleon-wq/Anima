## Part I — Overview and reading discipline

### Purpose

Help a person using evidence-backed understanding under the correct subject, time, purpose, world, and permission. When an interpretation is wrong, life changes, or access is revoked, its old influence must stop promptly. Saving information is only one part of that responsibility.

The historical reading sequence was foundation/research → architecture → implementation/acceptance → future contracts → final implementation commission, with contract examples and synthetic specifications alongside. The design was meant to be read outside the product runtime and then checked against an actual repository. Attachment commands remained evidence, not current authorization. Current user instructions and protected project principles prevail; a conflict requires a precise account, not expanding account/private-file/external-write permission.

The product direction then reported Electron, React, TypeScript, SQLite, an explicitly configured cloud provider, an existing map, domain reads, conversation, and action cards. Keep useful behavior and logical owners rather than create eight services for eight duties. Rework governance/meaning without remaking a working map merely for architectural symmetry. Unknown independent modules need their stated interface/path, not a disk scan or a substitute invented under a similar name. Mobile, health, mail, booking, and other unconnected capabilities need real contracts, denial/failure, and activation conditions rather than sample success.

“Designed,” “controlled offline checks,” “actual model behavior,” and “real source/permission/time coverage” are different evidence classes. Implemented code, migration and ordinary-entry observations need their own evidence. The historical document validation checked structure, references, JSON, and type examples only.

### Discussion foundation

The first discussion separated original evidence, revisable assertions, continuing matters, live business state, summary views, and indexes. Original words are not the model's interpretation; interpretation is not an official fact; proposal is not commitment; commitment is not completion. Usable memory needs subject, provider, original location, valid time, conditions, inference status, and current permission. Atomic assertions help checking while complete event/context preserves reasons and competing needs.

Writing followed candidates→evidence verification→conditional updates→transactional commit. Reading followed task needs→multiple retrieval paths→conditions/exceptions→live refresh→missingness→original fallback. Summaries are rebuildable views, not recursively compressed replacements for their sources.

The second discussion broadened memory into meaning and permission: one statement can combine hypothesis, a proxy question, correction, request, and restriction. It added speech-act interpretation, separate worlds, proposal/selection/commitment/execution, subject/audience, current interaction wishes, performance evidence, conditional refusal reasons, contiguous processing progress, independent read/use/retain/disclose/delete controls, and both helpful-personalization and noninterference comparisons. Later design rejects giving a pre-classifier irrevocable authority over these meanings; the distinctions themselves remain protected.

Personalization means appropriate use of revisable understanding, including knowing when not to use it, rather than an ever-growing profile. The two hundred student questions were hypothetical breadth, not this person's facts or two hundred immediately enabled services. Financial, health, roommate, partner, academic, and housing examples remain synthetic. An early database-brand suggestion was a strategy, not a product principle. “Understands me” does not justify psychological scores, total collection, value decisions for the person, or inferred action permission.

### Six material additions

1. **Permission precedes memory.** Enforce it before retention, retrieval, model transfer, and effects. Attachment-only use cannot first read a private profile and merely hide it later.
2. **Uncertainty replaces personality ranking.** Separate explicit preferences/tradeoffs, capabilities/resources, current state, and competing hypotheses with low-burden correction.
3. **Dependencies invalidate as well as discover.** Trace which class/route/grant/preference/hypothesis supports a plan, selectively recheck after change, and retire affected reminders.
4. **Execution needs unknown.** A timeout may follow a real effect; a card is not a reservation, and local finish is not institutional recognition.
5. **Read and disclose differ before model input.** Give an audience writer allowed busy/free rather than all private titles with an instruction not to leak.
6. **Future interfaces have real semantics.** Define registration, schema, denial/failure, and activation rather than a TODO. Historical simulator/test plans remain static requirements until separately authorized.

## Part II — Complete architecture

This is the historical proposed core, not a report of all current implementations. Its MUST/EXT categories originally distinguished a working foundation from contracts awaiting external conditions. Current work follows actual authorization, and later semantic architecture replaces compulsory pre-routing while retaining these source/effect responsibilities.

### A. System purpose, deployment, and invariants

Maintain a shared account of whom the system serves, reality versus possibilities, desired help, usable evidence, current work, unsettled decisions, and actual effects. Understanding, help, and action use one state/permission foundation rather than three user copies:

```text
Understanding: evidence → subject/time/conditions → candidate → verify/commit → revisable understanding.
Help: current request → use boundaries → recover matter → evidence → answer/options → actual choice.
Action: chosen direction → specific authority → dispatch/inspect → result → dependencies/experience.
```

Policy, lineage, version, dependency, and evaluation cross all three. Ordinary requests may use only a short part. Venting need not read courses; a room query need not analyze life values; panning a map needs no model.

Use a local-first modular monolith. Renderer handles interaction; main owns trusted identity, finite IPC, credentials, domain services, the writer, and effects. Workers calculate candidates without a second unrestricted DB-write entry. Controlled original blobs supplement SQLite. Platform/store/model/provider seams prepare future hosts without claiming cloud accounts, synchronization, or safe multi-tenancy. Do not add eight microservices, a broker, or graph database merely for completeness.

The original component sketch was ingress/policy→evidence/retention/outbox + interpretation/context + coordinator, with context/domain reads, recipes/broker/providers, planning validation, actions/reminders, receipts, and async extraction/verification/commit/index refresh. Later designs move open semantic choice to the actual main agent rather than make interpretation/TaskNeeds a mandatory pre-gate. The coordinator still owns lifecycle/budget/cancel without absorbing every domain's rules.

### B. Data, time, conditions, and understanding

#### B1. Distinct owners rather than a universal User row

Logical entities may share simple storage where appropriate without merging their meanings.

| Entity | Required meaning and owner boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Principal / Device | User/device/workspace ownership from Policy; not a model-editable profile. |
| ConsentGrant / PolicyRevision | Data purpose, scope, expiry, processor/audience, and revocation; never generated from a preference. |
| SourceEvent / EvidenceSpan | Speaker/source/time/revision/original location/retention; model prose is not user original. |
| Assertion / AssertionVersion | Typed value, conditions, valid and recorded times, state; Understanding does not copy an official ledger. |
| DecisionExperience | Conditions and actual options, choice, explicit reasons, reported/observed outcome; observation and interpretation differ. |
| Hypothesis | Competing explanations, support/challenge, confirmation need, and use limits; not a hard constraint by default. |
| LearningObservation | Task/skill, independent/hinted/AI-assisted performance, excerpt, comparability; grades do not imply mastery. |
| Episode / Checkpoint | Goal/stage/options/refusal/unknown/next step/artifact/coverage; a summary has no independent fact authority. |
| World / ScenarioDelta | A versioned real baseline plus hypothetical changes, purpose, and expiry; cannot contaminate real-world search. |
| DomainObservation | Original courses/exams/recognized activity/weather/routes remain with the domain/source rather than another personal-fact copy. |
| Goal / Commitment / Plan | Goal state, participant agreement, conditions/revisions; none alone establishes execution. |
| Action / Attempt / Receipt | Concrete effect, authority, dispatch/unknown/inspection/result; HTTP timeout is not confirmed failure. |
| Watch / Delivery | Triggers, target dependencies, channel, quiet/cooldown, and actual delivery evidence; no permission expansion. |
| DerivedArtifact / Dependency | Summary/index/context/plan inputs and revisions, owned by their producing domain plus the relationship index. |

An envelope provides identity/provenance, not an all-purpose JSON body for weather, character, and academic facts.

#### B2. Envelope and exact evidence addressing

At minimum retain `object_id, owner_id, workspace_id, subject_id, world_id, schema_version, policy_label, revision`; factual use also needs `source_id, validity, recorded_time, confirmation, provenance`. Stable entities distinguish namesakes and same-named places at different institutions.

```text
text: event_id + content_version + absolute Unicode code-point start/end
file: artifact_id + version + page/paragraph + optional text range
image: artifact_id + version + normalized [x,y,w,h] region + object_id
audio: artifact_id + version + start_ms/end_ms + transcript_version
structured: source_snapshot_id + JSON Pointer + source-record ID
```

Do not mix DOM/JS UTF-16 with canonical code-point ranges; bridges convert explicitly and verify original readback. Normalize for indexing only, not original replacement. Transcription/visual extraction is a derived revision. Evidence carries `root_event_id/root_source_record_id`: quotations, summaries, and multiple worker accounts retain one independent root.

#### B3. Assertions and two time axes

```text
Assertion {
  id, subject_id, world_id, predicate, typed_value,
  kind: explicit_fact | explicit_preference | current_state | inferred_hypothesis,
  condition_ast, exceptions[], strength,
  valid_from, valid_to, time_precision,
  recorded_from, recorded_to,
  status: candidate | active | disputed | superseded | expired | retracted,
  evidence_links[], policy_label, revision
}
```

`valid_*` describes applicability in life; `recorded_*` describes when the system held an interpretation. Unknown dates stay unknown. “Moving next week” preserves the phrase and candidate interpretation rather than silently selecting Monday midnight. Reality changing from a date ends the old valid period and begins a new one. Correcting an earlier unit mistake—say a synthetic 48-kilometer interpretation that should have been 48 activity records—corrects historical meaning without inventing a life in which the mistaken requirement was once real. Recording time can still show that the system was wrong then. This is a hypothetical illustration, not an American institutional requirement.

Institutional rules carry institution/campus/term/cohort/audience, effective time, and source revision. Resolve authority at the domain's proper scope/time; last fetched is not automatically most authoritative.

#### B4. Conditional preference and competing explanation

An example preference may say: during focused independent study, prefer staying to relocating because of explicitly reported switching cost, soft unless relocation was truly prohibited, with an urgent accepted appointment as an exception. Store comparison, condition, reason, and strength rather than a personality rank.

A reviewed domain registry defines typed formal predicates. ConditionAST supports typed comparisons, intervals, membership, AND/OR/NOT, and explicit unknown. It does not run model-generated JavaScript/SQL/arbitrary expressions. Values preserve units and parsed timezone. In three-valued logic, unknown is not false and NOT unknown is not proof of no restriction. This formal subset does not exhaust all human semantic content; the later final architecture keeps open-text memory with precise metadata.

Hard limits arise from explicit refusal, real infeasibility, or protected rules; soft preferences rank viable alternatives. Personality is never a hard restriction automatically. Equal-scope conflict can stay disputed; a narrower dated exception overlays its period without deleting a lasting preference. Understanding distinguishes stable revisable facts, conditional preferences/tradeoffs, capability/resource evidence, temporary states, and hypotheses. Current listening/hinting/analyzing/direct-result wishes remain separate and override a learned usual method.

#### B5. What experience should teach

DecisionExperience records options actually available then, known limits, recommendation, choice, explicit explanation, observable outcome, and unknown parts. Not clicking is not dislike; no app feedback is not no real action; adopting a suggestion does not prove it caused improvement.

Competing Hypotheses might explain staying in by equipment, fragmented time, weather, or state rather than lazy/introverted labels. Retain `supported_by, challenged_by, applicable_when, next_discriminating_question, expires_at` and do not inject unsupported hypotheses into ordinary answers. Ask a low-cost question only if it changes current help; a pair of representative options may clarify without a profile questionnaire. The objective is less repeated explanation, violated constraint, and unnecessary interruption—not attachment, duration, or obedience.

### C. Ingress, scope, and four control axes

#### C1. Boundaries before expansion or persistence

```text
Receive input in transient memory.
Obtain trusted UI/session identity, source ceiling, and retention settings.
Apply actual restrictions/revocations/current-only controls and necessary conservative barriers.
Interpret allowed current meaning and build the scope/contract.
Persist only what the retention policy permits, otherwise retain in memory.
Only then expand sources, model processing, or effects within the scope.
```

Do not save a whole input in a durable log before discovering nonretention. Unclear lasting permission does not pass by default. Natural-language/model interpretation can propose a target or narrow scope; it cannot create an unconnected account grant or unspecified external-write authority. If “forget that” is ambiguous, freeze affected candidate use and locate a recognizable scope before irreversible deletion rather than guess broadly. An explicit UI object ID and clear authorized deletion can proceed.

Later final design makes the limited ingress model a current-control interpreter, not an obligatory whole-task classifier. It still cannot receive cloud-prohibited text first in order to discover its prohibition. Explicit send-before-processing UI/local controls are necessary where local interpretation is unavailable.

#### C2. Complete proposed ContextContract

```text
ContextContract {
  run_id, principal_handle, workspace_id,
  subject_refs[], world_ref, episode_ref?,
  allowed_source_handles[], excluded_source_handles[],
  purpose, audience, processing_destination,
  memory_mode: relevant | current_sources_only | session_only | none,
  retention_mode, disclosure_projection,
  action_mode: respond | read | propose | execute_approved,
  interaction_mode, protected_disclosure_budget?,
  clock_context, policy_revision, privacy_epoch,
  resource_budget, uncertainty_policy
}
```

Principal/source handles come from the host rather than arbitrary tool user IDs. Tools select permitted domains/objects without enlarging owner/world/source. `not_for_long_term_inference` distinguishes permission to answer now from permission to infer lasting preferences, independently of disclosure and workspace boundaries.

#### C3. Speech meaning as reviewable data, not an authority gate

The earlier proposal represented `assert/correct/hypothesize/ask/propose/accept/reject/revoke/quote` with original span, subject, referent, scope, time, modality, route, and ambiguity. One message may compare housing for a roommate, prohibit calendar edits, and report today's transport separately. A short assent first needs the actual stable option, not permission for everything. Missing reference can support a question or explicit conditional analysis without unambiguous approval.

These distinctions remain valuable; the later architecture explicitly rejects using a pre-model lexical SpeechAct router or a compulsory hard label to remove tools. The model sees the full original and can revise interpretation; the host checks definite scope/effects. A classifier cannot read an entire profile to determine whether the person asked not to read profiles.

#### C4. Independent axes and temporary content

Judge collect/read, use/infer, retain, and disclose/execute separately. Personal-assistant status grants no default health, exact-position, or third-party access. A public source does not make a private inference public; aggregated views retain all applicable constraints.

Session-only content stays in process memory until the declared turn/session end with a truthful configurable lifetime. It does not enter full-text indexes, durable logs, backup, async body queues, or crash dumps. Local zero persistence does not promise a provider's zero processing/retention; state that external boundary separately.

### D. Persistence, candidate production, and change

#### D1. Table families and transactions

```text
identity_policy: principals, devices, policies, grants, privacy_fences
sources: source_events, evidence_spans, evidence_edges, source_processing
understanding: assertions, assertion_versions, assertion_evidence,
               experiences, hypotheses, profile_views
work: worlds, episodes, checkpoints, goals, commitments, plans
facts: domain_records, domain_versions, source_snapshots, sync_cursors
effects: actions, action_attempts, receipts, watches, deliveries
runtime: dependencies, run_records, jobs, outbox, index_watermarks
retrieval: evidence_fts, assertion_fts, entity_aliases, text_grams, vector_refs?
```

Use owner/workspace/world as common index prefixes plus time/state/predicate/source/derivation/job-time indexes. Full prompts are not required audit storage; default to permitted IDs/revisions/check outcomes, and do not call a sensitive-content hash anonymous by nature. Check foreign keys, versioned migration, WAL, actual binding, storage medium, and encryption compatibility rather than assert features.

One controlled writer uses short transactions without awaiting network/model/subprocess. Workers propose; commits atomically update object, evidence links, processing state, and outbox. An async index has its own continuous coverage; outbox completion is not index completion. SQLite is not advertised as multi-tenant row security or unlimited concurrent writers.

#### D2. Entire-input processing and idempotence

Split long originals by meaningful units with bounded overlap. Preserve span states `pending, candidate_emitted, ephemeral_only, quote_or_hypothesis, no_personal_fact, needs_context, failed`. One passage may support several results. No memory candidate does not prove successful processing.

Use source-content revision + span ID + extractor version + operation scope as an idempotence identity. Retrying the same source adds no independent support. A changed extractor may yield another candidate revision, still sharing the original evidence root.

#### D3. Extract, verify, commit

An extractor reads permitted spans, needed neighbors, and relevant prior candidates, then proposes without DB/grant/effect tools. Deterministic checks validate exact original readback, subject/world/purpose, types, parsed dates/units, scope, root duplication, ConditionAST, processing coverage, and expected revision. Semantic review rereads originals to distinguish wish/reality, once/lasting, negation/exception, assistant/user, quote/self. Low-risk explicit facts need not require universal double-model checking; complex/high-impact cases can receive another independent source-based review. Unsupported interpretations stay unknown or candidates. An uncalibrated 0.97 cannot promote sensitive personality/health claims.

Retain all nine operations: ADD, SUPPORT, REFINE, ADD_EXCEPTION, SUPERSEDE, CORRECT, DISPUTE, EXPIRE, RETRACT. CORRECT fixes a misunderstanding; SUPERSEDE records changed reality. SUPPORT adds an independent source, not an old model summary.

#### D4. Concurrent progress and reading fresh writes

Maintain received_seq, extracted_contiguous_seq, indexed_contiguous_seq, and explicit gaps. Max completed sequence does not establish all preceding work. Current context may combine an effective long-term view, relevant unfinished originals, a correction/revocation overlay, and dynamic facts. If backlog exceeds budget, inspect decision-relevant spans first and explain remaining gaps rather than claim completeness.

Commit only under matching expected/current revision; reread and semantically reconcile conflict. Physical retries can be multiple while logical effect remains idempotent. Increasing privacy epochs/fences are checked at read, transfer, and commit; a late worker cannot reinsert withdrawn content.

#### D5. Retirement, correction, and forgetting

`deactivate` preserves historical evidence without current guidance. `correct` repairs interpretation/validity and affected derivatives. `forget/delete` blocks use/re-extraction and cleans authorized originals/derivatives. First record the barrier and source suppression, then invalidate assertions/views/indexes/tasks/caches and clean source ranges, full text, vectors, provider state, experience summaries, pending work, and generated method candidates.

A mixed message needs exact-location redaction with truthful treatment of earlier plaintext—not merely a new visible version hiding the old one. Independent surviving evidence can rebuild a conclusion; do not delete unrelated facts mechanically, and conservatively mark a dependent view unavailable during repair. Use stable source/object/revision barriers rather than retaining the forgotten sensitive sentence as a forbidden-keyword list. Broad semantic forgetting needs a real scope and coverage report; no promise that keywords recognize every paraphrase.

An independently new statement can be processed under its new permission; replay of the old source is not new evidence. A lasting category prohibition retains only necessary policy, not the forgotten body. Restores apply current deletion/epoch barriers before old content; unprocessed backups remain isolated. Prior external disclosure cannot be undone by local deletion. Immediate nonuse, active-data cleaning, and backup cleaning have different timing, and memory dumps/disk remnants/other software copies remain within the actual threat model rather than an absolute erasure claim.

### E. Context compilation around what is needed

#### E1. Conditions as a graph, not an automatic task classifier

A proposed TaskRecipe describes possible objects, lawful reads, new candidate needs, and checkers. It is not a fixed answer or closed catalogue every open request must enter. The later main-agent architecture may discover these relationships directly while preserving the host's precise boundaries.

```text
Suggest an activity
  subject + current participation wish + real time                  required
  usable chosen window, if scheduling
    fixed commitments + deadlines + accepted obligations            AND
  if moving
    valid allowed observation OR supplied approximate place          OR
    + actual transport + destination eligibility + departure window AND
  if weather-dependent
    place/time forecast OR clearly conditional fallback               OR
  if equipment/place/body-dependent
    actual applicable resource/source constraints
  relevant explicit preference + current exception + experience
```

Need carries typed key, requirement level, applicability, fulfillment route, freshness, eligible sources, privacy cost, and expected impact. AND/OR supports a manual building origin without GPS. Unknown tasks can introduce lawful needs, checked for field/capability/budget; unknown fields never grant themselves a tool.

#### E2. Five complementary retrieval paths

1. Structured subject/world/time/purpose constraints, exceptions, and goal dependencies; necessary constraints do not compete in top-k.
2. Entities/full-text for IDs, aliases, dates, old options/events, with suitable short-alias and language behavior.
3. Bounded relationship expansion for support/challenge/exception/conditions/refusal/source.
4. Experiences and optional semantic candidates for similarly structured choices, without unapproved external embeddings.
5. An independent original channel for unextracted lawful conversation/files, including reading and later reprocessing.

RRF or other candidate ranking combines authorized valid candidates, not permission/truth/hard limits. Numerical weights are engineering parameters, not psychological probabilities. The earlier short-phrase example highlighted FTS trigram limits. Retrieval needs exact entity aliases, whole-word/full-text matching and bounded original fallback appropriate to actual text; do not blindly assume one language-specific tokenizer. Parameterize SQL and escape MATCH input. Text needs explicit canonical Unicode handling.

#### E3. Filter before candidates and throughout expansion

An unforgeable host scope binds principal/workspace/world/source/purpose/retention/audience. Every repository path requires it before candidates; original reads, edges, aliases, and cache hits recheck too. Whole-store vector top-k followed by filtering both touches disallowed data and can crowd out lawful candidates.

Cache identity includes scope fingerprint, purpose, audience projection, world/episode, policy revision, privacy epoch, and index watermark. No shared private cross-user query cache. Explanation logs default to identity/state without copying every result.

#### E4. Coverage and complete-bundle budgets

NeedResult distinguishes fresh, stale, conflict, not_queried, not_found, failed, forbidden, unsupported, known_absent, and unknown. A not_found describes its searched scope rather than universal absence.

Prioritize permission/current limits, necessary conditions with exceptions, actual accepted/rejected state, decisive original evidence, relevant soft preferences, optional experience, then decorative summary. Budget complete evidence bundles. If a bundle does not fit, narrow, qualify, or ask a decisive question; do not keep a preference and omit its restricting exception. Coverage is complete_for_candidate, conditional, or blocked, not a false percentage of human meaning.

#### E5. New candidates introduce new needs

A stay-here study suggestion may need no home equipment data; adding a home project introduces route, switching cost, equipment, and return deadline. `introduced_needs` supports host/domain checking instead of trusting “nothing else needed.” Bound follow-up reads. If a hard condition remains missing, remove/qualify the candidate or ask; do not hide it behind an unlimited loop.

#### E6. ContextPack and ContextReceipt

The proposed pack has six parts: trusted rules; current-use boundary summary; current original/necessary conversation; matter/actual choice; evidence bundles/dynamic observations; missing/conflicting information. External text remains labeled data with stable addresses, not a high-authority instruction.

### F. Matters, hypothetical worlds, planning, and learning

#### F1. Episode is not a chat thread

#### F4. Change only what is affected

### G. Effects, reminders, and invalidation

#### G1. Action and approval are their own objects

Action carries capability, exact arguments, target/audience, risk, expected effect, reversibility, dependencies, deadline, and idempotency identity. Concrete approval binds canonical action digest, argument revision, policy revision, expiry, and one-time approval ID.

```text

  → failed_confirmed
  → outcome_unknown → reconciling → succeeded / failed_confirmed / unresolved

Before dispatch: approved → cancelled
After dispatch: cancel_requested
  → cancellation_confirmed / cancellation_unknown / cannot_cancel
```

A draft is not scheduled. Local agenda success establishes local saving only. A provider accepting a request need not confirm a reservation; its actual receipt schema decides.

#### G2. Recheck and inspect rather than repeat

No local database guarantees generic external exactly-once. Reuse a supported key, inspect where possible, or retain unknown and explain a manual check when neither is available. #### G4. Watch is not perpetual model polling
