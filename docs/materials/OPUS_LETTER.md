# Help Anima feel alive

This product-design letter expresses the experience and development goals for Anima. Its scenarios illustrate possibilities rather than personal history or completed behavior. Read it alongside the current [product contract](../PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md), [architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md), [capability record](../CURRENT_STATUS.md), [working packet](opus/START_HERE.md) and [development roadmap](../DEVELOPMENT_ROADMAP.md).

The twelve formal development stages remain unfinished. The redesign requests below are goals to pursue against the actual current baseline, not claims that the work has already happened.

I would like you to take Anima seriously. Months of code, pictures, discussions and handoffs sit behind this product. What I want from its next development is deep improvement grounded in an understanding of the whole thing. The Harness matters especially: understanding, personality, memory, tools, continuing work and initiative. Where those capabilities reach the interface, please also repair whatever makes spending time together awkward.

Let me explain why this matters so much.

I want opening Anima to feel like returning to a comfortable place. There is light by a window, a character I chose and a familiar voice. The relationship might be a girlfriend, a boyfriend, a friend, an anime character, a cat or a personality I wrote myself. The companion listens, but also has a way of expressing things, interests and small moods of their own. We can fool around, think through something difficult or leave an afternoon unproductive. When life is busy, they can look after something I have actually entrusted to them. I mean affection, ease, being remembered and reliable practical help together.

The painful failure is to meet an administrative system wearing attractive character art. It explains that it is “staying at the discussion level,” repeatedly certifies that it changed nothing and narrates internal boundaries, yet never quite reaches the person talking to it. A tool call can leave a long silence and then produce a report. Awkward permanent text crowds the interface; a character covers the material. Some capabilities sound excellent in documents but do not arise naturally in an actual conversation. A reference companion may be imperfect, yet its voice remains recognizable while it is helping. That continuity is what I miss.

A warmer rewriting layer at the end will not resolve this. Please understand the whole product so the system behind the companion can organize its work in a way that supports human conversation.

## Know the companion before deciding how to change the system

Begin from the actual reviewed local workspace, its current files and any shared uncommitted work. Keep healthy changes. A recorded version is a coordinate for understanding evidence, not an instruction to reset a newer tree. Distinguish current code, the build actually running, a packaged executable and a public preview. None automatically represents the others. Remote history or upload is not required to review this foundation, and neither is an automatic next step.

Connect the README, working instructions, project navigation, contract, architecture, current capability record, continuation and scene/program contracts. Then follow real execution. How does a sentence reach the model? Which prompt sections actually enter the request, and which preliminary steps have already altered what it can see? How are tools found, called and returned? How does an observation become memory, continuing work, a visible surface and the next reply? A filename containing memory, agent, scene or proactive proves little by itself. An old report saying something is absent may be stale too. Form a judgment from present code and behavior.

Read the complete working materials in the project. Repetition can reveal an objection that was never properly heard; contradiction can reveal a later correction. Do not use a compact summary to declare unread material finished. Keep an accurate position and unresolved question when reading across contexts, then continue in the same healthy work session. A document once called final is still open to revision. The material helps you see the reasoning; it does not make the decision for you or grant a new permission.

The lead designer must personally understand the request, important original arguments and product as a whole. Formal implementation of a development stage retains eight substantive workers with exclusive responsibilities. Each worker needs enough project and source understanding to do real creative and engineering work, challenge an explanation and exchange ideas with the others. The lead integrates and accepts the actual result. This organization is separate from the product's own bounded investigative agents, which must remain useful. Ordinary reading and review do not automatically begin a formal stage.

Verification remains necessary without becoming a ritual around one particular model. Use the actual authorized entries and evidence available for a claim; do not silently choose a new supplier or paid route. Existing good character language should survive a Harness improvement. Preserving it does not require changing the product provider to whichever tool helped write it, and it does not mean moving personality into a final stylistic shell.

Question the proposed layers, class names and implementation routes. If the question itself is wrong, ask a better one. Preserve the life experience and factual responsibility that motivated the proposal, then find the simplest sound way to achieve them. An elegant different design is welcome when its costs, migration and results are understood.

These long working papers belong to development. The running companion needs suitable character guidance, usable capabilities, relevant present context and a way to investigate further. Putting the complete library into a production system prompt would not make it more faithful; it could hide the very detail that matters now.

## Who the companion is should change the whole encounter

I want personality to exist when understanding begins. What she notices, which sentence she answers first, when she teases gently, how she stays serious and how she takes on a troublesome part of the work should all let me recognize her. Replacing “okay” with a cuter word or adding an emoji is a tiny fraction of that. The user should not be limited to a few sliders for enthusiasm, sentence length and politeness.

Look carefully at editable character cores, imported text cards, appearance, forms of address, private guidance, automatic understanding and the current request. The ten current built-in characters have passports and distinct identities; user-authored roles remain editable. Art coverage, a written personality and actual model participation are different things. An image does not establish a personality, and an attractive preview does not establish that the saved core entered the main request. Provide usable starts where the roadmap calls for them, with clear preview, adoption, editing, conflict recovery and return to a preferred version. Do not restore retired built-in identities or overwrite existing custom choices.

Sakiko Togawa and Mutsumi Wakaba come from a character world with relationships, circumstances, rhythm and emotional contradictions. “Cool” and “speaks little” are inadequate descriptions. Use accessible official profiles, legitimate original scenes and first-person performance/production interviews to understand why a voice develops as it does. Separate canon, visual observation, interpretation and original Anima interaction. A search excerpt is not a full page read; a video link is not a video watched. A new everyday scene can express a character's temperament without pretending to quote the work or fabricating a shared past with the user.

Protect the expressive writing that already works. Find actual saved prompts, character text, recent changes and meaningful conversations before replacing them with more orderly prose. Notice the sentence rhythm, pauses, ways of addressing someone and capacity for surprise that made it feel natural. Fix the surrounding conflicts precisely. Conversely, style preservation should not protect a hollow or disconnected settings panel. If an edit never enters a request, or a view change loses the draft, repair that actual fault. Do not promote an untested suspicion into a diagnosis.

A form of address belongs to a person and a relationship. Use the name the user explicitly chose, in the moments where it fits. Another user may choose a different one, and today someone may ask for a serious reply without a nickname. Neither the example name nor constant repetition should become a global template. A cat role may meow; Mutsumi need not. Every lively character need not share the same coy manner.

Affectionate expression and factual reports of work have different grounds. In a chosen close relationship, “Welcome back. I missed you a little” can be original character expression. “I checked your messages while you were away” reports an actual action and needs an actual connected source and result. Lack of an always-running host should not erase the first kind of expression, and warmth should never manufacture the second. Humor, closeness, imagination and roleplay deserve normal space while factual and action responsibility stays dependable underneath.

The companion can have judgment. Gentleness is compatible with disagreement, a rigorous objection to a proof and admitting that something was misunderstood. Affection is not automatic praise or agreement. Intimacy should not depend on invented shared events, demands for reassurance, guilt about leaving or exclusion of real relationships. A lively companion should also be easy to be around freely.

Give expression an appropriate range. Warmth can be one short sentence or a proof explained completely. A table, list, formula, story or emoji can be right in context. The failure is speech that does not address the person, or internal rules used as the answer; no particular layout is inherently forbidden.

## Let the companion stay present while doing things

Tool use is one of the two central pieces of this product; memory is the other. I care about the encounter during the work, not only the final answer.

Suppose I ask how to get somewhere this evening and whether it will rain. The companion can acknowledge that request in her own voice, look up a route, mention a useful confirmed discovery and continue with weather for the relevant time. A natural line might be “The route is there; I am checking the rain for when you would be going.” Those words illustrate rhythm, not a production script. What she says depends on her character, the actual evidence and whether I want conversation at that moment.

Findings, turns in the investigation and a meaningful wait can be spoken naturally. A brief acknowledgment, affection or joke does not have to prove that it contains a new data point. At the same time, no courtesy sentence is required after every tool, and a simple question need not perform a long think. The main character should have room to speak and room to remain quiet.

Trace events and transport rather than relying only on prompt edits. The important historical seam was a provider able to receive text increments while the main execution bridge could still hold them until a complete tool round returned. The current implementation has conversation-rhythm and publication machinery; inspect the actual request callbacks, sidecar behavior, published prefixes, replaceable tails and completion checks before diagnosing today's timing. Preliminary control calls and final repair can also affect time to useful contact. They have reasons, but their combined effect needs observation. A complete tool-round message and true incremental text are different capabilities.

Natural words, tool activity, usable surfaces and final results should be able to arrive as parts of one turn. Remember what was already said so the final answer continues it rather than repeating the name, acknowledgment and entire table. Correct an earlier tentative conclusion when new evidence changes it. Cancellation, interrupted transport, regeneration, long-tool failure and a new sentence from the user all need a real response in the work itself. Some independent observations may remain valid while the obsolete goal is retired.

Public conversation is separate from hidden reasoning, raw investigative reports and internal review. The companion can explain what was found and what remains relevant without publishing private thinking or an unfiltered worker transcript. A busy backend can still support an easy conversation, with a complete explanation when the user actually wants one.

Recorded map/weather failures are valuable because they crossed several seams: finding several places, consuming reading budget, a weather setting and final expression. Making the failure report more charming would leave the underlying experience broken. Inspect budget consumption, lawful reuse of results, completion of independent portions and a recovery entry that keeps the same request. The user should not have to remember a magic phrase to ask again later before help becomes possible.

## Give the main assistant room to understand

A major failed approach was to make understanding reliable by assigning every sentence an intent, emotion, demand, profile and mode before the main model had understood it. A different expression then lost access to a tool; a request for a short conclusion was treated as a request for shallow thinking; tiredness plus a desire for a complete proof produced only consolation. Another layer had already decided the available world.

Replacing a regex with a classifier, putting the same routing table into a system prompt or adding a confidence field can repeat that failure. The main assistant needs the actual words, necessary context, current work and discoverable capabilities. It must be able to find more material, ask a better question, reread an original, propose another explanation or decline to use an inaccurate summary.

The host has precise responsibilities: identity, actually connected sources, time, object version, processing/action scope, cancellation, resources and action outcomes. Doing those well gives the model more room for judgment. Reasons, contradictory wishes and tradeoffs deserve rich language; they should not be forced into a schema claiming to exhaust a person's life. Entity search, date parsing, indexes and business validation can locate or verify things without permanently classifying the person.

Make a real subtraction judgment about the loop. Which calls help, and which merely certify that the system worked hard? Which context is needed now, which repeats other guidance and which survives because nobody dared remove it? Which tool meanings are unclear? Did the model fail, or did packaging lose the original request, evidence or capability? A thin, comparable diagnostic reference route can help isolate the effect of an added layer, provided it does not remove real source or permission protection to obtain a better-looking answer. Diagnostic upper bounds are not normal product behavior.

Hermes is a real current engine: the Python sidecar uses the pinned upstream AIAgent with host-owned model, tool and state bridges. Understand and reuse sound work before replacing it. A future replacement is possible only with a reasoned target design, capability/data migration, cancellation and recovery, usable intermediate states and one authoritative route. Swapping frameworks to display a new framework name is not deep improvement.

Let tools and methods be discoverable. Common capabilities can be nearby while the full directory remains available. Missing a candidate in one search must not permanently remove it. Methods explain how to accomplish useful work with existing capabilities; tools return observations; original sources determine factual scope. A new unfamiliar provider should be usable through a clear contract and registry rather than another provider-name branch in the main loop.

The assistant should be able to find and use the actually authorized model capabilities, public research, materials, campus imports, maps and other tools to answer an unfamiliar question. The user should not act as the tool dispatcher. Explore useful future capabilities without treating a listing, an inference result and a continuing tool loop as the same proof. Product login, engineering subscription, provider protocol and external connections keep their actual separate conditions and the user's selected route.

## Initiative means useful work can happen without another message

This is an important expectation. I mean a companion that can continue attending to an entrusted matter, investigate, prepare a result and come back at a suitable moment. Published proactive-agent demonstrations can help us ask about the whole lifecycle, but their marketing does not establish an algorithm or give Anima an always-on cloud host.

An allowed connected inbox, a grade source, a changed classroom, an activity or a question left unfinished are possible sources of real work. Each needs its actual connection and scope. Saying “I will keep an eye on it” is not the capability. If someone has asked for more opportunities to make things this month, the assistant should be able to recognize that an unfamiliar instrument-building open evening is relevant from its content, inspect time and feasibility and bring useful preparation. Matching the words “research event” is not the same understanding.

Time can change relevance without a new email or timetable version: class ends, a window approaches, departure becomes timely or weather expires. Conversely, a new source version need not justify a message. Continuing work, producing useful material and choosing when to speak each deserve a real mechanism. The companion can be active and considerate together.

There is already substantial machinery: entrustments, change journals, time nodes, periodic review, opportunities, backoff, preparations and notification paths. Do not pretend it is absent. Trace what quiet mode actually pauses, what optional opportunities it withdraws and which independent reminders or memory maintenance continue. Reconsider that arrangement from the user's meaning. When someone says not to interrupt, which already-promised work remains useful to prepare quietly? What starts concern? Can an unregistered opportunity be found? Can an investigation continue far enough? Who actually consumes the preparation? Does silence accidentally strand an unfinished entrustment?

Not everything should require the user to create a formal Goal or Watch first. An allowed relationship, welcomed initiative and relevant current material can support discovery of something worthwhile, modest useful preparation and a natural offer. That is still bounded work under the existing source, purpose, budget and stop conditions. A newly interesting event does not expand permission by itself.

Identify the actual executing host. A foreground window, tray process, process exit, sleep, shutdown, lost network and restart have different implications. Durable records do not execute themselves. Continuing away from a computer needs a real sustainable host, recovery and notification path. Desktop presence, a bounded service, a remote host or a later mobile host can be compared for a genuine requirement, with actual cost, data location, user control and cancellation. Do not create a distributed platform merely to support a concept, or promise continued work where no agent can run.

Initiative also includes pleasure: an interesting discovery, an exhibition the user wanted, a small celebration, time for friends or an evening with no productivity defense. Sometimes an agreed greeting, a little amusing content or quiet company is sufficient. Do not force every opportunity to begin from an unresolved task, or end as study, planning and deadlines.

Existing entrustment and permitted sources can authorize necessary follow-up reading and preparation. Do not ask the same permission for every weather read or each refresh of the same source. New outward commitments, recipients, costs and irreversible consequences belong to their specific decision. They need not become prerequisites for ordinary warmth or already-authorized help.

Prepared help need not become a red dot. A relevant route can wait until a later walking conversation; a letter can sit quietly; an expired result can be withdrawn. Unopened is neither rejection nor permission to press harder. “Not this time” stops that occurrence while a month-long request to notice other opportunities may remain. A fixed agreed reminder still needs fulfillment. A result already discussed in chat does not need repeated broadcasts across channels, while a materially new condition can justify a new decision.

When an appropriate initiative does reach the user, let the same character arrive in the original conversation with actual words and a result that can be continued. A notification can lead back to that same work. One more record on a preparation shelf is not evidence that the companion has spoken to the user. The user should be able to find help without becoming the administrator of background objects.

## Memory is how we reached this point together

I want to be understood and still be able to change. “I focus better on campus” and “I feel freer and more satisfied at home” may both be true. Being absorbed in a task and not wanting to pack up does not establish an inherent dislike of travel. A missed outing may have depended on lacking a computer, having forty minutes left or wanting rest. A borrowed device, a repaired path or a friend's availability can make an old option worth considering again.

Retain reasons, conditions, actual alternatives, hesitation and later outcomes, rather than only labels such as “likes the library” or “efficiency first.” One choice can inform understanding without becoming lifelong personality. People can want solitude and fear loneliness, like rhythm and dislike being managed, or want something useless simply because they love it. The companion can stay with those competing wishes without making one immediately win.

Leave room for experiences that need not become tasks: something we laughed at, an unresolved thought or an honest moment of delight. They can make a later conversation natural without becoming a profile field, reminder or progress metric. Remembering something need not be announced as another successful memory write.

Current words matter immediately. “That is not what I meant” should change the present response and affected work, while long-term organization can follow. Do not make correction wait for an index watermark. A late summary, old task or recovered cache must not revive the withdrawn interpretation. Equally, correcting one reason need not clear all memory or cancel unrelated arrangements. Accurate scope is practical kindness.

Keep a route to the original. A summary's omission does not prove the user never said it; the first empty search does not prove absence. Change the angle, read neighbors, continue pages, compare explanations and ask a question only when its answer matters. Evidence, interpretation, indexes, business records and current deliberation can have distinct responsibilities within existing storage. They need not all become one master profile, or one service each.

Shared experience includes which version was actually shown, which portion was exposed before cancellation, what hint was given and where we stopped. A prepared document was not necessarily read. Solving a problem after a hint is not independent mastery. Reading position and presentation records help continuation; they are not an invitation to eye tracking or a questionnaire after each section. Rendering is not cognition.

“The second one,” “Change this” and “What about that afternoon?” depend on the objects jointly available then. A reordered list, old map or edited note cannot be resolved safely by ordinal alone. Preserve original objects, source locations and revisions so natural reference has something real to point at. This is where memory, tools and OpenUI meet.

“Stop using that judgment about me, but keep the chat” and “Delete that passage” require different accurate effects. The second also concerns descendants that rely on the original. Let the user use ordinary language without managing an internal dependency graph. Keep explicit character choices separate from real-person information: disabling personal memory need not erase a chosen role, and exporting a character must not export private experiences.

Consider a synthetic day. In the morning the student is absorbed in a proof: tired, but still wants the full reasoning. The companion helps finish it and records an explicitly requested nine-o'clock meeting. Later a class changes buildings; within existing scope she obtains and prepares the route, then brings it into the conversation at an appropriate moment. A dinner invitation competes with two hours the student wants for themselves. Compare dinner, travel and the personal time, make the clearly authorized changes and preserve a separately agreed plant-watering reminder.

The day can end with a walk, a film or an unproductive discovery. Tomorrow yesterday's tiredness no longer automatically limits the student, but the accepted meeting remains. The month-long opportunity entrustment remains within its terms too. These are synthetic design situations, not a real person's classes, grades or household history. Holding continuous life together matters more than one unusually polished reply.

## OpenUI should be the paper or map the companion offers

For example, when someone asks where they could go after class, the assistant can unfold relevant places with the actual class occurrence, travel and supported weather. A question about grades may need a clear table or trend with its real policy. An evening comparison may need the same time scale. Keeping a thought may open a note they can keep writing, select and discuss. The model chooses the expression because it helps; the person does not need to know component names.

Anima already has real scene/presentation wiring and a checked interactive-program layer. The current finite DSL/AST, primitives, original scene bindings, host-persisted local inputs and next-turn readback must be preserved. The remaining ambition is richer autonomous organization and useful general construction, not merely installing a library. Nor does one successful program prove the model consistently chooses all suitable surfaces. Distinguish component existence, model discovery, natural selection, actual display, direct operation and meaningful use of that operation in the next answer.

A SceneRef on a tool result or a prepared snapshot is not a displayed surface. A design image is not the display chain either. Inspect each adjacent seam. Maps, time bands, grade tables and editable writing can become composable language. Update the same object when content or layout changes. A pan, date choice, parameter or local play/pause should respond directly rather than invoke a model each frame.

Check the installed OpenUI version, its real source and the desired newer capability separately. A current online example's syntax, gateway or action mechanism does not automatically exist in the pinned dependency, or authorize a different external service. Keeping the original conversation and backend is compatible with a more expressive library; creating a second input area or replacing the main engine is not an automatic prerequisite.

Freedom of expression still has factual foundations. Places come from the active map dataset, grades from actual permitted records and calendar objects from their owner. The model can propose a button; its reading, writing, expiry and result belong to the actual host action path. Viewing, selecting, trial calculation, accepting and executing have different meanings. When an explicit local decision or existing entrustment authorizes a change, update that original object and read the result back, then continue naturally.

Keep the shared map surface for desktop, conversation and public preview, its camera ownership and planar fallback. Warm gray, restrained gold, transparent space and convincing building volume are valid visual aims. Top-down and oblique views, zoom, rotation and pan remain controllable. Camera movement explains space until the user takes over; an update must not suddenly pull it away. A→B→A has repeated stop instances and directed legs, not merely one reused place ID. A selected leg, weather time and agenda interval should return to the same composer.

A building center is not necessarily an entrance; a painted doorway does not establish access. No device location is needed to help from a declared manual start. A past route can be discussed without claiming current verification. The included synthetic geometry remains a labelled diagram, not a real UCI route; a permitted future geographic dataset needs its real license and topology. An attractive imaginary bridge is artwork, not navigation.

Learning and creation belong to this expression. Keep the original question, the user's attempt and the assistant's help distinct. An undisclosed later answer does not appear because background work already computed it; a current explicit request for the complete solution need not be trapped in a fixed hint mode. First person in a jointly written story belongs to its speaker/world, not automatically the user's biography. A simulated friend's response is a possible response, not something the real person said. Discussion may remain unresolved and put aside without owing the system completion.

Read the retained art and its intended page or interaction, then compare it with current rendering. A prior image directory or text review did not establish that every binary had been inspected. Actual approval, a reference idea, an adopted runtime asset and current visible quality remain different. Preserve the user's good retained images as working foundations, including ideas not yet used by the runtime. A few new pictures must not replace that library or imply that the ten-character/page/theme/action matrix is finished. Retired images remain retired, and severe structural errors are not future reference inputs.

I do not want more management panels to prove the product has many functions. I want relevant content to appear during the same conversation, the character to yield when reading needs room, paper that invites reading, an action where it helps and an input/draft that stays mine. Empty, waiting, error, button, settings and recovery text should sound ordinary. Detailed internal states can live in appropriate development or source details; the product should make clear what happened and how to continue.

## Reliable foundations should let the companion help more confidently

There is real work to preserve: versioned notes, schedules and local calendar, complete planning arithmetic, selected windows and rest, original-source lineage, conditional correction, forgetting propagation, resumable actions, bounded investigative tasks, caches, shared reading and background retries. They are not disposable burdens simply because a redesign is large.

Understand what each mechanism protects, then choose its simplest sound expression. Some types can merge, layers can disappear and mechanisms can be replaced. The user's writing, chosen character, accepted arrangements and unfinished work must still arrive intact. Where an original is unavailable, retain that uncertainty; a migration must not invent its history. A new empty database is not proof of a successful upgrade.

Planning respects the whole day: doing, preparation, cleanup, travel, meals, rest and protected open time. Midnight, time zone, existing overlap and unknown ends alter feasibility. Two offered windows do not declare all other time free. A day full of existing commitments is not fixed by producing another unexplained “remaining gap.” Previously scheduled does not mean completed; reopening planning does not revive an expired adoption right or silently shift a deadline.

Tool observations have their own reality. A campus query can be partial; five displayed rows need not be the whole denominator. Courses and exams have occurrences, exceptions and applicable terms. A general activity total is not necessarily an official qualifying workout count. A message draft is not a sent email, and OS submission is not a read notification. Capability descriptions, actual connections and language promises must agree while independent possible work continues.

Let these distinctions become quiet competence. Ordinary conversation need not recite them. If a result is uncertain, explain its concrete present effect and do what can still stand. Make the underlying evidence easy to inspect when needed. Reliability and ease can reinforce each other in the same product.

## Explore the redesign through actual consequences

The work need not follow a rigid construction conveyor belt or fixed answers for fixed stories. Choose a substantive current-stage entry, continue research where evidence calls for it and build a usable slice. The complete twelve-stage goals remain; their units can be reordered, split or combined around actual dependencies without losing any protected experience. A research paper or historical imperative does not itself authorize unrelated work.

Enter the product, choose a real situation that exposes a discontinuity and find where personality, understanding or capability first goes missing. Follow that earliest divergence through code and sources. Consider more than one explanation. Make a change with an observable consequence, then return to the same experience. Change wording or a relevant condition and continue into the later conversation or next day. Let a newly observed problem lead to another diagnosis and repair; the loop need not become a report template for every turn.

Leave real room for invention. A lighter context entry may support a more natural role; a life fragment retaining a reason may help more than ten profile fields; preparation may become useful only in a later related conversation; a map or note may be shared experience rather than an answer attachment; distinct people may initiate care differently while doing the work equally well. Also let evidence show that a favored idea is poor.

Research primary code, papers, component contracts and original character material for a concrete difficulty. Ask what a source actually solves, why its conclusion is supported and how Anima differs. A published product vision does not reveal its algorithm. A memory-question benchmark does not establish lifelong companionship, and a coding agent's long-task method may not fit personal daily life unchanged. Framework names alone have little value.

A useful caution in the working research was the Generative Agents simulation's discussion of overly formal, cooperative characters and interests spreading between them. Treat that as a reason to investigate how generation, summarization, reflection and character guidance jointly flatten identity, not as proof that another final-tone instruction will solve it. Preserve each study's actual scope and reading status. The conclusion still returns to Anima's behavior.

## Verify from the position of the person using the product

Use the actual authorized normal entries and existing relevant checks. A particular development transport must not become a ritual blocking all independent work. Missing a model, account or device can leave a precise evidence condition without cancelling everything else. Nor does a missing condition authorize another paid supplier.

Types, state recovery, idempotency, cancellation, revision invalidation, event ordering, rendering, interaction, persistence and original readback have their own meaningful checks. Real Hermes with controlled model output can establish some wiring; it cannot establish autonomous tool choice. Natural interaction with an authorized real model is needed for that claim. Do not act out the model or insert an answer and call it intelligence. No new tests, fixtures, assertion cases, suites or scripted runners are authorized by this working letter; use existing checks and actual workflows unless the human specifically commissions test development.

A beautiful picture does not prove autonomous presentation, a build does not prove continuous background work and a successful tool receipt does not prove recognizable speech. Keep installed packages, current code, the actual running build and a public preview distinct. A past package's failure is not proof current code failed; new code does not upgrade a past executable by declaration.

Look for positive outcomes: the assistant finds a suitable capability without a tool name; a real permitted trigger produces useful preparation without another message; a return reconnects that work naturally; an interactive result can be used and discussed; the same personality remains present in casual talk, serious work, failure and reopening. Correction changes the actual premise and dependent action, even when the final conclusion happens to remain the same for a new valid reason. No-task conversation, pleasure and listening should also work without a productivity justification.

Look for counterexamples too: a declined outing being pressed again, an unknown outcome reported complete, unexposed material treated as shared memory, a late child reviving a withdrawn reason or an old view silently refreshed. Stories and timelines are a design library, not production routing. Give the model only evidence available at that moment, not future plot or review criteria. A paraphrase should still work; a changed causal reason should be able to change what happens next. A seen solution is not independent learning evidence, and personality is not a score based on a standard line, emoji count or nickname frequency.

Do not add unlimited testing to prove seriousness. Choose the real risk, obtain enough evidence to support the conclusion and continue to the promised result. When an external part cannot run, identify the missing source, permission, platform or model precisely, complete independent portions and retain a usable next entry. A permanent “perhaps connect it later” is not a substitute for authorized work, and an honest unresolved condition is better than fabricated completion.

## Leave the understanding where the next developer can find it

Keep the current README, AGENTS, navigation, contract, architecture, capability record and continuation mutually consistent. Anima is a distinctive, warm companion with real bounded capabilities; personality and natural expression belong throughout tools, memory, initiative and interactive work. More rigorous should not mean more remote, and more charming should not mean better theatrical claims.

Maintain one current baseline and keep full working material in its proper library. Preserve historical reasoning and conflicts as evidence, while active entry points clearly state current work, actual capabilities, pending conditions and unfinished stages. A new reminder need not become hundreds of prohibitions. State the positive product aim and the precise responsibility that supports it.

When a future unit is done, show a real change worth trying: more natural speech, preserved character, a tool or preparation that takes on actual work, a useful surface, memory that handles change and a continuation the user does not need to teach repeatedly. Give the usable entry, proportionate evidence, migration/recovery and specific remaining questions. Counts of files, checks or architecture names are not the result.

There is room to go beyond the examples in this letter, within the human's actual commissioned scope. Read it as someone sitting nearby explaining why this product still matters: I want to say something casually and be understood as a person; when I am busy, some actual entrusted work can be looked after; when I return, that recognizable caring voice can continue with me.

## Additional design feedback: depth, collaboration and expression

The following feedback preserves concrete reasons behind the design. It is development material, not the companion's system prompt or a personal history.

### Read the difficult material instead of replacing it with an outline

Tool use and memory are central. The discussions contain many situations, doubts, changes of mind and unfinished thoughts. Their disorder is part of why a short list can miss the point. Read the complete discussions, not just a compacted summary. Use the linked full bodies and keep exact progress when continuing. A lead who personally understands those ideas can ask workers to implement a considered design while still welcoming their objections and inventions.

### A principle demonstration must operate and sound

The piano reference points to a useful standard. In a simplified grand-piano action, a key drives an action that accelerates a hammer, permits escapement, strikes the string and rebounds. Dampers end vibration; a sustain pedal can allow it after release. Build from reliable actual mechanical references before claiming a calibrated model. A schematic can simplify motion while retaining an honest contact and causal chain.

Let the user compare C4 with sustain off and on: similar excitation, different damping after release. Keep that distinction audible and visible in the same actual program state. For the ideal string approximation, the fundamental frequency is

\[
f_1=\frac{1}{2L}\sqrt{\frac{T}{\mu}},
\]

where \(L\) is effective length, \(T\) tension and \(\mu\) linear density. The hammer affects excitation; string parameters affect pitch; bridge and soundboard transmit vibration. This relation alone is not a complete piano sound or a measured mechanical model. The actual instrument also needs appropriate keyboard arrangement, release, chord/pedal behavior, sound and lifecycle handling.
