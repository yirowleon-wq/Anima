# Research and process limitations

## The historical repository was not available

The original research did not obtain, run or modify the actual map project, enumerate real features, measure road widths/heights/registration residuals or count actual remaining defects. Images supplied anomaly leads, not complete spatial truth. The root causes remain baseline hypotheses; the research does not establish those missing facts.

## Source access was partial

The historical author read an official map entry and another campus-map entry, but did not obtain the original plan image or complete site geometry. Some athletics technical PDFs failed to download. The full large CityGML standard did not open; the source reading was its official overview, supplemented by the actually read modeling references, 3dfier and val3dity. Neither failed downloads nor unread standard chapters count as evidence. The [source register](../research/SOURCES.json) retains each reading scope. There was no claim of comparing current commercial imagery or current construction conditions.

## Precision has a ceiling

A photograph is not automatically orthorectified, a roof is not grounded footprint, and a schematic cannot become survey truth through prose. Without measurement or reliable records, prompts cannot create accurate heights. Continue targeted feasible evidence routes for important gaps; when those are genuinely exhausted, report the exact blocker rather than inventing elaborate massing. Acceptance of a useful map is not survey certification.

## Rights depend on the actual use

The historical author read OSM and several provider policies, but did not audit the user's contracts or special authorizations. Original region-specific providers are anonymized, not replaced with invented contracts. Public release may require additional applicable checks at release time. These are engineering risk boundaries, not a legal opinion or a blanket permission/prohibition for every manual reference. Public visibility, manual tracing and withholding the original image do not automatically authorize extraction, derivation, caching or redistribution.

## Client and collaboration capabilities were unknown

Official worktree documentation did not establish which entry points the actual client exposed, whether a goal command existed there or whether the author could open local chats. Files do not create real branches. Use current collaboration instructions and real capabilities; honest serial work or read-only parallel research is preferable to claiming nonexistent sessions. The source's old subagent ban was contextual and does not replace current Anima governance. A wait, compaction or local tool error does not establish a terminal model/session failure.

## Prompts do not enforce honesty or permissions

An untrusted executor can fabricate prose, hashes, templates and check outputs. Version binding, reconciled denominators and real failure witnesses improve inspectability, not absolute honesty. Stronger assurance needs actual reproducible inputs/outputs, protected thresholds and gates, independent review and actual human acceptance. If permission separation is unavailable, disclose the process risk rather than saying the prompt blocks every shortcut.

## More documents are not more map quality

Separate files support distinct handoffs. Real work should maintain one structured authoritative fact set and only useful derived views. The schema checks record structure, not geometry, reality, rights, cross-record integrity or acceptance. Nulls/placeholders and template records do not become completed scope. This package contains methods, research and future commissions, not a repaired map, zero-defect claim or proof that every building passed.
