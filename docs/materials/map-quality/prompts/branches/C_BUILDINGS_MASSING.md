# Family C: building identity, contours and supported massing

Read [COMMON](COMMON.md) and the actual contract before any future authorized writes.

## Inputs and exclusive writes

Read sources, parent outlines/parts, height/base fields, generator, approved reference/claims and complex/organization/entrance mappings. Roads and grounds are read-only neighbors. First distinguish grounded footprint, roof projection and part envelope. Write only assigned canonical buildings, part relations and building generator. Do not move roads, trim water, change reference, global color or thresholds. Propose a needed shared-renderer interface change to the integrator first.

## Work

Inventory all outlines and parts. Detect duplicate extrusion, lost courtyard holes, triangulation-derived imaginary parts, height/base-height confusion and arbitrary defaults. Apply F/B/P/D evidence bands to each important building. A height estimate needs a basis and range; an unknown cannot masquerade as measurement.

Use complex academic blocks, a specialist center and connected wings as anonymous challenging cases, then examine every building sharing the generator. Each wing, platform, elevated link and roof needs relocatable evidence. Remove unsupported detail while preserving real contour, courtyard, required buildings and supported complexity; do not turn the whole site into uniform boxes or flat planes.

Separate grounded footprint from upper overhang projection. An elevated structure needs base/clearance, membership and passage evidence; exceeding the ground outline does not automatically invalidate it. Choose validity rules from the declared Solid/MultiSurface representation. Mathematical validity alone does not make a building true.

## Required directions and delivery

Q04–Q06, Q11, Q13–Q17, Q22, Q24. Deliver building/part evidence, allowed complexity, real contour/massing patch, parent/entrance mapping, individual source comparisons for key buildings, geometry/neighborhood results and unknown height/form scope. Preserve actual scoped versioned witnesses, shared IDs and upstream invalidation as COMMON requires.

## Readiness gate

No unsupported protrusions or incorrect level relations; key massing has a reviewable basis; contours/courtyards are source-checked; illegal overlaps are resolved and neighboring relationships hold. Simple incorrect geometry, random height renamed LOD1 or a required unknown height counted complete prevents readiness. Submit scoped `MERGE_READY`, read-only research readiness or a real blocker; never whole-map acceptance.
