# Family E: semantic style, labels and multiple scales

Read [COMMON](COMMON.md) and the actual contract. Current product themes/no-green-interface rules govern any future authorized implementation.

## Inputs and exclusive writes

Read current theme, layers, light/shadow, labels, interaction state, stable entity schema and evidence grades. Study tokens/scales read-only early; write only after entity interfaces and exact ownership are approved. Own assigned theme tokens, layer styles, label placement/generalized display and camera-display interfaces. Do not change factual coordinates, physical width/height, classes or routing connectivity. Shared component changes need an explicit allowlist update.

## Work

Extract and preserve dark warm ground, gray/gold buildings, restrained transparency and technical clarity. Define ground, roads, building, land, water, paving, sports, state and shadow roles. Color, border, transparency and glow follow semantics/state/shared light rather than ad hoc entity overrides. Shadow is a derived effect, not vegetation; audit colors are not finished design.

Calibrate site/zone/block/close bands from the actual engine. Prioritize important roads/buildings and the current navigation target. Inspect candidate anchors, real text boxes, UI occupancy, oblique orientation and complex/part identity. Avoid repeated giant labels, unreachable essential identity and control collisions. Hiding labels or enlarging the viewport cannot evade a defect.

Generalize derived display only, retaining entrances, bridgeheads, junctions, holes and shared boundaries. Inspect LOD transitions and tile seams. A retained selection card must be assessed against intended spatial reference, not arbitrarily changed. Inspect both normal and focused audit views. Never move a building or sever a road to beautify a composition.

## Required directions and delivery

Q08, Q20–Q24. Hand off factual geometry needs rather than editing them. Deliver token table, previous/new mapping and justified exceptions, label/scale rules, purposeful view list, actual occlusion/transition observations, limited useful comparisons and patch. Preserve scoped versions/witnesses, shared IDs and upstream invalidation under COMMON.

## Readiness gate

Retained style with distinguishable geography, identifiable essential labels, suitable scale/oblique/control states and no concealed entities. Replacing the direction with white UI, moving facts, stripping all detail or presenting attractive screenshots while real interaction remains obstructed prevents readiness. Submit only scoped research/readiness/blocker for integration.
