# Family A: sources, coordinate reference and attribute evidence

Read [COMMON](COMMON.md) and the actual instantiated contract. This future commission grants no unspecified write access.

## Inputs and dependencies

Inspect the complete raw source list, parser/conversion chain, generator locations, manual annotations, baseline doubts and rights records. Stabilize common references before factual repair. Other families' entities are read-only study and decision recommendations.

## Exclusive writes

Only the assigned source index, claim increments, control-point/registration reports and an explicitly approved shared conversion module. Do not modify that module before approval. Do not directly change road, building or grounds geometry.

## Work

Check actual products and allowed uses. Evaluate names, contour, road relations, height and current construction separately. Record capture, publication and access dates, and independent lineage. Trace the actual geographic/projected/provider-specific reference, axes and units; detect both repeated conversion and missing conversion. Schematics acquire a geometric role only after it is demonstrated.

Choose stable ground control distributed across the applicable extent. Separate fit and independent points. Compare explainable models and retain parameters, vectors, RMSE/P95/maximum and local distortion. Calibrate thresholds from evidence and use, without loosening them to pass. Fusion uses entity matching and attribute judgment, not averaged conflicting coordinates. A key building's name, footprint, wings and unknown height remain separate claims.

A single failed search is not exhaustion; endless repetition of an ineffective entry is not progress. Record actual routes/findings, concrete restrictions and the next promising source. Keep unsupported key reference/rights blocked while continuing independent evidence work.

## Required directions and handoff

Q01–Q03, Q25 and the Q13 contour decisions affected by source suitability. Deliver the exact source-role/use matrix, property claims, reproducible alignment configuration, independent results, decisions/open conflicts, affected entities and invalidated downstream scope. Preserve shared issue IDs and owned increments. Compare baseline/schema/alignment on synchronization; changes invalidate dependent results. Cross-family changes are handoffs, not unauthorized edits.

## Readiness gate

Rights are explicit, the scoped reference chain is reproducible, independent error meets previously frozen use-specific thresholds and critical conflicts are decided. Unknown accuracy described as passed, a vague multiple-source sentence, fit-only residuals or unregistered production input prevents `MERGE_READY`. Report only scoped readiness or a real blocker. Integration and final acceptance remain the integrator/designated acceptor's responsibility.
