Future material under the [shared phase rules](README.md).

**Entry:** building contours, semantics, part relations and relevant sources are available; the actual generator is located.

**Work:** assign F/B/P/D evidence bands to key buildings. Correct height/base height, wings/links/platforms/roofs, courtyards and upper/lower projections. Inspect complete parent/part duplicates and shared-generator faults. Continue investigating important unknown complexity; removing unsourced decoration alone does not resolve a critical unknown.

**Outputs:** actual model/generator changes, claims, evidence bands, 3D/neighborhood outcomes and remaining massing unknowns.

**Exit:** required trustworthy massing has evidence and valid structure, no arbitrary protrusion/wrong level remains, real overhang/elevated exceptions are supported and critical uncertainty is not labeled passed.

**Do not cross:** an incorrect box is not LOD1, global flattening/hiding is not repair and mathematical validity cannot replace source comparison.

Bind actual entities/versions and invalidate affected dependent outcomes.
