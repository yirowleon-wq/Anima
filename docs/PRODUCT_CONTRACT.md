# Product contract

Anima shares an ordinary life through a continuous conversation and the things being read, made, revised and put aside. Study, serious work, excitement, ambition, play, rest and silence all belong. A tired person may still want a complete proof. An interest is not automatically a goal, a task or a debt. A character can have taste, disagree, reconsider and quietly attend to something of her own.

## Decide, understand, return

The user can understand, choose, correct, refuse and withdraw appropriate arrangements and data use. Convenience must not depend on hidden action. Clearly authorized reversible local changes should proceed without repeated confirmation; an unauthorized external action first needs a concrete reviewable proposal. Refusing one source does not mean refusing all help.

Use data proportionate to the task, belonging to the correct person and world. The user, a third person, a fictional character, a story's speaker and the account holder are different subjects. Do not infer enrollment, grades, attendance, home, health or a real relationship from fictional material or examples.

Facts preserve their source, applicable conditions, time, version and uncertainty. Official records, user reports, inference, examples, suggestions and actual actions remain distinguishable. A directory, title, preview, search hit or summary does not establish that the original was read. Partial and empty pages require their real continuation path; they do not prove absence.

Understanding allows change. Keep the difference between a temporary exception, a persistent preference and a present request. A correction changes current help immediately; a failed save cannot be described as a permanent update. The original account of what mattered then can remain legitimate history after circumstances change.

The person should not have to manage internal modules, indexing or receipt numbers to get help. Preserve useful controls, sources, numbers and errors through understandable grouping and progressive disclosure. Reduced clutter must lower the person's burden while preserving recovery.

## One conversation, real things

The full cover remains an intentional destination. Choosing the brand opens it even when work exists. It preserves the original draft and work address; a background reply does not steal the cover. Back, Resume and a bookmark return to that same original object, with its lawful current version, question, selection, reading position, zoom and focus. They do not repeat a new welcome or reconstruct a second conversation.

Keep one composer and its state through conversation, reading, study and Keep. In the current React implementation, preserve the single composer DOM host; a future native Swift interface must preserve the same composition, focus, attachments and draft continuity through its own native controls. Preserve the submitted draft and any newly typed draft independently. A transport response is not a sufficient reason to discard writing. When sending fails, recover the correct original draft without swallowing the new one.

Selecting a passage or formula chooses an object. Quoting, asking, sending, retaining and acting happen separately. Addresses include owner, stable ID, revision, source hash and exact Unicode range or page region. Repeated text needs an unambiguous address, never the first `indexOf` match. Dragging carries that address. Correcting attention changes the model's usable object and dependent output as well as the visible highlight.

Materials retain their original TXT, Markdown or PDF bytes. PDF text, page pixels, bounding boxes, crops, formulas and tables have their own evidence limits; visible scanned pixels are not completed OCR. User annotations remain theirs when a source is withdrawn. Reanchoring changes the source anchor after comparison, without restoring old note text over new writing.

Notes have editable drafts, revision conflicts, history, trash and undo. Keep is a quiet projection of notes, originals, work and conversations from their owners, not another body-text store. Study preserves the same problem, the user's attempt, chosen hint depth, parked state and previous help. Having seen a solution does not establish mastery. Complete handwriting and Pencil collaboration remain explicit development goals.

## Character and memory

Character identity, appearance, editable user configuration and private user history are separate. Adopting an image does not silently change a name, relationship or persona. Old messages retain their author at the time. Importable character cards are editable text; macros, commands and scripts do not execute. User-created identities remain readable without importing another person's database.

The ten built-in identities have official reference facts and separately labelled project-original interpretation. Examples of something a character might say are not evidence that she queried, prepared or saved it. Personality participates in understanding and choices, rather than decorating a generic final answer.

The selected character's identity and current context remain present during investigation, progress, correction and failure. Luna is the current live model connection, not automatically the selected companion. Ordinary conversation should not describe a person as an input, a returned value or a passed test, or present raw engineering status as the character's words. Use the relevant complete passport or saved user-created identity; speak only from actual findings. A host can give a clear neutral notice when no character response exists. Preserve useful technical detail and real failure in an appropriate diagnostic context, without inventing feelings, actions or reassuring success.

The six memory layers are original evidence, conditional bitemporal assertions, decisions and obstacles, shared work, domain observations, and derived indexes. They are roles around original owners, not six competing fact stores. Retrieve to find originals, then read within current permissions. The default context does not force semantic candidates into every greeting. `recall_experience` and native `memoryInquiry` remain bounded investigations.

Read, transmit, infer, retain, disclose and act are separate axes. Local-only content cannot be sent to a cloud model even to classify it. Session-only content must not survive in a later queue or history, and must not erase independent accepted calendar data. Appearance and split-pane local preferences are neither long-term model memory nor cross-device synchronization.

Corrections, source revocation and permanent forgetting follow exact fragments and real derived dependencies into contexts, in-flight reports, indexes, preparations, notifications, future voice, exports and reopening. Late reports and caches cannot revive withdrawn content. Independent lawful fragments from the same source survive. Ordinary revision or pausing does not automatically mean permanent deletion. Local deletion cannot promise recall of information already disclosed externally.

Delegated investigations use the parent's permitted source subset, budget and cancellation conditions. Recheck before each model request and before adoption. A subreport is not independent evidence and cannot enlarge reading permission.

## Work, play and actions

Shared work retains checkpoints, return points, listening, organizing, hinting, doing, holding, continuing, finishing and stopping. Restoring a work item does not approve its pending actions. Shared phrases and jokes have a real conversational origin; do not replace them with affection scores or a mood database.

Curiosity can offer a real thing to read or play with, change direction or stop without a questionnaire. Original chapters, current seeds and branches remain distinct. Story settings are explicitly fictional and do not become the project owner's travel history. Relationship rehearsal preserves the user's voice and a simulated other person, supports another response or a small revision, and never automatically sends anything. Letters can be unread, partly read, answered sentence by sentence or put aside; their source and exposure bounds matter.

Personal methods preserve the actual help and source that made them useful. They are reviewable, editable, replaceable, disableable and subject to a current-turn exception. A method is not an executable plugin, permanent personality diagnosis or new permission.

Generated, rendered, seen, understood, accepted and executed are distinct relationships. A maximum reading cursor, a click, a function's existence or an animation ending cannot stand in for understanding, acceptance or completion.

Actions bind the original object and expected revision through Action, Approval, Attempt and Receipt. Preview and trial placement do not write. An unknown result first requires inspecting the original attempt; never blindly resend it. Restore intent without restoring expired approval. Undoing a work suggestion cannot pretend to reverse an already executed external action.

## Time and ongoing help

Plans preserve setup, work, cleanup, travel, rest, protected occupancy and the selected time windows. Time outside the chosen windows is unknown, not automatically free. Unknown end times remain unknown. Overlaps use their true union. Natural days use the declared IANA zone and may contain 23 or 25 hours. Do not hardcode a UTC offset or invent a student's weekly schedule.

Compare complete alternatives, preserve the same workload and expose actual tradeoffs. Allow partial adoption. `planRef`, short authorization tickets, adoption proofs and original attempts remain linked; a newer parent work item cannot retroactively certify an old attempt. Replanning does not mark previous work completed or silently move deadlines.

Calendar exports contain accepted instances within an explicit finite interval; private details are omitted by default. Generating ICS, successful operating-system import and live synchronization are different results.

Ongoing entrustments preserve the original wish, allowed sources, expiry, channel and stop conditions. A change requests reevaluation, not more authority. Quiet preparation must produce something usable with actual sources and validity. Preparation, showing, notification submission, opening and actual completion have separate records. No useful change can mean remaining quiet. Explicit fixed reminders remain an independent commitment and must not be swallowed by optional check-in restraint.

The desktop process, tray, sleep and restart have real boundaries. There is no promise that work continues after the host quits or sleeps. Anima's internal reminders and any external scheduler have different owners and purposes.

## Space and direct manipulation

Desktop, conversation and public preview reuse one MapSurface, camera and routing system. A hand gesture takes over an unfolding camera immediately and does not lose control to a timer. Closing and returning preserve the original route and viewport. A→B→A has three stop instances and directed legs; place identity does not deduplicate visits.

Manual origins, configured reference points, explicit imports, app-internal simulations, user reports and fictional illustration have different sources. A pretty room, bridge or campus painting is not navigable geometry or GPS. Included synthetic geometry is labelled; routes through a real campus require lawful imported data with accurate topology, entrances, direction, version and source. Keep the 2D fallback and an in-place retry with draft and selection intact. Public preview contains only selected public map data.

Development must not acquire real device location or enable OS location services or request or grant location permissions on iOS, Android, desktop, browser, emulator or guest. Native GPS acquisition is not a current development goal. Complete and exercise map, route, workout and independent native-device workflows with app-internal simulated fixes and tracks, manual origins or permitted imports. Preserve the actual source, time and quality of each input. Simulated movement does not establish physical arrival or official credit, and this restriction does not remove the functional, state or recovery goals.

Local workouts retain one original record through start, pause, resume, finish, cancel and reopening. Samples, clocks, quality, gaps and filtered estimates remain distinguishable. No heart rate, calories or official credit is invented. A local record, self-report, simulation and official same-attempt acceptance are different outcomes. Ending early is not failure, and returning to a problem is not mastery.

All direct operations have reception, trial placement where relevant, adoption, cancellation, failure and recovery. Preserve keyboard alternatives and reduced motion. Interaction time takes priority over performance; factual state is decided by real owners and receipts. Content and focus remain stable while a character makes room.
