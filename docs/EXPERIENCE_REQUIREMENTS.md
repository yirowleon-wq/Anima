## Six ways to be present

The forms can coexist in the same conversation. Choose their relative prominence for the current task and compare useful alternatives in that same task. A compact sphere, paper corner, tray, small window, status or edge signal is a design option; no one form is the universal answer.

| ID | Form | What makes it useful | Continuity and completion boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AP1 | A page corner or bookmark | Reading, derivation and sustained attention can carry the actual unfinished question | Stable original/version/range; keyboard access, reveal, cancel and return; a decorative folded corner is insufficient |
| AP2 | A fine pointer or note beside an object | “Here” or “these two” has a visible correctable referent | Real single or paired anchors; the model's explanation and dependencies follow a correction; the user's cursor remains theirs |
| AP3 | A small shared work surface | Two people take turns with the same diagram, plan or draft | Local suggestions, takeover, partial adoption, revision, undo and conflict remain under the original owner |
| AP4 | A compact carrier of the current thing | Move between pages without losing the problem, route or phrase | Carry object groups, relation, lawful version, question and stopping point; reopen the same work rather than a fresh welcome |
| AP5 | Voice with very little visual demand | Walking, running or household activity can remain easy to glance at | Explicit quiet/resume/stop, audio focus, editable transcription and a stable visible control; do not claim unimplemented voice or background service |
| AP6 | A light response without a task | Calling the character can be a complete interaction by itself | She changes attention, listens or naturally returns; no forced questionnaire or emotional diagnosis; the actual conversation remains real |

## Eight details that make continuity tangible

| ID | Detail | Observable outcome and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DT1 | Make interruption graceful | Typing, composition, taking up a pen, direct dragging or a new topic immediately takes over the old goal. Gestures, emphasis and optional voice settle safely without moving focus or clearing a draft. |
| DT2 | Remember how things were placed | A comparison group preserves both objects' versions and stopping points, A/B roles, correspondence, question and local placement. A single scroll offset does not restore a comparison. Other devices adapt layout while preserving the relation. |
| DT3 | Give sounds a family resemblance | Receiving, putting down, unfolding and calling share related materials and accents; inverse actions relate, small variations reduce repetition. Actual piano audio follows its own physics and audio clock. Mute remains fully usable. |
| DT4 | Show only useful real changes on return | Reveal relevant differences in the original material or arrangement. Let the person inspect old/new and dismiss the trace. No change means simply continuing; do not invent a recap or unread debt. |
| DT5 | Put preparation where it will be used | A real example is available beside its problem, an old draft at the comparison, a route at departure, and an outing's thought beside the original proof. Preserve the preparation, association, validity and retrieval path; a message claiming readiness is insufficient. |
| DT6 | Let actions have a reason | A call turns attention; a handed passage is received; uncertain dragging can leave room; finishing puts things down. A character may also read her own book or rest quietly. Idle life need not be an invented business event. |
| DT7 | End quietly and without penalties | “I'll read on my own,” an early return or a taskless pause can be an entire ending. Leave the needed objects and draft. No reward, blame, forced new plan or progress debt. |
| DT8 | Leave room around a shared joke | Keep the context and feedback, use it occasionally, allow interruption or refusal and allow it to be absent. Do not fill silence with a timer's punchline. |

## One outing, from desk to return

| ID | Part of the same day | Required behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RUN1 | A reason to go outside | A university requirement, a voluntary goal and simply wanting air have different evidence and motivation. Keep the actual words, original problem/bookmark and chosen route. Leaving is not failure or proof of not knowing the problem. |
| RUN2 | The departure | Open the selected route and relevant conditions from the same work, preserving their source. The route is a candidate until adopted. Start a local record only through its authorized owner; label simulated progress, and do not invent movement while waiting for a source. |
| RUN3 | A light rhythm on the way | Offer a short grounded response and let music, silence or conversation take priority. “Quiet for now” stops optional speech; “we can talk” first reevaluates relevance rather than replaying an old segment. |
| RUN4 | A half-formed thought | A thought about the problem attaches to that same problem as editable original words/transcription and a draft. Do not force note organization on the road, convert uncertain speech into a lasting assertion or infer mastery. |
| RUN5 | Unknown conditions and an early finish | Keep quality, gaps and time sources honest. Unknown heart rate, cadence or calories stay unknown. Follow the person's actual report; slowing or ending early is legitimate, without an invented failed goal. |
| RUN6 | Come back to the original question | The same route, local record and road-side thought return beside the original proof and draft. The person may continue the proof, leave the thought parked or end. Original version and source permissions are rechecked. |
| RUN7 | Keep local and official results distinct | Saving the outing can settle into a small route bookmark immediately. Official acceptance changes only the matching record after the real provider's same-attempt receipt; a simulated outing cannot establish physical arrival or official credit. Unknown is inspected, never blindly submitted again. |

Music, character voice and cues have separate volumes and focus. Voice stops for a person speaking, a call or lost relevance. A map gesture owns the camera until the person explicitly resumes following. Clocks distinguish monotonic sample intervals, host receipt and official record time. A restart invalidates a former monotonic origin. Develop and exercise the complete route, workout, state and recovery experience with app-internal simulated fixes and tracks, manual origins or permitted imports, preserving provenance and quality. Never acquire real device location or enable OS location services or request or grant location permissions on any platform, browser, emulator or guest. Native GPS acquisition is not a current development goal.

## Richer possibilities that remain open

Borrow an hour of a possible future self: a short photography exploration, an interesting piece of a field, a competition's week or a day in another imagined home. Keep fiction separate from actual experience and irreversible commitment. Characters can offer a reasoned preference, be persuaded and make a useful rough beginning rather than turning every vague wish into a questionnaire.

Strange small shops, puzzles, rhythm toys, light, a story branch or an unfamiliar interest can be worth spending time with without becoming a goal. A former chapter or exact sentence must be recoverable even when it is not the latest seed. While making a program, retrieve actual newly needed signatures efficiently; do not repeatedly inject an entire manual. Text, equation, wave, number, color and sound can share one parameter relation. Bounded layout readback should help repair a real clipped creation without claiming whole-computer perception.

The two independent sixteen-item extension families are retained in the [roadmap](DEVELOPMENT_ROADMAP.md) as **Experience-X01–X16** and **Platform-X01–X16**. The [full synthetic scenarios](scenarios/README.md) preserve 128 situations, twelve changing timelines, forty broad directions, thirty-six stories and two hundred questions. [Mobile requirements](scenarios/MOBILE_REQUIREMENTS.md), [dated mobile references](scenarios/MOBILE_REFERENCES.md) and [map quality](scenarios/MAP_QUALITY.md) retain the independent mobile and geographic design specifications. A count or title is not a substitute for their complete conditions and counterfactuals.

These collections inform judgment. They are not proof of approval, real biography, finished capability or a production route keyed by scenario number.

## Independent devices and full performance

First establish an independent iOS/iPad host and polish its real lifecycle, then bring Android to the same quality. A phone has its own private primary store, credentials, permission, clock, scheduler, outbox, cancellation and recovery. With desktop Vite/Node/Electron/gateway/debug proxies stopped and USB forwarding disconnected, it must reopen local work and perform permitted outgoing work itself. A location bridge, installed window or remote desktop does not meet this goal. The native hosts still need complete map, route and workout behavior using the permitted virtual, manual or imported inputs described above.

Synchronize stable original objects, author/source/causal versions and deletion barriers; deduplicate and preserve conflicts. Apply barriers before old offline content on reconnection. Do not copy live SQLite/WAL, credentials, OS permissions or immediately dispatchable effects during import. Another device's continuation is adopted by the user; layout, focus, scroll, volume and per-frame motion normally stay local. Pencil strokes remain original data, with recognized text as a revisable derived version.

All ten characters, nine page families and four themes need their meaningful full quality and required motions. The coverage matrix includes scene, character, time, season, mood, action, activity, outfit, theme, weather, pose/contact, appearance, device/layout and audio. Approved, compatible shared use, static fallback, not applicable and missing mean different things. Missing a necessary handoff is not “not applicable”; a blink or static fallback is not a completed normal gesture.

## Completion means the real result

A formal stage preserves its original responsibilities, all eight substantive implementation roles and its current gates. A first-character sequence proves the route and must be expanded; a screenshot, build or numerical score cannot certify the full experience. Mechanism, real model, interaction/motion/audio, art and platform evidence have separate scopes. If an in-scope defect can be corrected, repair and recheck it. If an external condition is genuinely unavailable, keep that part incomplete with the exact dependency while finishing independent work.

The final development story uses the same person, passage, route and creation across pointing/correction, a first attempt, an outing/thought, return/partial adoption, music/a letter and cross-device withdrawal. A second story must also support excitement and aimless play. Quiet, reduced motion and large text remain complete ways to use the product. Nothing requires constant movement or a constant task.
