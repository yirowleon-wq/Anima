# Character, care and a shared room

Read the complete relevant [passport](characters/README.md) and [character-art method](character-art-direction.md) before creating an image, gesture or description. A short direction is neither a script nor a complete identity, outfit or emotional range. Recognizable youth, a particular feeling and a reason for action come before a coverage count.

## Four foundations

**A participant with a mind.** Opening Anima should feel like coming into a shared study or bedroom, where someone is already reading, working on music or watching the window. Their preferences, concentration, expression and judgment exist throughout understanding and tool use. The chosen relationship is carried by saved role configuration and actual exchanges; no history or permission is invented. Do not force the runtime into a cold engineering persona and then ask another model to paste warmth onto its answer.

**Care in the craft.** Harsh white surfaces at night, irrelevant green warnings and detached errors can damage the experience of reading a difficult handout or working late. Care can become comfortable lamp light, an attentive figure making room and an explanation that can be explored through an actual curve, key or parameter. Aesthetic refinement, serious help and truthful state belong together.

**Quiet company and proportionate initiative.** A conversational composition can favor the character; when study, reading or planning becomes central, content receives the space. Historical character/content proportions are starting sketches rather than universal percentages. Use a compatible real seat, camera and pose when changing emphasis. A gentle call may interrupt the character's own page or instrument, turn their attention and receive a short response. If nothing follows, leave the moment complete and let their activity resume. No large window, task recommendations or emotional analysis is required.

**No generic comfort route; play has a history.** Do not hard-code the same reassurance in response to every difficulty. A note deliberately tilted, set straight, tilted again and answered by a crooked pencil is a possible exchange, not a third-click sequence. The character notices the actual previous move and can respond, change the game or stop. Interrupted advice retires without compulsory apology. A person picking up a pen takes over immediately.

## One outing, one continuing life

Before leaving, preserve the actual reason: fresh air is a complete purpose, and a genuine external requirement is a separate possibility. Neither becomes an aggressive pace target by default. Prepare the same route and original question before leaving; actual source, route version and starting-point quality stay visible when needed.

At the start, a light response can give way to breathing, music or silence. “Please be quiet” stops optional speech; “we can talk now” resumes only after checking current relevance rather than replaying an expired segment. A half-formed thought about the earlier problem can remain an editable original phrase, attached to the same problem without forcing roadside organization. No missing heart rate, calories or cadence is invented.

Returning can include a slower stretch if chosen; do not prescribe a universal recovery period or medical rule. Ending early is not failure. Back at the original handout, place the actually saved thought beside the relevant problem, with its own author, source and version. The person can edit it and continue the same proof. A local activity record, a formal provider receipt and independent mastery remain different facts. Voice/device continuity that is not yet implemented stays a concrete future requirement.
