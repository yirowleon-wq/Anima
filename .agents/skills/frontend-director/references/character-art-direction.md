# From understanding a person to sharing the same picture

Read the complete relevant [passport](characters/README.md) and selected outfit before creating. Stable terminology prevents omissions; a character's entire dialogue and expression must not be mechanically repeated. This is a production method, not an extra runtime permission.

## Begin with the experience

Ask what someone should feel when opening this place. “She points,” “the character is smaller,” or “there is a card” describes an object, not the purpose. Perhaps she is still reading her own book but raises her eyes because someone familiar returned; perhaps a shared note is sounding and the next explanation belongs beside it. Establish that relation before deciding hands, gaze and composition.

Affection need not become the same soft smile. It can bring concentration, excitement, a wish for credit, a half-beat of awkwardness after being understood, annoyance at unfairness or a willingness to keep sitting together. The character has interests and judgment; the person can continue, decline or remain silent. Choose one or two motivated feelings, not simultaneous shyness, sorrow, tenderness and exhaustion.

Human warmth includes truth. A painted offered note does not establish completed research. A pointing gesture needs a real usable target in the running interface. Concept art may express a proposal, while live words and actions follow current state, actual intent and original-owner receipts.

## Bring identity into each creation

Read the current full passport, complete outfit, reviewed inputs and latest human corrections; a name plus hair color and “gentle girl” is insufficient. Production records retain passport/outfit version, the actual submitted prompt and every input, rather than a reconstructed summary.

Keep base hair color, iris color, hairstyle/accessory relationships, garment construction, sock opening, shoe shape and characteristic bearing. “Below-knee calf socks, uncovered knees” cannot become ambiguous “stockings.” A person can change clothes, but select a concrete outfit instead of mixing school, casual and stage pieces. A publicly displayed outfit proves it was shown, not that it is a favorite. Unverified birthday, height, food, wealth details or relationship must remain unknown.

Future continuous creation preserves the approved face, head/body scale, clothes and linework. In the same activity/camera, weather chiefly changes the complete light field, rather than random head size, leg arrangement or body proportion. A deliberate activity change needs its own cause and motive. Gaze has an actual object: finishing a line before looking up should connect book, eyes and paused hand. Already accepted minor differences keep their actual approval/candidate status; do not retrospectively rewrite exact prompts or manufacture a scoring matrix. Structural failures and retired images still exit.

Published facts, actor/director reading, visible image observation and original Anima interaction remain separate. Saved personality and address belong to the original owner; changing a picture does not edit them.

## Recreate as coherent two-dimensional art

Official references identify a person and outfit, but are not product cutouts. Re-create expressive line weight, facial planes, eyes, fabric, hair and room reflections as one coherent scene. A source image may look animated, flat or rendered; that material is not a template to paste into another atmosphere.

Do not elongate the jaw, narrow half-closed eyes or use adult fashion anatomy for composure. Do not substitute infant proportions, giant glass eyes or a vacant doll face for youth. Youth, intelligence and particular feeling can coexist without copying a rigid official standing pose or uniform smile.

Rejected anatomy/identity/emotion art, runtime collages and polluted thick-panel compositions do not become new inputs, even labeled “identity only.” Review their derivatives through real lineage and rebuild from clean empty space with detailed text. Independent portraits, cats and other small forms are not casually cropped heads from room pictures.

## Direct one causal moment

Write a short continuous account, then turn it into natural visual direction:

1. **Her own activity:** the page, thought or object has a reason before the person arrives.
2. **Shared object and trigger:** a familiar voice, actual source line, a key just played or a pen put down. Without a target, do not force pointing.
3. **What matters to her:** a distinctive responsibility, curiosity, pride, hesitation or affection and its relation to the current wish.
4. **Eyes, brows and mouth:** where gaze arrives, what loosens, what smile has or has not caught up. Do not turn every facial channel to maximum.
5. **Hands and body:** hold a page, steady a cup, withdraw a suggestion or protect a corner. Shoulder, hip and balance cooperate; objects have weight and a place to land.
6. **The next person's choice:** continued speech, writing, correction, refusal and silence each permit a natural stop, yield or return to her activity.

Add necessary camera, support, occlusion and dimensions afterward. Neither a list of joints that freezes the person nor poetic wording that excuses bad anatomy is sufficient. Show the visible result: the hand still holding her page while the eyes have already greeted someone says more than merely declaring love. Avoid empty decorative adjectives.

## Review the whole, then the detail

At normal size ask whether the person is recognizable, youthful, particular and inviting. Then inspect outfit, eyes/hands, support, room light and real-content safety, using detail views to resolve doubts before returning to normal scale. Correct anatomy is not emotion; correct clothes are not spatial integration; clean-looking UI is not a working drag.

Judge the chosen posture rather than fixed foot count or leg ratio. Together knees, knee-cross, crossed ankles, curled sitting and hanging legs each have different hip/knee/ankle continuity, balance, occlusion and shoe placement. A human correction about sock type, age or targetless pointing retires affected candidates and derivatives until repaired and seen. Old approval is no override.

Character × activity × theme and body topology are separate. The same seated skeleton with a new book is a new situation, not necessarily a new major spatial action. A true change of bodily relation needs new support, scale, silhouette, contact and safe layout. The primary agent accepts the actual integrated result and interactions rather than a subagent's “pass.”

## The interface belongs in the room

A PDF does not reduce the complete room to an advertising strip above a cramped reader. Spread the original between the two people, tall and stable, with conversation opening as needed. A small cover represents that same real document; references keep page/version/origin. A movable frame must truly follow, reverse, cancel and dock.

A small warm daytime paper can work; a huge bright cream slab may fail at night. Reconsider text support, edges, contrast and input through the actual wood, cloth and light. Use an image-led scheme, then live components. Grey-to-white cards, universal transparency or more gradients do not automatically integrate. Preserve actual PDF pixels and source text; shell, surroundings and reading mode have different owners.

## Cartoon cats and edge presence

The Anima small cat is original project expression, not canon or a personality change: a simple two-dimensional character head and complete horizontally resting cartoon cat body, closer to a graphic joke than realistic fur. Compose head, body, paws and tail in one simple style from the start. Do not paste a refined human head onto a realistic cat or near-3D plush ornament. Public visual research can teach abstraction and performance, but does not authorize copying another character or redistributing its image.

Give the form a moment of its own: lying on the page edge, listening to a note or holding a paper corner. Forepaws and abdomen need actual support, and the tail grows from the right root. Inspect transparency at actual small scale on light/dark surfaces. Make eye/ear/tail layers only where needed; move the tail about its root, not the whole PNG. Follow the same live DOM edge as panels move. Content, controls, takeover, hidden state and reduced motion take precedence.

Full body, independent portrait and edge cat can change expression appropriately within the same task, retaining role, object and return. One cat proves one form's route, not ten characters or the whole human motion matrix.

## Portraits reveal youthful character immediately

An independent portrait has a different purpose from a distant room scene. Show a particular attractive moment—being understood, a cherished melody landing, pride or a genuine small smile. Reserve cannot simply become coldness, tiredness or no expression; liveliness does not require ten identical grins.

The human-selected original portrait takes precedence over a later agent candidate. If a long face appears mature, refine the selected face's middle/lower vertical impression while keeping youthful cheek volume, a natural short chin, identity and intelligence. No mechanical face-length percentage, infant roundness or enlarged glass eyes replaces judgment.

Begin with a true close composition. Face/hair use the main canvas; only necessary neck/shoulder/collar remain, with identity accessories and circular crop safety. Do not shrink a large torso drawing into a tiny circle or crop a room head. Framing responds to the actual hair and face rather than a universal percentage.

First choose the moment she herself values. Then make eyes, brow release, readable mouth curve/asymmetry and head/shoulder response visibly express it. One or two character-appropriate feelings suffice. Writing “warm, young, loving, smiling” does not draw the result; clothes, iris, accessories and age do not change merely to achieve a smile.

Review in order: independent original → real 34px/48px circular view on light/dark surroundings → actual chat/title with name and theme. Check readable eyes/brows/mouth, face scale, accessories, edges and crop. Recalibrate the new close-up rather than reuse old torso zoom. Keep direction adoption, static image qualification, small-size qualification and runtime adoption separate, preserving actual prompt, input and passport/outfit version. A beautiful large picture or a producer's emotional adjective cannot approve the result.
