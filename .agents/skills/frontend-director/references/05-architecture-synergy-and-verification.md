Protect credentials and private state.

## Substantive stage collaboration

Formal twelve-stage execution requires eight agents each stage after the primary agent understands the complete input, product and stage. The following are responsibility areas, not four extra teams: character/scene production and extraction; study/PDF/OpenUI; agenda/notes/map; room/cast/theme/resume and shared craft. Assign actual exclusive files and a single writer for each shared contract. Every agent takes on meaningful creative or engineering work rather than supplying only counts, hashes or reports. Routine checks may assist real work but are not the stage contribution itself.

The primary agent personally inspects relevant original designs and new assets, chooses aesthetic/spatial direction, integrates global tokens/shell/state, and operates the final result. Read-only review and explanation do not automatically trigger formal stage staffing. A design comparison is not a reason to reopen every historical image. Respect the per-result image transport budget while inspecting all necessary originals.

## Acceptance through the actual workflow

Compare the rendered affected screen to the appropriate approved design at a comparable viewport. Inspect paper/glass material, radius and shadow; a compatible youthful person; support, body continuity and occlusion; purposeful cropping; stable stage contact during scale; navigation/composer/companion relationships; and protected neighboring alignments. Equality of coordinates does not establish optical alignment.

Operate the affected sequences at ordinary speed, including interruption and reversal. Examples include a legal-seat drag, agenda ghost trial/Escape/save/undo, an actual interactive curve/key/parameter, the same map expanded and returned, and PDF quote → note/bookmark → original page/selection. These examples identify risks; they do not authorize a new scripted button traversal or test suite. A screenshot cannot establish velocity, takeover, audio or state persistence.

Use relevant existing `check`, `test`, `test:hermes` and `build` commands when changed code requires them. Live model inspection uses only the currently authorized transport and the actual returned model identity; a configured label is not evidence. Do not call an unapproved paid provider for acceptance. Sound, OS notifications, native PDF, touch, particular devices and Mac operation need their own observations. A build or one character does not certify them.

Fix an in-scope observed defect and recheck its original trigger. Keep evidence proportionate: real input, owner state/result, visible result and the actual scope observed. Report implemented, currently exercised, unexercised, partial, design and externally conditional work distinctly. Do not claim user research when the evidence is heuristic or synthetic. Once the authorized outcome is established with no correctable in-scope gap, deliver rather than endlessly restyling sound work.
