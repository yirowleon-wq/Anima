# Spatial craft, themes and optical alignment

Use the current [visual specification](../../../../FRONTEND_DESIGN.md), actual tokens and rendered page. This reference supplies methods and candidate studies, not an instruction to restore an older shell.

## A room with changing attention

Anima is not a fixed half-and-half administration screen. Home can reveal the complete room; study can unfold a problem and explanation; materials need a stable tall original page; agenda needs its real timeline, trial and conflicts; notes and map each need room for their object. The companion yields through a compatible camera and supported pose. A broad historical study imagined about 68–75% work and 25–32% companion; that is a proposal, not a universal ratio.

Preserve these relationships:

1. Home-to-work continuity does not need to destroy the room or recreate the object. A camera adjustment, compatible pose and native paper surface can shift attention. Glass is a material option, not a requirement to put all content in blurred cards.
2. Character and content do not injure one another's space. Keep face, shoulders and meaningful hands away from buttons or bubbles that cut through them, and keep the character out of core text and formulas.
3. Returning to the full cover keeps the actual work address, composer/draft and relevant PDF/map state. Choosing the cover and resuming work are distinct actions. Avoid remount flashes and unexplained input jumps while adapting for keyboard and window safety.

## Shared slots without blind pixel rules

Historical wide-window candidates included 24px horizontal/20px vertical stage padding; 48px top bar; 36px pill height with 14px horizontal padding, 16px icon and 6px text gap; 22/16/12px radii; a 56px composer about 20px from the bottom with 760px maximum width; and a 16px split handle region with a 3px visible line. These are **candidate studies**. Do not override a working 72px current bar, existing split relationships, control size or narrow layout because a past table named another value.

Find real repeated edges and baselines, then consolidate the appropriate token. Concentric radii should relate to padding rather than fight at nested corners. A composer can remain the same DOM and draft while its safe-area position adapts; geometric immobility is not a reason to hide content behind a keyboard.

Actual PDF page pixels stay truthful. Adjust shell, surroundings and text reading mode through their proper objects.

## Repair without damaging sound work

First identify the neighboring relationships already working. Do not apply a global wildcard to fix one bad alignment. Then inspect optical weight: a triangular play icon may need a small rightward correction (roughly 1–1.5px was a starting example), and a typeface's actual glyphs may need different padding. A number alone is not visible centering.

Align the title, first body character and related lower control by the intended visual guide, avoiding meaningless small indents. A historical four-level type study suggested 18–20px headings, 14.5–15px body with 1.65 leading and 55–68ch measure, 13px controls and 11.5–12px metadata with tabular numbers. It is not a global four-size limit or permission to shrink the current interface. Real reading, formulas, text enlargement and normal controls determine final sizes.

Inspect the actual target windows and long content. Desktop examples 1280×800, 1440×900 and 1920×1080 can expose different relationships; they do not replace a genuinely narrow window. Check overlap, meaningful wrapping, intact controls, deliberate character crop, seat contact and return focus. Do not create a scripted viewport matrix merely to satisfy this list; focused normal interaction and existing tools are sufficient when they establish the result.
