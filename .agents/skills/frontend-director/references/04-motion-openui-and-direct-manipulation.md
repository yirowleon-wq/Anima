# Interruptible motion, direct manipulation and shared work

Good feel comes from immediate response, continuous position and takeover. Drag tracks the person directly. Return and spatial changes can use a velocity-preserving spring; color/opacity/reveals may use easing, and a constant-rate process may be linear. Damping, constraints and external forces do not constitute universal momentum conservation.

## Candidate spring studies

The current `use-spring.ts` default is stiffness 280, damping 30, mass 1. Alternatives below are studies to tune through actual distance, interruption and boundaries, not current defaults or guaranteed durations:

| Use | Stiffness / damping / mass | Intended quality |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Micro press, pill, paper corner, pin | 420 / 28 / 0.8 | Crisp, light; an earlier 160–200ms observation was an aim rather than a fixed duration. |
| Spatial yield, split or tray | 280 / 26 / 1 | Settled and comfortable; a roughly 280–340ms starting aim depends on travel and response. |
| Docking a valid pose, agenda block or route carrier | 320 / 24 / 0.9 | A cushioned return with usable continuity. |

When input arrives mid-transition, take the current position and appropriate velocity, stop the old target and respond immediately. Do not wait for an animation or jump back to its initial position. A precise pointer or supported contact cannot overshoot into a false relationship. Only one writer owns each transform channel.

Optional video references can inform amplitude, direction, emphasis and hold: a subtle loop may become controlled layers/keyframes; a justified spatial move can use a spring camera; actual fresh cadence may drive an optional sound pulse; a mechanism reference can guide a slow interactive model. Measure CPU, decoding, memory and energy on the target device. A default cadence, a video and a damping equation are not evidence of observed motion or a real piano mechanism.

## Five-stage direct manipulation

| Stage | Interaction and real state |
| --- | --- |
| Pickup | Local acknowledgment on press. A small lift/shadow and original-position cue may clarify ownership; 1.025 scale is an example, not a universal requirement. |
| Preview | Candidate slot, neighboring yield or ghost object makes the possible placement visible. Do not write SQLite or accept a business change. |
| Commit | A legal release submits through the original owner with expected revision. The element may settle while pending; only the actual success receipt establishes saving. |
| Cancel | Escape or release outside a valid area returns from the current position and restores the candidate layout. Keep the actual draft, attachments, IME and focus. |
| Recovery | Keep the edit after conflict or confirmed failure and compare the current version in place. Inspect an unknown action before retrying; a visual bounce is not a confirmed undo. Explain the concrete effect and a route forward. |

Keyboard and non-drag alternatives complete the same semantics. Cancellation need not discard a draft, and an interrupted performance must not cancel unrelated legal business.

## Seven substantial creative responsibilities

### 1 · Point to the same thing, then correct it naturally

A real selected equality can receive a restrained pencil bracket while its explanation opens beside it. When the explanation mentions a symbol, emphasize only the corresponding symbol in the source. If the person selects the next line, retire the old target and revise understanding and dependent explanation. A changed selection is not always an assistant mistake; do not force a fixed apology.

An `AIPointerLayer` is a design name, to map to existing owner references and valid anchors. `{spanId,page,rect}` alone does not establish an identity across pages. Object/revision/Unicode range belongs to the owner; screen rectangles are projections. The companion pointer differs from the user cursor and may indicate an A/B relation. No reliable anchor means stop pointing and recover it, not confidently point into empty space. Stage 01 establishes understanding/correction, 08 refines paper and cross-domain correspondence, and 12 inspects late old-target behavior.

### 2 · Earlier and current work recognize each other

An actual earlier rotation sketch can meet a current explanation of `e^(it)=cos(t)+i sin(t)`. A paper-corner reveal may overlay the sketch with a defensible registration and restore it on release. Opacity .85 and multiply blending are presentation candidates; they are not the relationship itself.

Read both originals, explain pairings, differences and conditions, and let the person reject or move a match. Geometric rotation, complex numbers and coordinate expression need real mathematical correspondence rather than arbitrary formula layout alignment. Where overlay does not work, use paired pointers or side-by-side views. Plans/actual outings, old/new drafts and concept/full model deserve the same possibility. Preserve click and keyboard equivalents. Stages 03/04 establish experience, conditions, versions and comparison recovery; 07/08 establish route and paper interaction.

### 3 · Take turns changing one object

A user main line and an assistant auxiliary line can share a meaningful constraint; moving an endpoint recalculates the real dependent relation. Removing a premise retracts dependent suggestions; taking the pen yields immediately.

`user_active / companion_assisting / shared_rest` can describe emphasis but cannot replace offering, trial, partial adoption, withdrawal, conflict and stopping points. The actual delta returns to the geometry, route, agenda, paragraph or program owner with author, base revision and dependencies. Accept one stroke and keep the rest proposed. Stage 04 supplies local operation foundations; 07–10 finish them in actual domains. Related offer/unfold/take/retract gestures do not delay usable results.

### 4 · Carry the current work across pages

A compact carrier can show a real symbol, route arc or unfinished musical bar. A paper corner, orb, tray, small window, shared desktop, glanceable status and sound have different uses. Compare them within the same read–change–fold–outing–return task instead of leaving only edge glow.

Carry the object group, versions, A/B relation, unfinished question and stopping points, not just a label. Unfold into the same input and owner. Continue a motion only when endpoints/contact remain compatible; writing, invalidation or a new layout makes it draw back safely from its current pose. A working first form does not finish all required forms.

### 5 · Shared marks retain the person's own craft

A route comment such as “too narrow” or a playful name for a difficult step can remain a sparse mark on the original object, opening its actual reason on click or keyboard focus. Hover is not the sole entry. Presets may coexist with real user strokes, vectors, images and editable shapes, all versioned.

Meaning returns to the original words or work rather than becoming another permanent private quote store. A route reason can matter in a plan and an old problem name can connect to new work. Rename, removal and correction reach these projections. Stages 03/04 provide source and relation; 07/08/10 provide actual expression. A mark is not an approval ticket.

### 6 · A gentle call can have no task

A click or light edge tap can interrupt a book or score, turn attention and receive the character's own response. The person may type, remain silent or let the exchange end. Waiting is naturally variable by character/activity/input, not a fixed three- or five-second emotional classifier. No task list, questionnaire or automatic diagnosis follows. The current product call uses the same Harness and preserves the unsent draft; it does not submit that draft or pretend a local eye animation is a conversation.

### 7 · Play remembers the previous move

A tilted note, its correction and a later crooked pencil illustrate a growing exchange. Record only related events, causes and explicit feedback, under a sensible budget. “The last three turns” or “within ten seconds” is not a law of relationship; the third tap has no guaranteed reward.

Respond locally to pushback, drag and pen input. Where semantics are needed, the same model chooses a response in character, rather than per-frame inference or a second animation agent. Silence does not prove liking. An explicitly liked action may occasionally return when appropriate; no expired joke queues up after hidden time. Do not prank the active writing. The character's own blinking, page turning and music can have a life reason without a business receipt. Stages 01/06/10/12 carry understanding, motion/sound, causal humor and continuous use.

## Outing attention and a return thought

Optional edge light can be a low-attention cue, historically a roughly 2px soft edge. A fresh actual cadence may influence it; unknown cadence has no default substitute. Keep a stable invocation and accessible pause/end/record controls in a compact form when needed. A sample display such as cadence, pace and distance is not a real reading, and a hover alone is insufficient access. On return, only an actually retained, editable phrase appears beside the same original problem. Voice/device capture not yet available remains a future condition, not an invented saved thought.

## Copy subtraction and honest character expression

Remove an explanatory line if layout and behavior already communicate its useful meaning. Do not replace deleted text with more icons, colors or cards. Redundant instructions such as announcing that a visible list is a list can go. Necessary labels, real numbers, sources, permission controls, errors and recovery remain.

Do not place internal coverage, queue status, SHA/UUID, raw JSON field names, CAS/revision checks or engine names into everyday main content as labor receipts. Necessary configuration and user-opened diagnostics can state exact technical facts. Do not hide that a demonstration is synthetic or that a real service is unavailable merely because those words sound technical.

An error needs **fact, effect and a useful continuation** in the current character's manner. Instead of making a 409 code the conversation, explain that the original arrangement changed, this edit remains a preview and the person can compare it before saving. Do not invent a proposed time or claim the draft survived unless those facts are true. Give the model the facts and let it express them; a fixed warmer rewrite is not personality.
