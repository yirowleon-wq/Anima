# Matrices, image production and physical coherence

Read the relevant complete [passport](characters/README.md), selected outfit and [art-direction method](character-art-direction.md). Start with the same person, youth, feeling and a reason for the action, then inspect anatomy and space. Correct limbs alone do not approve art. Full body, independent portrait and edge cat have separate coverage.

## Design and generation

Use a coherent chain: **a directed scene and timeline → optional visual/motion references through the actually selected tool → complete GPT Image scene → same-canvas layers where needed → live interface and controlled motion**. Tools differ in reference counts, editing, frames, duration, output dimensions and billing; inspect the actual available entry point. A web control is not an API flag, a historical model label is not current capability, and prompt words do not prove returned specifications.

Image-led design covers home, study, materials/PDF, agenda, notes, map, cast, theme and return/interruption recovery, including important new small interfaces. Reuse an approved design with the same meaning; do not substitute a few covers for all surfaces. Keep dynamic text, formulas, dates and controls out of painted backgrounds.

Video can supply a hair/environment loop, a narrated spatial transition or motion reference. Use the selected provider, current controls and authorization rather than inventing a `generate-video` command. A fixed reference, identity, seat, camera and light are useful for a subtle loop. Three to five seconds may be an editing aim, not a supported generation parameter. Identical endpoint images do not prove matching velocity, lighting or sound. A desk-to-track scene is fictional unless a legitimate geographic source establishes it. A suggested cadence is a directing example, not observed physiology.

Illustrative directions, adapted to actual tools:

> A single continuous shot of Sakiko in the approved sunlit chair scene. Keep identity, outfit, camera and support stable. Choose one small motion—hair responding to a light breeze or fabric settling—while her attention remains on her own page. No cut, new prop, title or spoken line. Review the downloaded length, dimensions and loop at ordinary speed.

> For a deliberately fictional transition, begin beside an amber desk lamp and a real handout, then move toward a neutral outdoor track at dusk. Preserve the same character and an appropriate new sports pose; a desk-seated body cannot simply slide into a running scene. Endpoint references are creative direction unless the chosen interface actually supports hard frame control.

> For a running-motion reference, direct a natural arm/leg cycle at a specified illustrative cadence, with stable character identity. Do not treat a generated foot strike, force or cadence as measured biomechanics or a recommendation for the user.

## The four-step pipeline

### 1 · Clean stage and grid

Choose a clean environment without a defective figure. Mark real seat/bed edge, chair plane/back, floor/footrest and perspective. Establish content safety, camera and light direction. A left window and approximately 2700K lamp can be one room's setup; do not impose them on every scene or describe a theoretical lamp temperature as measured output.

### 2 · One character, complete in the same scene

Generate character, furniture and light as one canvas from clean space and detailed text. Inspect the entire person before extending:

- Support and weight: hips/thighs/seat or the relevant leaning, standing or kneeling support. A soft cushion may compress; hard wood need not deform. Neither a fixed one-pixel gap nor exposed sitting bones is the criterion.
- Limb continuity and feet: distinguish a knee-cross, crossed ankles, curled sitting, raised knees and hanging legs. Judge hip–knee–ankle continuity, balance, perspective and occlusion for the chosen pose. Grounded, raised or naturally hanging feet can be sound.
- Proportion: evaluate the whole character and camera, not a fixed screen leg ratio. Avoid accidental cut feet in a full-body composition; a deliberate close or half-body view need not expose them.
- Light: skin, fabric, room and reflected wood/lamp illumination belong to the same field.

A broken body, unsupported figure or structural proportion failure exits expansion. Preserve necessary source-side diagnosis without putting rejected art back into reference, identity input, cache fallback or target manifest. Rebuild from a clean setting. User-deleted imagery stays retired regardless of an old approval.

### 3 · Expand the matrix

After the single-character path is sound, expand the shared camera/spatial language to the other characters and four themes. Independently compose portraits with their own face/hair framing, expression and small-size behavior. Do not crop a room head or count a small form against whole-body coverage. Retain the original identity and chosen outfit throughout theme changes.

### 4 · Extract and register

Separate only the layers actually needed: clean background, same-source character alpha, contact shadow, foreground occlusion, hand/eye/prop rigs. There is no compulsory three-layer count. Default to the mother canvas; record offsets and a common transform if cropped. Feet not touching the floor receive no invented floor-contact shadow.

Layers share one aspect-preserving stage and mother-to-screen transform. A 16:9 example is not a reason to distort a different actual canvas ratio. Controlled local transforms require real contact/occlusion rules. Inspect scaling and dragging in the app; equal sizes or coordinates do not prove optical contact. One effective writer controls each transform channel.

## Complete-scene prompt examples

**Sakiko, warm home:** Describe her recognizable youthful blue-violet hair, golden eyes, dark band and the selected complete outfit. Use crisp expressive two-dimensional linework, restrained cel shading and room light. In one coherent eye-level view she sits on the actual chair beside the window. State the chosen leg arrangement and its support, rather than combining contradictory instructions. Her hand holds a page or cup with weight. Let window light, curtains, wood, score and cup form one atmosphere; leave the actual interface content area clear. No painted buttons, green interface illumination, floating stickers or hard split.

**Mutsumi, a dusk return:** Use the complete selected passport and a clearly identified original sports outfit. She stands or sits in a legitimate, newly established outdoor setting and holds a real notebook or towel only if the scene needs it. Lavender/rose dusk, reflected light and shoes belong to one space. A visual “saved” journal mark may express a real local result only after its owner receipt; an official acceptance stamp requires that provider's same-attempt receipt. Never place an institutional landmark or formal recognition into the picture merely to make the scene convincing.
