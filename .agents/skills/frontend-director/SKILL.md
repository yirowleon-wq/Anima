# Frontend, motion and shared presence

Anima should feel like entering a room shared with someone who has their own attention, judgment, interests and affection. Design, light, movement and words serve that relationship and the work in hand. Current human instructions take precedence over historical summaries. Loading this skill does not begin all twelve development stages.

For any character image, gesture, portrait, small cat form or scene, read the **complete relevant [passport](references/characters/README.md)**, selected outfit and [art-direction method](references/character-art-direction.md). A name or short table is not a character. Official art identifies appearance and clothing; create new, coherent two-dimensional art rather than pasting the official figure into a room. Sound anatomy does not by itself establish youthfulness, feeling or recognizable identity. A huge white night panel, runtime collage or head cropped from a room is not an acceptable design answer.

Behavior and state work follows the [shared-presence contract](../../../docs/design/PRESENCE_SYSTEM.md), [motion production specification](../../../docs/design/CHARACTER_MOTION_PIPELINE.md) and [development roadmap](../../../docs/DEVELOPMENT_ROADMAP.md). Current implementation and observation belong in [CURRENT_STATUS](../../../docs/CURRENT_STATUS.md).

## Priorities

1. **Character, care and a life worth sharing.** The companion is neither a service counter nor a productivity enforcer wearing a portrait. Warmth accommodates rigorous work, ambition, excitement, play, rest and silence. If someone is tired but wants the full proof, explain it completely. If they want something new to play with, bring curiosity. An early return from a walk is a valid ending. A gentle call may end after a natural response without a task selector.
2. **Make the design's useful ideas real.** Implement the interactions implicit in approved design as part of the authorized outcome. Bring worthwhile, relevant ideas of your own and integrate them with existing objects. Do not replace correctable authorized work with a future suggestion or send ordinary product judgment back to the user.
3. **Spatial order before expansion.** Inspect the appropriate current design, establish common slots and optical relationships, then use the four-step production method. Cover the ten characters, nine page categories and four themes through meaningful combinations. Create the body, furniture and light together; a transparent sticker is not physical integration.
4. **Motion yields to the person.** Respond locally to direct manipulation, preserve velocity where useful and support pickup, preview, commit, cancellation and recovery. A movie that must finish before input is accepted is not an interactive performance.
5. **Facts and authority remain exact.** Mind, state, action and feedback form one experience, while permissions and authoritative storage remain with the host. Cross-module movement carries original identity, revision and source. Corrections and withdrawals reach the real runtime rather than changing only a highlight.

## A living character rather than a template

The room can show the companion reading their own book, listening to rain or absorbed in music. Their attention can turn toward a real question, make room for writing or remain quietly elsewhere. Character changes weight, gaze, timing, small gestures and expression; there is no universal puppet. Care comes through remembering a legitimate detail, locating the actual obstacle or taking on difficult work. Do not install a compulsory “I’m here,” “take it slowly,” “don’t worry” or “drink water” response route.

New built-in choices are the ten MyGO!!!!! / Ave Mujica characters. Custom roles and valid historical configurations remain readable. The [passports](references/characters/README.md) distinguish published biography, visual observation, actor/director interpretation and original Anima interaction. Saved names and relationships remain the user's choice; none of the characters has a universal private nickname for every person.

## Production and coverage

Maintain three connected matrices: **identity** (appearance/personality sources and versions), **scene** (camera, furniture, safe content space and light), and **posture** (support, contact, occlusion and allowed transitions). Extend coverage with scene × character × time × season × mood × action, and useful activity, theme, weather, outfit, device, layout and audio dimensions. Page category, activity, true body topology and portrait/small form are different dimensions. A mirrored drawing is not a new pose; ten portraits or cats do not finish the human scene matrix.

Use **approved**, **shared-compatible**, **static-fallback**, **not-applicable** and **missing** accurately, with actual adoption separate. Compatibility requires a reason. Do not call a required combination inapplicable because it is difficult. A first-character slice proves that route; fallback and reduced motion do not complete normal required motion.

Follow four steps:

1. Establish the common grid, camera, content safety, floor/footrest, seat and window/bed planes in a clean empty environment.
2. Create one complete character in that environment across the necessary situations; the primary agent inspects identity, youth, feeling, body continuity, support, contact and light.
3. Expand the approved spatial language to other characters and themes, preserving their individual timing and identity.
4. Extract required layers from the same approved canvas and fill missing slots. Layers share the mother-to-screen transform; cropped layers retain offsets. Contact shadows and foreground layers depend on the action, rather than a compulsory count of three.

A bad limb connection, broken selected leg-crossing relationship or unsupported figure must not become a reference for another generation, even under an “identity only” label. Rebuild the complete pose from a clean empty setting and precise text. Judge hips, knees, ankles, balance, perspective and occlusion for the chosen pose. Both feet on the ground, one foot raised or naturally hanging legs can all be sound. Hard wood need not deform, and hidden support need not be exposed merely to satisfy a pixel rule.

Preserve the approved character, facial relationships, head/body scale, outfit and linework through future continuous production. For the same activity and camera, weather primarily changes the whole light field; a deliberate new action needs a new reason. Do not randomly alter proportions or the gaze. Do not retroactively rewrite original prompts or automatically redraw already accepted minor differences. Retired or rejected imagery never reenters the production chain.

### Portraits and small forms

Portraits are independent close-up compositions. Face and hair dominate, with only enough neck, shoulder and collar to make the view work. Show the character's own youthful attraction, a moment of being understood, pride or delight, rather than copying distant reserved performance into a cold face. Ten characters do not share one smile. Avoid mature facial elongation, adult fashion anatomy, glassy oversized eyes or infant proportions.

Inspect the original, real **34px and 48px circular renderings**, and the actual chat/title location against light and dark surroundings. Check eyes, brows, mouth, accessories, crop and alpha edges. Direction adoption, image approval, small-size approval and runtime adoption are separate results.

The original Anima cat form combines a simple two-dimensional character head and a complete, horizontally resting cartoon cat body. Head, abdomen, paws and tail belong to one graphic language; avoid a detailed human head pasted onto realistic fur or a near-3D ornament. Use real support, transparent edges and a correct tail root. A separate tail moves around that root; shaking the whole PNG is not a tail movement. Select the qualified existing horizontal original for the desired facing direction and retain qualified opposite-facing horizontal variants. Preserve Rāna's eye colors and Mutsumi's paired clips from that selected original; mirroring must not swap asymmetric identity. Left/right facing selection is separate from docking orientation: a vertical edge uses the same selected horizontal resource with a joint 90-degree rotation of all layers and contact geometry. Separately drawn upright cats are retired and must not return as working art, reference inputs or remake assignments.

## Four themes and optical craft

Warm Apricot, Morning Mist, Dusk Violet and Night Rain Gold share layout and control relationships. Light changes across the complete room and the character. Manual choices remain chosen; automatic time uses a declared time zone and does not pretend to measure weather. Never use a hard day/night split or comparison slider.

**No green interface colors**, including accents, controls, cards and broad surfaces. Natural foliage, scenery, Mutsumi's established hair and original clothing colors remain natural. Color is judged in the rendered result, not by a class name.

Optical center is not numerical equality. Inspect glyph weight, shadow and the visible relationship of an icon to its container. Repair the concrete problem while protecting adjacent aligned regions. Current [FRONTEND_DESIGN](../../../FRONTEND_DESIGN.md) and actual tokens take precedence over old candidate dimensions. Do not force a historical 48px bar or reading ratio over a healthy current layout.

## Three clocks and seven action grammars

- **Fact time:** actual saving, revisions, tools and external receipts determine what happened.
- **Interaction time:** typing, IME, pen, drag and cancellation take over immediately with local feedback.
- **Performance time:** gaze, gesture, paper, light and sound can shorten, redirect, stop or be omitted without delaying interaction.

Use seven composable grammars: **look**, **yield**, **offer**, **unfold**, **retract**, **resume**, **play**. Look at a real target; yield before touching the user's focus; prepare and offer without forcing acceptance; expand from the original object; retract affected text/audio/intent as well as the visual; return to the exact work; enjoy something without demanding a goal. A gesture ending never authorizes a calendar write.

Drag follows the hand. Spring return and spatial takeover should preserve continuity and useful velocity. The existing spring default is 280/30/1, not a universal law. Alternative damping ratios, frequencies or timings are candidates to tune by distance, constraints and device. Color, opacity and brief reveals can use interruptible easing; an exact pointer or contact must not overshoot into a false relation. A brief ink-like completion flourish may settle while the actual item, history and undo remain readable.

### Seven creative responsibilities

1. Shared pointing can express a target or a relationship between A and B. A correction changes the model's working context and dependent explanation as well as the highlight.
2. An earlier attempt and today's object can recognize each other through real correspondence. Use overlay only with defensible registration; paired pointers and side-by-side comparison are equally valid.
3. Take turns changing one object. Keep author, base revision and dependencies, accept one stroke if desired, and retract suggestions whose premise disappeared without erasing the person's later work.
4. Carry the current work across pages through a paper corner, compact window/orb, tray, small status, shared desktop or sound. Preserve the comparison group and stopping points; compatible motion can continue, incompatible motion must safely yield.
5. Preserve personal drawings, names and imperfect marks with their real reason and source. A mark can connect a route to a plan or an old attempt to a new question; it grants no permission.
6. A gentle call turns attention from the character's own activity, allows a response and can end with no task, questionnaire or emotional diagnosis. Preserve the unsent composer draft.
7. Small jokes remember an actual previous move and explicit feedback. The person can answer, push back, interrupt or stop. Three successive actions are an example, never a compulsory third-click reward.

The eight finer obligations—beautiful interruption, remembered comparison layout, related materials in sound, only useful return changes, preparation where it can be used, motivated actions and private character life, guilt-free ending and occasional context-sensitive humor—continue through these responsibilities and the roadmap. An edge glow or blink does not replace them.

## Interactive works and sound

Preserve the real OpenUI generation, checked program, local state, user takeover, incremental patch, send-time snapshot and next-turn readback. Live text, formulas, dates and controls remain live, not painted into an image or video. For the piano, preserve the 88-key, chord, sustain and parameter-exploration goals. Explain key, action, escapement, hammer flight/return, backcheck, damping and pedal using a verified reference mechanism. One damped cosine is a component, not proof of a realistic piano. Listen to excitation, harmonics, damping and resonance; separate teaching slow motion, performance audio and UI clocks. Do not invent key pressure or a hand rig.

Video can provide timing, direction, emphasis and environmental material when appropriate; it does not replace required interactive motion. Use the actually available image workflow; the observed project-local Mac workflow supports new PNGs. Reference input, editing, masks and concurrency have separate evidence requirements. Do not invent CLI flags or switch billing routes. Optional video use requires its own chosen provider and actual controls, downloaded specifications, full playback and seam review. Static frames cannot establish motion quality.

## Architecture, collaboration and acceptance

`src/` is the implementation. Electron main owns credentials, SQLite, network, tools and effects; renderer uses the limited preload Bridge. Original owners retain text and facts; cross-module movement sends stable identity, revision/hash and source coordinates. Appearance and split preferences are not long-term memory or synchronization.

Formal twelve-stage development uses **eight substantive subagents per stage**, independent file boundaries and a primary agent who first understands the whole task and personally integrates and accepts it. Reading, explanation and review do not automatically start a formal stage. Do not replace substantive work with eight reports, nor require arbitrary line counts. The primary agent personally inspects relevant design originals, new assets, final rendering and actual interactions; delegate assurances do not substitute for acceptance. Batch images within transport limits without skipping necessary detail.

Reject an in-scope result with green UI, broken body/support, engineering receipts in the main conversation, uninterruptible motion, a cockpit of controls, wrong text anchors, artwork changing a saved identity, rewritten historical authorship, local activity masquerading as official recognition, or fabricated verification. Explain a real limitation plainly with its effect and a useful way forward.

For affected runtime work, tell one concrete story, direct the entering/pointing/preparing/correction/continuation beats and interruption points, check all three clocks, and operate it on the real target. Run relevant **existing** checks and actual model paths within current authorization. Do not create test suites, fixtures or runners without a request for test development. A build, image, old receipt or one character slice cannot certify all interaction, motion, devices or the matrix. Repair correctable defects and recheck their original trigger. Report implementation, current observation, partial work, design and external conditions separately.
