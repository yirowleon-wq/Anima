# Mission and protected principles

This guides Anima development. It is neither a student profile, a runtime system prompt nor a grant of operating permission.

## Input and reading

Establish the current request, scope and object of change. Read this entry when a decision affects the principles below. Find current strategy, implementation, unconnected modules and evidence through [project navigation](../../../PROJECT_NAVIGATION.md); earlier conversations do not establish a missing module or permission.

## Why Anima exists

Help with student and campus responsibilities, personal aims and everyday circumstances by providing trustworthy information, understandable judgments and adjustable next steps. Reduce the work of organizing and repeatedly checking things. Help people direct their own lives. A partner can take a difficult proof, an ambitious project, ordinary conversation, play, rest and a quiet afternoon seriously.

P1–P5 are inspectable statements of the interests this product protects, not claims that its current code already satisfies them. Ordinary iteration preserves them. A product owner can revise the mission through the major-revision process below. To distinguish a principle from a strategy, ask whether it should still protect the person if the model, institution, map provider, interface and entire implementation changed.

## P1 · Decision-making and explicit authorization

- **Protection and reason:** People can understand, choose, correct, decline and withdraw arrangements and data use within the appropriate scope. Convenience must not depend on their being unaware of what the system does.
- **Violations:** Treating permission to read as permission to book; inferring consent from personality; accepting a suggestion by default; hiding a necessary control to reduce clutter.
- **Resolving tension:** Establish the current authorization, then avoid repetitive confirmations inside it. Existing explicit authorization remains useful. Prepare a concrete reviewable object before an unauthorized external action. Declining one source is not declining all assistance.

## P2 · Proportionate data belonging to the right person

- **Protection and reason:** Useful personalization coexists with boundaries on purpose, access and retention. Adding something to a profile does not make it available for every task.
- **Violations:** Collecting data because it might be useful; injecting a complete user record, grades, health history and chat into every turn; reusing another person's records; passing credentials to a model.
- **Resolving tension:** Identify the minimum information and the basis for reading it. Narrow the conclusion or ask when essential information is missing. Business classification and privacy restrictions are separate questions. Recheck a new purpose, withdrawal or change of user.

## P3 · Honest facts, sources, freshness and action states

- **Protection and reason:** People can distinguish institutional records, self-report, inference, examples, suggestions and execution. They must not act on nonexistent or stale information.
- **Violations:** Treating a module name as implementation, a standalone component as integrated, a static timetable as actual occurrences, a local completion as official recognition, or an unknown booking result as success.
- **Resolving tension:** Preserve each domain's authority and scope. Explain conflicting sources together; a summary or preference cannot rewrite a fact. Show the specific uncertainty and a useful next step.

## P4 · Understanding can change, be corrected and be forgotten within real boundaries

- **Protection and reason:** A temporary state, lasting preference, goal and voluntarily shared tendency are different. One record must not freeze a person into an identity.
- **Violations:** Turning “I don't want to run today” into a permanent dislike; deriving personality from grades; overriding a current wish with a persona; deleting a preference in the interface while an old summary still treats it as active.
- **Resolving tension:** Assign the information to its proper owner, maintain its source and effective/replacement relationships, and prefer explicit wishes over inferred tendencies. Check affected derived understanding after correction or forgetting. Explain the actual boundaries for originals, already disclosed content and deletion.

## P5 · Simplicity reduces human effort and preserves recovery

- **Protection and reason:** People need not understand internal modules to receive help. Content, controls and state remain legible across relevant devices, abilities and circumstances.
- **Violations:** Hiding errors, sources or permissions to create empty space; repeated reentry; losing drafts on stop, navigation or retry; delaying results for visual effect; imposing one study duration or layout on everyone.
- **Resolving tension:** Preserve comprehension, control and recovery, then choose the expression. Judge actual tasks, rendered screens and interaction rather than adjectives such as minimal or smooth.

## Non-goals and four layers

Do not build a private-data warehouse for unrestricted model access. Summaries are not a second timetable or gradebook. Automation counts, data volume and personality categories are not value by themselves. Do not promise omniscience, real-time knowledge or universal success without evidence.

- This skill maintains the **mission and P1–P5**.
- [Current strategy](../../../PROJECT_NAVIGATION.md#current-strategy) maintains the conversational entry point, institution defaults, model direction and stage scope. The current request sets task scope; a historical single-turn constraint does not govern all future work.
- [Product design](../product-design/SKILL.md) maintains methods with conditions of use.
- Stacks, fields, folders, colors, dimensions and service vendors are replaceable implementation choices. Replacing one does not itself change the mission.

## Major revisions and completion

To change the mission or P1–P5, present the old and proposed statements, reason, impact on people and existing commitments, correspondence and mitigations to the product owner. Do not amend a principle through a quiet exception. If the current instruction already approves the change, record that authorization without asking again. A normal implementation choice is not automatically a principle-approval process.

Typographical fixes, links and meaning-preserving editing are not major revisions. Changes to protection, priority, exceptions or promises are.

A completed decision identifies its layer, protected interests, compatibility with the current request, sources of facts and gaps, and what remains proposed. If implementation cannot be shown to meet a principle, keep the gap in navigation; do not rewrite the facts to manufacture compliance.
