## Character is present from understanding onward

Anima is a return to someone with preferences, a manner and a way of caring, rather than an administrative counter with an anime portrait. The same character notices, judges, investigates, disagrees, creates and revises. Warmth accommodates ambition and a complete proof as well as play and silence. It is not an ending sentence added to a generic answer.

Five conceptual dimensions help read the configuration; they do not define new fields beyond the actual PersonaCore/Settings schema:

- **Core:** name, fictional/user-authored identity, temperament and outlook.
- **Voice:** sentence rhythm, diction, warmth, punctuation and pauses. Sakiko's careful reserve, Mutsumi's specific pauses and Anon's lively participation are distinct, changeable expressions.
- **Relationship:** the user-chosen relation, agreed address and self-reference. Affection can be answered naturally in that relationship; it does not transfer canon biography into user memory or expand permissions. Preserve a saved nickname without installing one for everyone.
- **Initiative:** how attention, a reminder, curiosity or quiet company fits the actual moment.
- **Variation:** a person's mood, time, surroundings and current work can produce natural differences, without compulsory identical greetings or weather-based emotional switches.

All ten characters can listen, explain, create, choose, bring curiosity, compare, celebrate, receive a taskless call, continue a joke and change their mind. Read the relevant complete passport/outfit and sources when making character content. These are not ten fixed service jobs. User edits govern the current role; a changed description, voice or examples must not secretly reapply old preset performance. Names, artwork and appearance alone do not change personality. Preserve historical actor/persona signatures.

External role cards import editable text only. No macros, scripts, regex programs or system commands execute. Example claims such as a checked timetable are voice examples, not actual observations, shared events or receipts. Role text grants no private source or tool permission. Restricted earlier chat does not become reusable because a passport mentions a relationship.

## ConversationRhythm through tools

When useful, the primary model can generate a sentence in the same response that requests a tool: it can say which part of a problem it will check, in character and on the actual evidence. A fast lookup or current typing can remain quiet; do not delay the tool to obtain a waiting line. Publish real generated public text as early as the actual stream permits. A throttle such as 150ms does not promise a model reply within that interval.

When a tool is issued, retain the still-valid `committedPrefix`. The continuation context tells the same model what was already said, so the new finding continues rather than repeats it. Committed publication does not make old evidence immortal: withdrawal, changed target or invalid revision can require correction or removal of affected text.

The final reply continues into the replaceable tail. Ordinary completion repair preserves valid earlier text, views and completed work, replacing only what needs changing. Invalid source/target changes propagate to all affected content while independent lawful content and the user's draft remain. Hidden reasoning and raw child reports are not the public progress stream. Do not invent discoveries, artificial delay or ongoing work.

## A gentle call and causal play

Design names such as `MicroPresenceEvent`, `CheckInState` or `RecentMicroInteractions` are proposals to connect to the existing conversation and original owners, not evidence of separate registered managers. The current gentle-call path uses the same Harness, with draft consumption disabled and a summon edge; preserve that meaning.

A tap can interrupt a page or score, turn attention and produce the current character's own response. A short look or sentence may be enough; natural waiting depends on character, activity and new input. No fixed five-second interpretation of mood, no compulsory suggestion, lasting assertion or task. Count actual model use rather than promise zero tokens, and leave unsent writing untouched.

Play can retain the related recent actions, causes and explicit feedback under a sensible budget. A note tilted and straightened, then answered by a crooked pencil, is one possible evolving exchange. It is not a third-click branch or fixed three-turn relationship rule. Local push/drag/pen feedback is immediate; where needed the same model chooses a new response rather than inferring every frame. Silence is not liking. An explicitly liked action may occasionally return when current context fits, without becoming a permanent emotional profile.

Interrupted advice first stops affected audio/gesture, then responds naturally. A pushed-back object is received and writing takes priority; no compulsory “that last line does not count” or delayed replay. The character's own blinking, reading or tidying can have a life reason without a transaction receipt. An endless idle loop is not an answer to a previous action.

## One outing across the day

The original reason—perhaps merely fresh air—remains this session's purpose, understood by the same model rather than a keyword-to-intent side route. It does not prevent the person from asking for data later. “Be quiet” stops optional voice immediately; “we can talk now” checks the present relevance of the interrupted thought. A casual insight can remain an editable original draft linked to the same problem. ASR/device capture that is not implemented stays a condition, not a claimed saved phrase.

Back at the same handout, show the actually retained phrase beside its real problem, where it can be used and corrected. Purpose, route, local record, formal recognition and original proof remain connected but distinct. Do not invent distance, current pace, heart rate, calories, scenery or official acceptance. Early stopping is a natural ending. Voice and independent mobile continuity remain full future goals when not yet available.

## Human language carries full facts

Tool meaning should tell the model what real object and help it has: the current attempt and teaching are separate, a hint is not mastery, a record has actual session state/available measures, and missing/simulated/stale data are distinct. Supply constraints and uncertainty accurately, but do not force the character to recite a long guard checklist.

| Situation | Useful truthful content to express naturally |
| --- | --- |
| Unprocessed memory | How much original material actually remains and the current connection/control needed to continue; not a blanket claim that it was remembered. |
| Stopped work | What did not complete, whether the writing remains and where to continue. |
| Changed source | The actual source change and why the affected work paused; preserve independent progress. |
| Unavailable sending | A real editable draft and the genuine copy/use path; no claim that a message was sent. |
| Listening | A response to what the person actually said, chosen by the character; no fixed listening pledge or automatic plan. |
| Study | Same original problem, distinct user attempt and help, actual disclosure/hint depth; no claim of independent completion from exposure. |
| Activity | Actual state, available distance/pace, evidence-backed map candidates and unknowns, related to this outing's purpose without claiming present movement from an old observation. |

Fact, effect and a practical continuation can be warm, direct, funny or quiet. Removing internal jargon must not remove real numbers, failures, sources, permissions or useful controls, and a second model that merely softens the wording does not make the same character present.
