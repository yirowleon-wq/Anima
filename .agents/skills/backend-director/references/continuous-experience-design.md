# Designing a continuous shared life

## Original objects join the product

One problem may begin in a handout, enter study, become an editable thought during an outing and resume at the desk. Its identity, source, revision, unfinished step and the person's choice provide continuity. Repeating an avatar on each page does not.

For a stage, identify origin, authoritative owner, current attention, allowed operations and what returns after leaving. Views may be reconstructed; the fact identity is not replaced.

## Groups preserve each owner

An operation group associates children. Each has its own target, preview, authorization, expected revision, result and undo capability. “Finish earlier tomorrow, and keep the part I did not understand” might suggest a bookmark, study draft and agenda trial. Accepting only the bookmark does not approve the other two, even if one receiving animation displays them together.

Whether local bulk writes are atomic follows the actual storage boundary. External institutions, calendar providers and messages have their own actions and receipts. Explain partial failure, inspect uncertainty and compensate independently; do not claim natural cross-service atomicity.

## Memory changes the help

Original anchors support precise reading, conditional assertions keep the original premise, and shared marks come from actual use or agreement. A passing wish not to study is not permanent aversion, and each small gesture need not become a profile.

Correct current understanding before waiting for asynchronous consolidation. Old and new meanings can be shown together, with a causal arrow only if evidence supports it. Folded corners, return-to-sentence and sparse marks may serve memory without making people administer six internal layers. Clear edit/delete controls preserve sovereignty.

## An initiative has somewhere to go

An opportunity needs a reason, actual preparation, appropriate entry, usable content and a natural exit. Quiet preparation can stay in the background; attention-bearing presentation waits for a suitable moment. Agreed fixed reminders are a separate commitment within actual host operation.

Curiosity is not a task accessory. Music, story, a shared imaginary sky or a useless playful afternoon can be complete. Refusal or silence does not earn a follow-up question or count as failure. Fresh air need not become an unfinished performance metric.

## Devices share meaning, not body geometry

Desktop can spread sources, iPad can hold Pencil/two-paper work, and a phone can offer a short phrase, route and glanceable state. iOS/iPad precedes Android; equal care does not imply pixel copying. Sync actual source/object versions, legitimate operations and fences. Split ratio, volume, focus, scroll and per-frame motion generally stay local. Retain offline branches/conflicts and apply withdrawal barriers before reusing content after reconnect.

A phone package must run independently of desktop gateways, Vite/Node/Electron, debugging proxy, screen mirroring and USB forwarding. It needs a real local owner store, credentials/permission ports, legal scheduling, cancellation, outbox and recovery. First prove a narrow viable runtime/package slice: desktop Python Hermes does not automatically embed in Swift. Preserve the one assistant/owner semantics without creating a second memory brain or calling a PC remotely and naming it independence.

Compare causally meaningful events, revisions, transactions, outbox, deletion and work state rather than random IDs or clock strings. Network work does not hold a database transaction. Original-text fallback and language-appropriate retrieval survive. Material schema, lineage, attachment identity and tombstones travel; credentials, OS permissions, expired approvals and immediately activated external effects do not. After process recovery, inspect uncertain writes rather than replaying visual effects.

The required independence path is: PC closed → phone changes a real object → process reclaimed → original matter restored → another device continues. Installation, a simulator screen and mirroring each prove narrower facts. Mobile systems do not grant unlimited background execution; update timestamps and stale states stay honest, and lock-screen presence remains finite.

## Counterfactuals guide focused verification

Ask what happens if the person changes their mind, no image is available, a receipt is late, another device changed first, only half is accepted, the screen never appeared or the original was deleted. Use the original workflow and relevant existing checks. Do not create suites, fixtures or runners without test-development authorization; do not damage a healthy implementation merely to demonstrate a check. Documents establish references/order/meaning, while runtime work needs mechanism and actual experience evidence. Live paid models, external sends and real institutional writes need the current specific authorization.

## Full creative responsibility

The six forms, seven ideas, complete outing and eight details connect shared attention → real explanation/correction → an actual old attempt participating in new understanding → turn-taking → cross-page/device continuation of the relation. Own marks, gentle calls, causal jokes and preparation at the point of use make it a relationship rather than only persistent chat.

Extend actual focused/current context through every registered surface. A group can include related materials, routes and versions; the same model investigates, uses tools, changes the object and rereads the result. A four-module enumeration, three geometric actions or a moving highlight cannot substitute for that understanding. Semantic context, screen projection and performance timing remain distinct responsibilities.

Formal stages retain eight substantive agents, complete responsibilities and the roadmap's expanded gates. A first vertical slice proves a route only. Normal motion, reduced motion, resource fallback, comparison recovery, true source/device conditions and the ten-character world retain their separate acceptance boundaries.
