# Backend, Harness and shared presence

The backend makes a continuous human-facing experience possible. It does more than store and validate: it carries actual findings, character participation, public conversational rhythm and legitimate handoffs between objects. Read the [presence contract](../../../docs/design/PRESENCE_SYSTEM.md) and [motion specification](../../../docs/design/CHARACTER_MOTION_PIPELINE.md) with the frontend when changing behavior, state or device continuity. Current request and authorization determine scope; this skill does not launch every stage.

## Keep three responsibilities clear

**The model understands and expresses.** Give it real natural words, permitted context, source limitations and useful facts. Personality participates in attention, investigation, tool choice, disagreement and correction, rather than restyling an answer afterward.

**The host enforces.** Permissions, versions, privacy fences, budgets, cancellation, idempotency and commits belong in deterministic TypeScript/Python. Do not make the character recite these checks as a defensive instruction manual.

**The product communicates.** Present a real result with satisfaction, a problem with its actual effect and a way forward, and uncertainty honestly. Plain language does not mean withholding facts or deleting a genuine missing capability. Signatures and parameters stay exact where needed. An old snapshot does not establish present activity; a warm sentence does not establish a successful action.

## Five cooperating layers

1. **Inputs:** current words, explicit interactions and genuinely connected observations. Voice and images enter only within their actual capabilities and permissions.
2. **Intelligence and orchestration:** `harness.ts`, the pinned Hermes adapter/sidecar and context compilation, with public rhythm, bounded investigation and coordinated presentation.
3. **Transactional domain capabilities:** notes, materials/PDF, agenda, study, general campus resources, maps, activity records and finite live OpenUI programs.
4. **Evidence memory and privacy:** verbatim sources, conditional bitemporal assertions, decisions/barriers, shared works, domain observations and derived indexes; exact invalidation reaches all affected outputs.
5. **Device continuity and adapters:** iOS/iPad first, Android afterward, with independent hosts and local stores, causal objects, deletion barriers and real platform ports. A desktop location bridge is not that completed product.

## Ten axioms

1. **One implementation and primary loop.** `src/main/harness.ts → src/main/runtime/hermes.ts → runtime/hermes/sidecar.py →` pinned Python `AIAgent`. Main owns secrets, SQLite, file access, hardware/tools and effects. Renderer uses `preload.ts`; no parallel chat engine or frontend fact database.
2. **Model understanding, host guards.** The model interprets, creates, investigates and revises; the host guards source, purpose, transfer, retention, audience, budget, cancellation, revisions, idempotency and commit.
3. **Character is a participant.** The ten distinct characters and user-authored roles can be rigorous, ambitious, playful, affectionate or silent. User facts, third people, fictional worlds and current wishes remain distinct.
4. **Speak while working, truthfully.** `ConversationRhythm` presents actual generated public text early and continues from useful findings. Local receipt feedback is prompt. A publication throttle such as 150ms is not a model-response guarantee, and prewritten dialogue is not understanding.
5. **Two kinds of initiative.** Honor a real entrusted commitment and allow a character's own curiosity or care. Preparation can be quiet, offers may be declined, and a person's concentration matters. Fixed reminders remain separate.
6. **Evidence first.** Preserve exact sources, conditions, valid/recorded times and falsifiable friction. One exception is not a permanent profile. Correction changes current help; privacy fences immediately block affected further use, followed by actual dependency cleanup with honest offline/backup boundaries.
7. **One owner per object.** Carry `{ id, revision/expectedRevision, sourceAnchor }` or the owner's concrete equivalent across modules. Do not duplicate a quote, problem, route or event as orphan text.
8. **Transactional actions.** Preview is not a database write. Local saved activity is not institutional recognition. Action, approval, attempt and receipt differ; an unknown result is inspected before recovery. Reversible local work already explicitly authorized need not be approved again.
9. **Audience and purpose separation.** Read only what the present purpose permits and send only what transmission permits. Private collaboration and a projection for another audience differ. Closeness grants no access; a public draft is not a reason to reduce every private conversation to public-only content.
10. **Falsify claims before accepting them.** Use the original workflow, relevant existing checks and concrete counterexamples to expose false delay, false success and stale state. Without explicit test-development authorization, add no tests, fixtures, suites or runners. Mechanism, model, screen, asset and device evidence do not substitute for one another.

## Shared mind, state, action and feedback

Separate **fact time**, **interaction time** and **performance time**. A write or external receipt decides a fact; typing, IME, pen, drag and cancellation take over locally; gesture, light, paper and audio can redirect or end without delaying input. Animation completion does not save, send or approve.

`rhythmState`, `gazeTarget` and `handOff` describe design responsibilities. Their appearance in a document does not prove an IPC field exists, nor do they disclose hidden reasoning or diagnose human feelings. Map a proposed field to current types and owner contracts before implementation.

Five design protocols preserve the same objects:

- **AIPointerTarget:** versioned original targets and an optional A/B relation. User correction changes context and dependent explanation, not just coordinates.
- **TracingPaperMatch:** actual old/current originals, explainable pairings and changed conditions. Overlay requires defensible alignment; paired pointers and side-by-side comparison remain peers.
- **CanvasCollaborativeDelta:** local operations with target, base revision, author and dependencies; accept one stroke or reject the rest, while exact undo preserves later user work.
- **TravelingContextSnapshot:** the original object group, relations, legal versions, unfinished question and stopping point, across pages and devices. Do not constrain all functions to a short module enumeration or copy bodies into it.
- **RelationalToken:** a versioned preset or user-drawn mark with actual meaning references. It connects experience across original objects and grants no permission.

These are design names rather than a claim that all APIs are registered. The six appearance forms, seven creative responsibilities, complete outing and eight fine details remain in the [roadmap](../../../docs/DEVELOPMENT_ROADMAP.md). A first slice or static fallback does not close the ten-character or full-motion obligations.

### Withdrawal reaches five outlets

First distinguish correction, changed reality, exception, disabling an understanding, withdrawing a proposal, stopping work, revoking a source and permanent forgetting. Process the affected scope through its original owner:

1. Update usable model context and preserve independent valid words/work; already disclosed material cannot be unsent by assertion.
2. Cancel or revalidate affected investigations and reject late stale results without stopping unrelated legal work.
3. Invalidate actual dependent caches and conclusions. Ordinary revision is not wholesale privacy deletion.
4. Retract affected preparation/proposals; executed actions use real undo or compensation. Flying away is not undo.
5. Cancel affected queued speech/notifications and attempt real removal through the original delivery port, recording its result. Do not claim the person never saw a delivered item or that every offline device erased it instantly.

### Partial adoption

An operation group relates child operations rather than replacing their owners. A request for only the latter half can submit just that authorized part; the rest remains proposed or exits. Preview, authorization basis, expected revision, actual result and undo capability accompany each child. Cross-service work is not magically atomic; partial results, uncertainty and compensation stay truthful.

## Runtime and long-term implementation

Use progressive discovery: compact capability directory, `look_up` for relevant methods and prerequisites, then precise working details/signatures only when needed. Directory reading is not private-body reading. Native delegation is read-only, a subset of the parent’s legal sources, shared budget and cancellation; the root user's words, not the parent's task wording, support purpose. Revalidate every source before a model request and parent adoption. A child report is not independent evidence.

Keep six memory layers as cooperating responsibilities, not competing fact stores. Use original text, structured conditions and bitemporal history, decisions/barriers, works, live observations and derived retrieval indexes. Default opening context does not inject unverified vector candidates. English retrieval must keep literal/original/neighboring-text fallback; indexes address evidence and do not become it.

Preserve the actual finite OpenUI program, checked construction, versioned incremental patch, SQLite CAS local inputs, send-time snapshot and next-turn readback. A local parameter is not an agenda fact; generated, rendered, seen, understood, accepted and executed remain separate. Whole-runtime and OpenUI redesign must map all old capabilities, data, authorization, cancel/recovery and exit conditions through staged replacement, rather than leaving two permanent engines or discarding features before a rewrite.

General campus capability preserves nineteen resource purposes through real configured providers or manual/file sources. Institution-specific authentication, rules and formal submissions require that institution's genuine contract. Public institutional calendars are not a person's private timetable. Demo geography is labeled synthetic and never becomes campus navigation by renaming it. Develop and exercise complete map, route, workout and independent native-device behavior using app-internal simulated fixes and tracks, manual origins or permitted imports with honest provenance and quality. Never acquire real device location or enable OS location services or request or grant location permissions on any platform, browser, emulator or guest. Native GPS acquisition is not a current development goal; simulation does not prove physical arrival or official credit.

Formal stage execution uses **eight substantive agents per stage**, exclusive write ownership and primary-agent integration/acceptance after understanding the full task. Ordinary review and documents do not automatically invoke that stage structure. Preserve the complete roadmap, independent mobile goal and all extension requirements; unknown hardware, credentials, APIs and Mac execution remain actual conditions rather than completion claims.
